# Every early August booking on a new facility

**Difficulty:** hard · **Language:** SQL (SQLite) · **Topics:** [SQL: filtering](../sql-filter/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-986?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

The first half of August 2012 is 1 to 15 August inclusive. Output the columns `memid` and `surname` of the members (not the guest) who have bookings in the first half of August and each such booking was their first visit of that facility: before its start the member had never booked the same facility, neither earlier nor in the same two weeks. What happened after the booking does not matter. The row order does not matter.

The tables and data are already in the starter code and are created afresh on every run (SQLite):

- `facilities(facid, name, membercost, guestcost, initialoutlay, monthlymaintenance)` — club facilities; `membercost` and `guestcost` are the price of one slot for a member and for a guest.
- `members(memid, surname, firstname, address, zipcode, telephone, recommendedby, joindate)` — members; `recommendedby` is the `memid` of who invited them, or `NULL`; `memid = 0` is a guest.
- `bookings(bookid, facid, memid, starttime, slots)` — bookings; one slot is 30 minutes; `starttime` is text such as `2012-09-14 09:00:00`.

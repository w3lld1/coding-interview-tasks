# Bookings after the recommender stopped coming

**Difficulty:** medium · **Language:** SQL (SQLite) · **Topics:** [SQL: filtering](../sql-filter/), [SQL: joins](../sql-join/), [SQL: aggregation](../sql-aggregate/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-985?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Output the columns `bookid`, `memid` and `recommendedby` of the bookings that started strictly after the very last booking of this member's recommender (the one in their `recommendedby`). Members with no recommender, or whose recommender has no bookings, are not in the answer. The row order does not matter.

The tables and data are already in the starter code and are created afresh on every run (SQLite):

- `facilities(facid, name, membercost, guestcost, initialoutlay, monthlymaintenance)` — club facilities; `membercost` and `guestcost` are the price of one slot for a member and for a guest.
- `members(memid, surname, firstname, address, zipcode, telephone, recommendedby, joindate)` — members; `recommendedby` is the `memid` of who invited them, or `NULL`; `memid = 0` is a guest.
- `bookings(bookid, facid, memid, starttime, slots)` — bookings; one slot is 30 minutes; `starttime` is text such as `2012-09-14 09:00:00`.

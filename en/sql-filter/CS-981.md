# Facilities whose September earned more than July and August together

**Difficulty:** medium · **Language:** SQL (SQLite) · **Topics:** [SQL: filtering](../sql-filter/), [SQL: aggregation](../sql-aggregate/), [SQL: joins](../sql-join/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-981?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A booking's revenue is `slots` times `guestcost` for a guest (`memid = 0`) and times `membercost` for a member. Output the columns `facid` and `revenue` (the facility's revenue for September 2012) for the facilities whose September revenue is strictly greater than their July and August 2012 revenue together. The row order does not matter.

The tables and data are already in the starter code and are created afresh on every run (SQLite):

- `facilities(facid, name, membercost, guestcost, initialoutlay, monthlymaintenance)` — club facilities; `membercost` and `guestcost` are the price of one slot for a member and for a guest.
- `members(memid, surname, firstname, address, zipcode, telephone, recommendedby, joindate)` — members; `recommendedby` is the `memid` of who invited them, or `NULL`; `memid = 0` is a guest.
- `bookings(bookid, facid, memid, starttime, slots)` — bookings; one slot is 30 minutes; `starttime` is text such as `2012-09-14 09:00:00`.

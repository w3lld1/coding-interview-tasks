# Found a facility before their recommender

**Difficulty:** hard · **Language:** SQL (SQLite) · **Topics:** [SQL: filtering](../sql-filter/), [SQL: joins](../sql-join/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-994?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Find a member's first bookings of the facilities on which their recommender (the person in `recommendedby`) had not been even once by the start of that booking, including when the recommender was never there at all. Members without a recommender do not count. Output the columns `memid`, `facid` and `bookid`, that member's first booking of that facility. The row order does not matter.

The tables and data are already in the starter code and are created afresh on every run (SQLite):

- `facilities(facid, name, membercost, guestcost, initialoutlay, monthlymaintenance)` — club facilities; `membercost` and `guestcost` are the price of one slot for a member and for a guest.
- `members(memid, surname, firstname, address, zipcode, telephone, recommendedby, joindate)` — members; `recommendedby` is the `memid` of who invited them, or `NULL`; `memid = 0` is a guest.
- `bookings(bookid, facid, memid, starttime, slots)` — bookings; one slot is 30 minutes; `starttime` is text such as `2012-09-14 09:00:00`.

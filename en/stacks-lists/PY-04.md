# Matching Brackets

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/), [Strings](../strings/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/PY-04?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Determine whether the parentheses, square brackets and curly braces in a string are correctly placed. Each closing bracket must match the most recent bracket that is still open, and no unclosed brackets may remain at the end. Ignore all other characters; an empty string is considered valid.

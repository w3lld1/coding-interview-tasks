# A card trick: the reveal order

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-531?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A deck has `n` cards numbered `1..n`, card 1 on top, 2 under it and so on down to `n` at the bottom. A magician repeats two actions while the deck has cards: reveal the top card and set it aside, then move the new top card (if there is one) to the bottom of the deck. Return the card numbers in the order they were revealed. For `n = 0` return an empty list; `n ≤ 1000`.

Example: `5` → `[1, 3, 5, 4, 2]` (deck 1 2 3 4 5: reveal 1, 2 goes to the bottom → 3 4 5 2; reveal 3, 4 to the bottom → 5 2 4; reveal 5, 2 to the bottom → 4 2; reveal 4, 2 to the bottom → 2; reveal 2).

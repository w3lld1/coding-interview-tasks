# Managing energy across activities

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-59?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Your day is planned: activities come in a fixed order, and activity `i` has value `values[i]`. In the morning you have `max_energy` units of energy, which is both your stock and your cap. On each activity you spend any non-negative whole amount of energy, no more than you have right now; the gain from it is its value times the energy spent. After each activity you regain `regain` units, but you never hold more than `max_energy` (the excess is lost).

Return the largest total gain for the day. Limits: `1 ≤ max_energy, regain ≤ 10⁶`, 1 to 10⁴ activities, values from 1 to 10⁴.

Example: `5`, `2`, `[1, 2]` → `12` (spend 2 on the first activity, regain back to 5 and spend all of it on the second).

# Car fleets at the finish

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-116?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Cars drive along a single lane towards the finish at point `target`. Car `i` starts at `position[i]` (all positions are different, `0 <= position[i] < target`) and drives at a constant speed `speed[i] >= 1`. Overtaking is not allowed: a car that catches a slower one ahead then drives at its speed, and together they form a fleet (a single car is also a fleet). Cars that meet exactly at `target` count as one fleet. Return the number of fleets that arrive at the finish. Values are up to 10⁵, up to 1000 cars; for an empty list the answer is `0`.

Example: `12`, positions `[10, 8, 0, 5, 3]`, speeds `[2, 4, 1, 1, 3]` → `3`.

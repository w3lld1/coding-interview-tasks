# Asteroid collisions

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-17?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Asteroids fly along one line at the same speed. The sign of a number gives the direction (positive moves right, negative moves left) and the absolute value gives the size; there are no zeros. The list order is the left-to-right position order. When two asteroids meet, the smaller one explodes; if they are equal, both explode; the larger one keeps flying. Asteroids moving in the same direction never meet. Return the asteroids that remain after all collisions, left to right; it may be empty.

Example: `[5, 10, -5]` → `[5, 10]` (the `-5` explodes against `10`).

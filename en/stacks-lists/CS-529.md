# When customers leave the tills

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-529?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A shop has `tills` tills (`tills ≥ 1`), all free from minute `0`. All customers stand in one common line, and serving them takes `durations[0]`, `durations[1]`, ... minutes. Customers go in line order; each walks to the till that becomes free earliest (on a tie, the till with the smaller number) and is served at once when it is free. Return the list of moments when each customer's service ends, in line order.

Example: `2`, `[5, 3, 4, 1]` → `[5, 3, 7, 6]` (the first at till 0 until 5, the second at till 1 until 3, the third at till 1 from 3 to 7, the fourth at till 0 from 5 to 6).

# Simple Linked List

**Difficulty:** medium · **Language:** Python · **Topics:** [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/), [Classes and OOP](../oop/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/PY-12?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement a singly linked list whose nodes store a value and a reference to the next node. Support pushing and popping an element at the head, getting the head and the length, iteration, and building the list in reverse order. When creating a list from a sequence, push its elements onto the head one by one. Getting the head of an empty list or popping from it must raise EmptyListException.

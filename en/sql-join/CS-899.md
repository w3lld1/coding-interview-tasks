# How many full days passed before the first booking

**Difficulty:** easy · **Language:** SQL (SQLite) · **Topics:** [SQL: joins](../sql-join/), [SQL: aggregation](../sql-aggregate/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-899?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

For each club member (without the guest, `memid = 0`) who has bookings output the columns `memid`, `surname` and `full_days`, the number of full days (24 hours each) from the moment of joining (`joindate`, with its time) to the start of their first booking. A partial day does not count: 6 days and 14 hours give 6. The row order does not matter.

The tables and data are already in the starter code and are created afresh on every run (SQLite):

- `facilities(facid, name, membercost, guestcost, initialoutlay, monthlymaintenance)` — club facilities; `membercost` and `guestcost` are the price of one slot for a member and for a guest.
- `members(memid, surname, firstname, address, zipcode, telephone, recommendedby, joindate)` — members; `recommendedby` is the `memid` of who invited them, or `NULL`; `memid = 0` is a guest.
- `bookings(bookid, facid, memid, starttime, slots)` — bookings; one slot is 30 minutes; `starttime` is text such as `2012-09-14 09:00:00`.

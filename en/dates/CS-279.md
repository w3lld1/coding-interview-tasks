# Date after N days

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-279?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a date and an integer number of days (it may be negative or zero). Return the date that is that many days after the given one (earlier for a negative number), in the same format `YYYY-MM-DD` with leading zeros. The result always falls in the years 0001 to 9999.

A date is a string `YYYY-MM-DD` of exactly 10 characters: a four-digit year (from `0001`), a two-digit month and a two-digit day. The calendar is Gregorian with no time zones; a year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4 and not by 100, or divisible by 400. A date that does not exist (`2023-02-29`, month `13`) is invalid. If the input date is invalid, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

Example: `"2024-02-28"`, `2` → `"2024-03-01"`.

# Mirror clock readings

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-616?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A digital clock shows the time `"HH:MM"` (`00:00`-`23:59`, always two digits for hours and for minutes). A reading is a "mirror" if its four digits without the colon read the same left to right and right to left (for example `12:21`, `00:00`, `23:32`; `10:10` is not). You get `start` and `end`, two readings of the same day, `start <= end`. Return how many readings from `start` to `end` inclusive are mirror ones (the clock shows every minute).

Example: `"10:00"`, `"11:11"` → `2` (`10:01` and `11:11`).

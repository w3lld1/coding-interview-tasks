# Leave requests with accrual and carry-over

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-640?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

An employee was hired on `hire_date` (`"YYYY-MM-DD"`). Leave days accrue like this: on the first day of every month, starting with the first "first of the month" not earlier than the hire date (if hired on the 1st, from that day), `accrual` days are added to the balance. Before the January 1 accrual the balance is cut to `carry_cap` if it is larger. At first the balance is `0`.

The requests `requests` are strings `"SUBMITTED FROM TO"` of three dates: the submission date and the leave from the first date to the second inclusive. Requests are decided by submission date (ties in list order), and at that point every accrual and cut dated no later than the submission date has happened. A request costs the number of working days (Mon–Fri, except the dates in `holidays`) in its range that are not already covered by approved leave. If the cost does not exceed the balance, the request is approved, the cost is deducted and its working days become covered; otherwise it is rejected and changes nothing.

Return for every request, in list order, the string `"approved N"` or `"rejected N"`, where `N` is its cost. All input is valid: the dates exist, a submission date is not before `hire_date`, and a leave starts no later than it ends. The calendar is Gregorian.

Example: `"2024-01-15"`, `["2024-03-10 2024-03-18 2024-03-22", "2024-03-20 2024-03-25 2024-03-26"]`, `[]`, `2`, `5` → `["rejected 5", "approved 2"]`.

# SLA deadline in business hours

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-632?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A support desk promises to answer a ticket within `sla_minutes` business minutes. You get the opening moment `opened` (`"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"`), the weekly schedule `hours` — exactly 7 strings from Monday to Sunday — and a list of holidays `holidays` (`"YYYY-MM-DD"`; the desk is closed all day on a holiday). A schedule string is empty (a day off) or holds comma-separated windows `"HH:MM-HH:MM"`, for example `"09:00-13:00,14:00-18:00"`; a window is open from its start inclusive to its end exclusive.

Return the deadline — the earliest moment by which exactly `sla_minutes` business minutes have passed since `opened` — as `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"`. If the count ends exactly at the end of a window, the deadline is that end (for example `"18:00"`), not the start of the next window. With `sla_minutes = 0` the deadline is `opened` itself. The calendar is Gregorian with no time zones; `sla_minutes` is at most 100000.

Report an error (throw an exception, or return an error in Go) if `sla_minutes < 0`, there are not 7 schedule strings, a window is malformed, hours are not 00 to 23 or minutes not 00 to 59, a window is empty or starts before the end of the previous window of the same day, a date is not `"YYYY-MM-DD"` or does not exist, `opened` is malformed, or `sla_minutes > 0` while the schedule has no window at all.

Example: `"2024-03-08 16:30"` (a Friday), `240`, weekdays `"09:00-13:00,14:00-18:00"`, weekend `""`, `[]` → `"2024-03-11 11:30"`.

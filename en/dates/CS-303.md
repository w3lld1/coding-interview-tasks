# Longest gap between logins

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-303?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a user login log: strings `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"` in any order (repeats are possible). Return the longest gap in minutes between two logins that are neighbours in time. The calendar is Gregorian, with no time zones; mind month lengths and leap years. With fewer than two logins return `0`. There are at most 1000 logins.

Example: `["2024-03-01 22:00", "2024-03-01 23:30", "2024-03-02 08:00"]` → `510` (from 23:30 to 08:00 the next day).

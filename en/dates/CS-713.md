# Cheapest travel passes with a purchase limit

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-713?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get travel dates `travel_days` (in any order, possibly repeated) and the prices of three passes `prices = [day, week, month]`: a day pass covers one date; a week pass covers seven consecutive dates starting on any date; a month pass covers one whole calendar month (for example, all of February 2024). You may buy at most `max_passes` passes. Return the smallest total for which every travel date is covered by at least one pass, or `-1` if that is impossible with this limit (with no travel the answer is `0`).

Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` in the Gregorian calendar with a year from 2000 to 9999. There are up to 1000 dates, and prices are 1 to 10⁶.

Report an error (throw an exception, or return an error in Go) if a date does not follow the format, is out of bounds or does not exist, `prices` does not hold three numbers or has a price below 1, or `max_passes` is negative.

Example: `["2024-03-01", "2024-03-04", "2024-03-05", "2024-03-07", "2024-03-20", "2024-03-29", "2024-04-01"]`, `[4, 15, 40]`, `2` → `44`.

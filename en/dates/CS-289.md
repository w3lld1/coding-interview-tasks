# How many times a weekday occurs in a month

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Math](../math/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-289?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a year, a month number (1 to 12) and an English weekday name: `"Monday"`, `"Tuesday"`, `"Wednesday"`, `"Thursday"`, `"Friday"`, `"Saturday"` or `"Sunday"` (exactly like that, capitalised). Return how many times this weekday occurs in that month. The calendar is Gregorian, a leap year is divisible by 4 and not by 100, or divisible by 400. `2024-03-04` is a Monday.

If the year is below 1, the month is not from 1 to 12 or the weekday name is not in the list, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

Example: `2024`, `3`, `"Friday"` → `5` (1, 8, 15, 22 and 29 March).

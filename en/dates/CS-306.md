# Shared minutes of two shifts

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-306?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Two employees have shift schedules for one day. Each schedule is a list of strings `"HH:MM-HH:MM"` (start strictly before end); shifts inside one schedule are in ascending order and do not overlap (they may touch). Return how many minutes both employees are on shift at the same time. If one of the schedules is empty the answer is `0`. Touching shifts of different employees share nothing: "until 10:00" and "from 10:00" give 0 common minutes.

Example: `["09:00-13:00", "14:00-18:00"]`, `["12:00-15:00"]` → `120` (12:00–13:00 and 14:00–15:00).

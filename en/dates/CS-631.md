# Recurring event with exceptions

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-631?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A calendar event repeats by a rule. You get the date of the first time `start` (`"YYYY-MM-DD"`, year 1900 to 2100) and a rule `rule` — parts `KEY=VALUE` separated by `;` in any order:
- `FREQ` — `DAILY`, `WEEKLY` or `MONTHLY` (required);
- `COUNT` — how many times the event happens by the rule, 1 to 50 (required);
- `INTERVAL` — a step of 1 to 12 days, weeks or months (default `1`);
- `BYDAY` — `WEEKLY` only: comma-separated weekdays from `MO,TU,WE,TH,FR,SA,SU` (default: the weekday of `start`).

`DAILY`: the dates `start`, `start + INTERVAL` days and so on. `WEEKLY`: weeks run Monday to Sunday and the week of `start` is week zero; the event happens in weeks `0, INTERVAL, 2·INTERVAL, …` on the days in `BYDAY`, never before `start`. `MONTHLY`: on the day of month of `start` every `INTERVAL` months; a month without that day is skipped and not counted. The first `COUNT` dates by the rule are the original dates.

Exceptions `exceptions`: `"-YYYY-MM-DD"` cancels an original date, `"YYYY-MM-DD>YYYY-MM-DD"` moves an original date to another one. An exception for a date that is not an original date does nothing. Cancelling and moving apply to an original date: cancelling `D` does not cancel an event moved onto `D` from another date; each original date appears in at most one exception. Return, without repeats and in increasing order, the dates of the final schedule inside `[range_from, range_to]` inclusive. All input is valid; the calendar is Gregorian.

Example: `"2024-01-31"`, `"FREQ=MONTHLY;COUNT=3"`, `[]`, `"2024-01-01"`, `"2024-12-31"` → `["2024-01-31", "2024-03-31", "2024-05-31"]`.

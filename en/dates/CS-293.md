# Fiscal quarter

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-293?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A company's fiscal year starts in the month with number `start_month` (1 to 12). Its first quarter is that month and the next two, the second quarter is the next three months, and so on, wrapping around the end of the calendar year; there are four quarters of three months. You get a valid date as `YYYY-MM-DD` and the start month of the fiscal year. Return the number of the fiscal quarter (1 to 4) that contains the date. The year and the day do not affect the answer.

Example: `"2024-10-01"`, `7` → `2` (the first quarter is July, August, September).

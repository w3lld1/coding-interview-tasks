# Due date in working days

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-310?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A task is issued on day `start` (`"YYYY-MM-DD"`) and is due after `working_days` working days. Return the due date as `"YYYY-MM-DD"`. A working day is not a Saturday, not a Sunday and not a day in `holidays` (which may contain repeats and weekends). Go forward from `start`: every next working day lowers the remaining count by one. The day `start` itself is not counted. If `working_days` is `0`, return `start` unchanged even when it is a weekend. The calendar is Gregorian, `working_days` is at most 1000.

Example: `"2024-03-08"` (a Friday), `1`, `[]` → `"2024-03-11"`.

# Support on-call: rotation and overrides

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-707?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement an on-call schedule. A moment is a string `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"` (Gregorian calendar, year 2000 to 9999, hours `00`–`23`, minutes `00`–`59`, no time zones); a range `[from, to)` holds the minutes from `from` up to but not including `to`, and `from` is earlier than `to`. A name is a non-empty string of Latin letters, case-sensitive. You get a list of operations `operations` and a list of their arguments `values` (`values[i]` belongs to `operations[i]`; all arguments are strings). Return a list of results, one per operation. At first there is no rotation and no override.

- `"rotation"` `[anchor, hours, names]`: sets the rotation, replacing the previous one. `names` are comma-separated names (they may repeat); from the moment `anchor` on, shifts of `hours` hours (an integer from 1 to 1000, up to four digits) go to the first name, then the next ones in a circle; before `anchor` nobody is on call by the rotation. Overrides do not depend on the rotation and stay in force under a new one. Result `"ok"`.
- `"override"` `[from, to, name]`: in the range `name` is on call instead of the rotation and instead of earlier overrides. Result `"ok"`.
- `"clear"` `[from, to]`: removes all overrides in the range (the rotation applies there again). Result `"ok"`.
- `"who"` `[moment]`: who is on call at that minute: the override's name, otherwise the rotation's, otherwise `"-"`.
- `"minutes"` `[name, from, to]`: how many minutes of the range `name` is on call, as a decimal string (the number may not fit in 32 bits).

Report an error (throw an exception, or return an error in Go) for the whole call if an operation is unknown or has the wrong number of arguments, a moment does not follow the format, is out of bounds or names a date that does not exist, `hours` is invalid, a name is empty or has anything but Latin letters, or `from` is not earlier than `to`.

Example: `["rotation", "override", "who", "minutes"]`, `[["2024-03-04 09:00", "24", "ann,bob,cid"], ["2024-03-05 12:00", "2024-03-05 18:00", "cid"], ["2024-03-05 12:00"], ["bob", "2024-03-04 00:00", "2024-03-11 00:00"]]` → `["ok", "ok", "cid", "2520"]`.

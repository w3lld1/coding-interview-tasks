# Age in full years

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-280?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a birth date and today's date. Return the age in full years: the difference of the years, reduced by 1 if the birthday has not happened yet this year. The birthday has happened when the pair "month, day" of today is not less than that of the birth date; so a person born on 29 February gets a year older on 1 March in a non-leap year.

A date is a string `YYYY-MM-DD` of exactly 10 characters: a four-digit year (from `0001`), a two-digit month and a two-digit day. The calendar is Gregorian with no time zones; a year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4 and not by 100, or divisible by 400. A date that does not exist (`2023-02-29`, month `13`) is invalid. If either date is invalid or the birth date is after today, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

Example: `"2000-05-15"`, `"2024-05-14"` → `23`.

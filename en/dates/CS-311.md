# Validated work log

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-311?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a work log: a list of strings `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM-HH:MM"` (the day, then start and end of work). Return the total number of worked minutes.

A log line must be exactly `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM-HH:MM"` (digits in their places), the date must exist in the Gregorian calendar, hours 00 to 23, minutes 00 to 59, the end strictly after the start. Entries of one date must not overlap (touching is fine). In any other case report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go. The first bad entry makes the whole call an error. An empty log gives `0`.

Example: `["2024-03-04 09:00-12:30", "2024-03-05 08:15-09:00"]` → `255`.

# Product price on given dates

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-643?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A shop keeps a log of price changes `changes`: strings `"YYYY-MM-DD PRICE"` in any order; a price holds from its date inclusive until the next change. If several changes share a date, the one later in the list wins. For every date in `queries` (`"YYYY-MM-DD"`) return the price in effect that day — the price of the latest change dated no later than the query; if there is none, return `0`. The answer is a list in the order of `queries`.

The dates are valid (Gregorian calendar), prices are integers from 1 to 99999, and there are up to 5000 changes and up to 5000 queries.

Example: `["2024-03-01 120", "2024-01-15 100", "2024-06-10 150"]`, `["2024-01-01", "2024-02-29", "2024-06-10"]` → `[0, 100, 150]`.

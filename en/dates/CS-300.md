# Busy minutes in a calendar

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-300?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get the meetings of one day, each a string `"HH:MM-HH:MM"` (24-hour clock, start strictly before end, both inside the same day). The meetings come in any order and may overlap or repeat. Return how many minutes of the day are taken by at least one meeting: time covered by several meetings counts once. Back-to-back meetings simply add up. For an empty list return `0`. There are at most 1000 meetings.

Example: `["09:00-10:30", "10:00-11:00", "13:00-14:00"]` → `180` (09:00 to 11:00 is 120 minutes, plus one hour after lunch).

# 12-hour to 24-hour time

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-285?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a time in the 12-hour format `H:MM AM` or `H:MM PM`: an hour from 1 to 12 (one or two digits, a leading zero is allowed: `09:30 AM`), a colon, two-digit minutes `00` to `59`, one space and exactly `AM` or `PM` in capital letters. Return the same time in the 24-hour format `HH:MM` with a leading zero. `12:00 AM` is `00:00`, `12:00 PM` is `12:00`.

If the string does not fit this format, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

Example: `"1:15 PM"` → `"13:15"`.

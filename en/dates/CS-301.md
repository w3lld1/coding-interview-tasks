# Free slots in a workday

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-301?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

The workday runs from `day_start` to `day_end` (`"HH:MM"` strings). You get a list of meetings `"HH:MM-HH:MM"` in any order; meetings may overlap and may stick out of the workday. Return the free slots inside the workday that last at least `min_length` minutes (`min_length` ≥ 1), in chronological order, as `"HH:MM-HH:MM"` strings. A slot ends exactly where busy time begins. If there is no suitable slot, return an empty list.

Example: `["10:00-11:00", "13:00-14:00"]`, `"09:00"`, `"18:00"`, `30` → `["09:00-10:00", "11:00-13:00", "14:00-18:00"]`.

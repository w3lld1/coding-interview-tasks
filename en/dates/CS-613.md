# Minutes between two moments

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-613?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get two moments `start` and `end` in the format `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"` (24-hour clock, Gregorian calendar, no time zones, no daylight saving, no leap seconds). Both are valid and `start` is not later than `end`. Return the number of whole minutes between them.

Example: `"2024-03-10 23:30"`, `"2024-03-11 00:15"` → `45`.

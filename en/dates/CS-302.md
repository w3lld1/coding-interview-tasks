# Parking: hours, night and a cap

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-302?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A car entered a parking lot at `entry` and left at `exit` (`"HH:MM"` strings). If `exit` is not later than `entry`, the car left on the next day (equal times mean exactly 24 hours). The first `free_minutes` minutes are free. The rest is billed by **started** hours: after the free period the first hour starts at minute `entry + free_minutes`, the next one 60 minutes later, and so on while the car has not left yet. The price of an hour depends on when it starts: an hour that starts from 22:00 to 05:59 inclusive costs `night_rate`, all others cost `day_rate`. The total is at most `daily_cap`. If the stay fits into the free period, the fee is `0`.

Example: `"21:30"`, `"00:30"`, `0`, `100`, `60`, `1000` → `220` (the hour from 21:30 costs 100, from 22:30 costs 60, from 23:30 costs 60).

# Working days between dates

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-309?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get dates `start` and `end` (`"YYYY-MM-DD"`, `start` not later than `end`, at most 3700 days apart) and a list of holidays. Return how many days from `start` to `end` inclusive are working days. A working day is not a Saturday, not a Sunday and not a holiday. The holiday list may have repeats, dates on weekends and dates outside the range; they change nothing beyond what is said. The calendar is Gregorian.

Example: `"2024-03-04"` (a Monday), `"2024-03-10"`, `["2024-03-06"]` → `4`.

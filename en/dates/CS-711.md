# World clock with daylight saving time

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-711?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement a world clock for meetings. A zone has a standard offset from UTC and a daylight saving rule; during summer time the offset is 60 minutes larger:
- `"none"`: no summer time;
- `"eu"`: from the last Sunday of March 01:00 UTC to the last Sunday of October 01:00 UTC;
- `"us"`: from the second Sunday of March 02:00 local standard time to the first Sunday of November 02:00 local summer time (that is 01:00 standard).
The start belongs to summer time, the end does not. Local time = UTC + offset. A meeting stores its local time and zone; its UTC moment is recomputed with the zone's current definition. If the local time does not exist (a jump forward), take UTC = local − standard offset; if it occurs twice, take the earlier moment.

You get a list of operations `operations` and a list of their arguments `values` (`values[i]` belongs to `operations[i]`; all arguments are strings). Return a list of results, one per operation. At first there are no zones and no meetings.

- `"zone"` `[name, offset, rule]`: defines or redefines a zone; `offset` is `"+HH:MM"` or `"-HH:MM"` from `-12:00` to `+14:00`. Result `"ok"`.
- `"meet"` `[id, zone, local]`: stores a meeting; the result is its UTC moment.
- `"utc"` `[id]`: the meeting's UTC moment.
- `"local"` `[id, zone]`: the meeting's moment in the local time of `zone`.
- `"order"` `[]`: the `id`s of all meetings, comma-separated, by increasing UTC moment, ties by `id` in lexicographic order; `"-"` if there are no meetings.

Moments are `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"` in the Gregorian calendar; in the `local` argument of `meet` the year is 2001 to 9998. A zone name has Latin letters, digits and `/`, an `id` Latin letters and digits, both non-empty.

Report an error (throw an exception, or return an error in Go) for the whole call if an operation is unknown or has the wrong number of arguments, a name, `id`, offset or rule is invalid, a moment does not follow the format, is out of bounds or does not exist, a zone is unknown, the `id` of `meet` already exists, or the `id` of another operation does not.

Example: `["zone", "zone", "meet", "local"]`, `[["Berlin", "+01:00", "eu"], ["NewYork", "-05:00", "us"], ["standup", "Berlin", "2024-03-29 15:00"], ["standup", "NewYork"]]` → `["ok", "ok", "2024-03-29 14:00", "2024-03-29 10:00"]`.

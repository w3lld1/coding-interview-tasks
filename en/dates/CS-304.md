# Longest run of consecutive days

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-304?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a list of activity dates `"YYYY-MM-DD"` in any order, with possible repeats. Return the length of the longest run of consecutive calendar days. The calendar is Gregorian: mind month lengths, year changes and 29 February in leap years. Repeats of one day count once. For an empty list return `0`. There are at most 10000 dates.

Example: `["2024-02-28", "2024-03-01", "2024-02-29"]` → `3` (28 February, 29 February and 1 March 2024).

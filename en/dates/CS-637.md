# First common meeting slot across time zones

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-637?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

The participants live in different time zones: `offsets[i]` is participant `i`'s offset from UTC as `"+HH:MM"` or `"-HH:MM"` (from `-12:00` to `+14:00`, no daylight saving time). Each works Monday to Friday from `09:00` to `17:00` in their own local time. `busy[i]` lists the participant's busy spans in UTC as `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM/YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"` (start inclusive, end exclusive; spans may overlap and come in any order).

Find the earliest UTC start of a meeting of `length` minutes (1 to 480), no earlier than the moment `after` (`"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"`, UTC) and strictly earlier than `after` plus 14 days, such that for every participant the whole meeting lies inside one of their working windows (a local weekday, `09:00`–`17:00`) and does not overlap their busy spans. Return the start as `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"` in UTC, or `""` if there is none. There are 1 to 10 participants, the calendar is Gregorian and all input is valid.

Example: `["+03:00", "+00:00"]`, `[[], ["2024-03-04 09:00/2024-03-04 11:00"]]`, `"2024-03-04 00:00"`, `60` → `"2024-03-04 11:00"`.

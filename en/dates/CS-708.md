# Nightly prices and bookings of a flat

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-708?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement the price and booking calendar of one flat. A date is `YYYY-MM-DD` in the Gregorian calendar with a year from 2000 to 9999; the night of date `d` is the night from `d` to the next day. A range `[from, to)` is the nights of the dates from `from` up to but not including `to`, with `from` earlier than `to` (check-in and check-out work the same way). You get a list of operations `operations` and a list of their arguments `values` (`values[i]` belongs to `operations[i]`; all arguments are strings). Return a list of results, one per operation. At first no night has a price (sales are closed) and there are no bookings.

- `"rate"` `[from, to, price]`: the nights of the range get the price `price` (one to six digits, a number from 1 to 100000). Result: the number of runs, the longest stretches of consecutive priced nights that all have one price.
- `"close"` `[from, to]`: the nights of the range lose their price. Result: the number of runs.
- `"undo"` `[]`: reverts the latest `rate` or `close` not yet reverted (bookings stay). Result: the number of runs, or `"none"` if there is nothing to revert.
- `"quote"` `[checkin, checkout]`: `"closed"` if some night has no price; otherwise `"busy"` if some night is booked; otherwise the sum of the nights' prices as a decimal string.
- `"book"` `[checkin, checkout]`: the same as `quote`, and on success the nights get booked. Later price changes do not change bookings.
- `"cancel"` `[checkin]`: removes the booking with that check-in date: `"ok"`, or `"none"` if there is no such booking.

The sum may not fit in 32 bits. Report an error (throw an exception, or return an error in Go) for the whole call if an operation is unknown or has the wrong number of arguments, a date does not follow the format, is out of bounds or does not exist, `from` is not earlier than `to`, or `price` is invalid.

Example: `["rate", "rate", "book", "quote"]`, `[["2024-06-01", "2024-09-01", "120"], ["2024-07-01", "2024-08-01", "150"], ["2024-06-29", "2024-07-02"], ["2024-07-01", "2024-07-03"]]` → `["1", "3", "390", "busy"]`.

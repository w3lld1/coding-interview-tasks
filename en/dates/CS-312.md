# Subscription renewal dates

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-312?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A subscription started on day `start` (`"YYYY-MM-DD"`) and renews every month on the same day of the month. Return the first `count` renewal dates (`count` from 0 to 120): 1 month after `start`, 2 months after, and so on, ascending, as `"YYYY-MM-DD"` strings. If the target month has no such day (for example the 31st in April), the renewal falls on the last day of that month. The day is always taken from the original `start`, not from the previous renewal: from 31 January you get 29 February, then 31 March. The calendar is Gregorian.

Example: `"2024-01-31"`, `4` → `["2024-02-29", "2024-03-31", "2024-04-30", "2024-05-31"]`.

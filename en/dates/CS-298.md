# Timestamp check

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-298?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a string. Return `true` if it is a valid timestamp of the form `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS` (a date, one space, a time), and `false` otherwise (an empty string included). Date: a four-digit year from `0001`, a two-digit month and day, and a day that exists in the Gregorian calendar (a year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4 and not by 100, or divisible by 400). Time: hours `00`-`23`, minutes and seconds `00`-`59` (no leap seconds), each with exactly two digits. There are no time zones.

Example: `"2024-02-29 23:59:59"` → `true`, `"2023-02-29 12:00:00"` → `false`.

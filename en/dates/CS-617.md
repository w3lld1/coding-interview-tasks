# Angle between clock hands

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-617?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a time `time` in the format `"HH:MM"` (24-hour format, `00:00`-`23:59`). The hands of an analog clock with a 12-hour dial move smoothly: the hour hand makes a full turn in `12` hours, the minute hand in `60` minutes. Return the smaller of the two angles between the hands in degrees (a number from `0` to `180`, possibly fractional).

Example: `"03:15"` → `7.5`.

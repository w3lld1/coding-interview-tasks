# Scheduled reminder with pauses

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-710?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement a reminder. A moment is `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"` (Gregorian calendar, year 2000 to 9999, hours `00`–`23`, minutes `00`–`59`, no time zones); a range `[from, to)` is the minutes from `from` up to but not including `to`, with `from` earlier than `to`. You get a list of operations `operations` and a list of their arguments `values` (`values[i]` belongs to `operations[i]`; all arguments are strings). Return a list of results, one per operation. At first there is no rule and no pause.

- `"rule"` `[days, start, end, every]`: sets the rule, replacing the previous one. `days` are comma-separated weekdays without repeats (`Mo`, `Tu`, `We`, `Th`, `Fr`, `Sa`, `Su`); on each such day the reminder fires at `start`, `start + every`, `start + 2·every`, … while the time is earlier than `end` (`start` and `end` are `HH:MM` from `00:00` to `23:59`, `start` earlier than `end`; `every` is one to four digits, a number of minutes from 1 to 1440). Result `"ok"`.
- `"pause"` `[from, to]`: adds a pause; firings inside a pause are dropped. Overlapping and touching pauses merge. Result: the number of separate pauses.
- `"resume"` `[from, to]`: removes the pause from this range (a pause may get cut in two). Result: the number of separate pauses.
- `"next"` `[moment]`: the first firing not before `moment` and not in a pause, in the moment format; `"none"` if there is no rule or no such firing up to `9999-12-31 23:59` inclusive.
- `"count"` `[from, to]`: the number of firings in the range outside pauses, as a decimal string (`"0"` without a rule).

Report an error (throw an exception, or return an error in Go) for the whole call if an operation is unknown or has the wrong number of arguments, a moment or time does not follow its format, is out of bounds or names a date that does not exist, the weekdays are invalid or repeat, `start` is not earlier than `end`, `every` is invalid, or `from` is not earlier than `to`.

Example: `["rule", "next", "pause", "next"]`, `[["Mo,We,Fr", "09:00", "18:00", "90"], ["2024-03-04 09:01"], ["2024-03-06 00:00", "2024-03-07 00:00"], ["2024-03-05 12:00"]]` → `["ok", "2024-03-04 10:30", "1", "2024-03-08 09:00"]`.

# Duty rota over dates with constraints

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-634?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Build a duty rota for the days from `start` to `end` inclusive (`"YYYY-MM-DD"`, at most 14 days). The staff `staff` are up to 6 distinct names made of Latin letters. A weekday (Mon–Fri) needs exactly `weekday_need` people on duty, Saturday and Sunday exactly `weekend_need`. Strings `unavailable` like `"Name YYYY-MM-DD"` mean that person cannot be on duty that day. Nobody is on duty more than `max_streak` days in a row or more than `max_shifts` days in the whole period.

Among all valid rotas return the smallest in this order: days are compared one by one from the first, and a day's team — its alphabetically sorted list of names — is compared as a list of strings (by character codes). Write the rota as a list of strings `"YYYY-MM-DD:Name1,Name2"`, one per day, the day's names in alphabetical order; a day with nobody on duty is `"YYYY-MM-DD:"`. If no valid rota exists, return `[]`. The calendar is Gregorian.

Example: `"2024-03-08"`, `"2024-03-11"`, `["Cid", "Ann", "Bob"]`, `2`, `1`, `["Ann 2024-03-09"]`, `2`, `3` → `["2024-03-08:Ann,Bob", "2024-03-09:Bob", "2024-03-10:Ann", "2024-03-11:Ann,Bob"]`.

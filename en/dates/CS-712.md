# How many orders the workshop finishes on time

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-712?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A workshop handles orders one at a time, starting on the date `start`. Working days are Monday to Friday, except the dates in `holidays` (the list may have repeats, weekend days and dates before `start`; those change nothing). Order `i` takes `durations[i]` consecutive working days; the workshop may do the orders in any order without idle days and may refuse any of them. An order is ready on its last working day and is on time if that day is not after the date `deadlines[i]` (if `start` is a working day, it is the first working day). Return the largest number of orders that can be done on time.

Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` in the Gregorian calendar with a year from 2000 to 9999. There are up to 1000 orders with durations up to 1000 days.

Report an error (throw an exception, or return an error in Go) if a date does not follow the format, is out of bounds or does not exist, a duration is less than 1, or `durations` and `deadlines` differ in length.

Example: `"2024-03-04"`, `["2024-03-08"]`, `[3, 2, 4, 1]`, `["2024-03-07", "2024-03-06", "2024-03-12", "2024-03-05"]` → `2`.

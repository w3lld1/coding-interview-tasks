# Days until a birthday

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-286?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get today's date as `YYYY-MM-DD` and a birthday as `MM-DD` (both are valid; Gregorian calendar, a leap year is divisible by 4 and not by 100, or divisible by 400). Return how many days remain until the nearest birthday: `0` if it is today. A birthday of `02-29` exists only in leap years, so the nearest one is in the closest leap year (this year or later).

Example: `"2024-03-10"`, `"03-09"` → `364` (the next one is 9 March 2025).

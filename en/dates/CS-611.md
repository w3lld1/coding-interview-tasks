# Leap years in a range

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-611?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get two years `start_year` and `end_year`, `1 <= start_year <= end_year`. Return how many leap years lie between them, both ends included. In the Gregorian calendar a year is leap if it is divisible by `4` but not by `100`; years divisible by `400` are leap too.

Example: `1896`, `1904` → `2` (1896 and 1904 are leap, 1900 is not).

# Day number in the year

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-290?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a date. Return the number of that day within its year: 1 January is `1`, 31 December is `365`, or `366` in a leap year.

A date is a string `YYYY-MM-DD` of exactly 10 characters: a four-digit year (from `0001`), a two-digit month and a two-digit day. The calendar is Gregorian with no time zones; a year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4 and not by 100, or divisible by 400. A date that does not exist (`2023-02-29`, month `13`) is invalid. If the date is invalid or the string has another shape, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

Example: `"2024-03-01"` → `61`.

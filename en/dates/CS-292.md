# Shift a clock time

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-292?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a valid time `HH:MM` (24-hour format) and an integer number of minutes that may be negative or longer than a day. Return the time the clock will show after that many minutes (earlier for a negative number). The clock goes around: after `23:59` comes `00:00`; there is no date. The result is `HH:MM` with leading zeros.

Example: `"23:50"`, `20` → `"00:10"`.

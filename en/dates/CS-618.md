# Payday dates

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-618?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A company pays salaries on the last working day of each month. Working days are Monday to Friday; holidays are ignored. You get a year `year` (from `1600` to `9999`, Gregorian calendar). Return a list of `12` numbers: for each month from January to December, the day of the month on which the payment happens.

Example: `2023` → `[31, 28, 31, 28, 31, 30, 31, 31, 29, 31, 30, 29]`.

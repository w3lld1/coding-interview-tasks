# Validate an mm-dd-yyyy date

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-124?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a string, return `true` if it is a valid date written strictly as `mm-dd-yyyy`: a two-digit month, a hyphen, a two-digit day, a hyphen, a four-digit year. No other characters are allowed, and an empty string is invalid.

The month must be 1 to 12. The day must be at least 1 and at most: 31 for months 1, 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12; 30 for months 4, 6, 9, 11; 29 for month 2 (leap years are not checked, so February 29 is allowed in any year). Any year is fine.

Example: `"03-11-2000"` → `true`, `"04-31-3000"` → `false`.

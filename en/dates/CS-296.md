# Duration in words

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-296?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a non-negative number of seconds. Return the duration in English words as `"<h> hours <m> minutes <s> seconds"`, where hours, minutes and seconds come from the number of seconds (hours are not converted to days), parts with a zero value are skipped, and a unit gets a plural `s` when its value is not 1 (`1 hour`, `2 hours`). Parts are separated by one space. For zero return `"0 seconds"`.

Example: `3725` → `"1 hour 2 minutes 5 seconds"`.

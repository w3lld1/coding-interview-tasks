# Parking fee

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Math](../math/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-295?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

The arrival and the departure at a car park are given in minutes from a common start (both numbers are non-negative). The stay lasts `departure − arrival` minutes. Pricing:
- a stay of up to 15 minutes inclusive is free;
- otherwise each full day (1440 minutes) costs 20;
- the remainder after the full days costs 3 per started hour, but at most 20; a remainder of 0 minutes costs 0.

Return the amount to pay. If the departure is before the arrival, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

Example: `600`, `661` → `6` (61 minutes are two started hours).

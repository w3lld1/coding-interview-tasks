# Bank queue with impatient customers

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-648?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A branch has `tellers` tellers (`tellers ≥ 1`), all free at the start of the day. The customers `customers` are listed in arrival order — strings `"HH:MM:SS SECONDS"`: the arrival time (non-decreasing along the list) and the service length in seconds (`≥ 0`). There is one shared queue: each customer in turn goes to the teller who becomes free earliest and starts being served at `max(arrival, teller free time)`; the teller becomes free one service length after the start.

A customer waits at most `patience` seconds (`patience ≥ 0`). If the wait (start minus arrival) would be longer than `patience`, the customer leaves on arrival and takes no teller. A wait of exactly `patience` seconds is fine.

Return, for every customer in list order, the service start as `"HH:MM:SS"`, or `"left"` if they left. All starts are guaranteed to fall on the same day (before `24:00:00`); there are up to 10000 customers.

Example: `["09:00:00 300", "09:01:00 60", "09:02:00 60"]`, `1`, `180` → `["09:00:00", "left", "09:05:00"]` (the second would wait 240 seconds, the third waits exactly 180).

# Playlist length

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-288?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a list of track lengths. A track length is a string `M:SS`: one to three digits of minutes (leading zeros are fine), a colon, and exactly two digits of seconds from `00` to `59`. Return the total length of the playlist as `H:MM:SS`: hours without leading zeros, minutes and seconds with two digits. An empty playlist gives `"0:00:00"`.

If any length is written wrongly, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

Example: `["3:45", "4:20"]` → `"0:08:05"`.

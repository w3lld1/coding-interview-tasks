# Meeting rooms for offices in different time zones

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-633?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Meetings are booked by people in different cities, each in their own local time. A meeting is a string `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM±HH:MM D"`: the local date and start time, the zone's offset from UTC (for example `+03:00` or `-05:30`) and the length `D` in minutes. A meeting occupies the half-open interval `[start, start + D)` in UTC, so a meeting starting exactly when another ends may take the same room.

Assign the meetings to rooms numbered from `1`. Process the meetings by increasing UTC start, ties in list order; give each meeting the lowest-numbered room that is free at its start, or a new room with the next number if none is free. Return the room numbers in the order of the input list; an empty list gives `[]`. The calendar is Gregorian, there is no daylight saving time, and there are at most 1000 meetings.

Report an error (throw an exception, or return an error in Go) if a string is not in this format, the date does not exist, the year is outside 1900–2100, the hours exceed 23 or the minutes (of the time or the offset) exceed 59, the offset is outside `-12:00`…`+14:00`, or the length is not 1 to 1440.

Example: `["2024-03-10 09:00+03:00 60", "2024-03-10 07:30+01:00 30", "2024-03-10 06:00+00:00 120"]` → `[1, 3, 2]`.

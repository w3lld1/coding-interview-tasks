# Friday the thirteenth

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-612?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a year `year` (from `1600` to `9999`, Gregorian calendar). Return the list of month numbers (from `1` to `12`) in ascending order in which the `13`th falls on a Friday. Every year has at least one such month.

Example: `2023` → `[1, 10]`.

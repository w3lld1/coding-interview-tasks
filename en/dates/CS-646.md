# Hackathon scoreboard from submission times

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-646?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A hackathon started at `start` (`"HH:MM:SS"`) and runs within one day. The submissions `submissions` are strings `"HH:MM:SS TEAM PROBLEM VERDICT"` (the verdict is `OK` or `FAIL`, every moment is not before `start`) in any order; consider them by time, equal times in list order. A team solves a problem with its first `OK` submission; its later submissions for that problem are ignored. A solved problem's penalty is the whole number of minutes from `start` to the accepted submission (rounded down) plus `20` for every `FAIL` on that problem before acceptance. Unsolved problems give no penalty.

Return the scoreboard — strings `"TEAM SOLVED PENALTY"` for every team with at least one submission: more solved first, then smaller penalty, then team name ascending (by character codes). An empty list gives `[]`.

Example: `"10:00:00"`, `["10:12:30 owls A FAIL", "10:15:00 owls A OK", "10:40:59 foxes A OK"]` → `["owls 1 35", "foxes 1 40"]`.

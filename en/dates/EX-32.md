# Delivery dates from descriptions

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-32?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a meeting start time formatted `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS` and a short delivery description, return the actual delivery date in the same format. Rules:
- `"NOW"`: two hours after the meeting started;
- `"ASAP"`: if the meeting started before 13:00, today at 17:00, otherwise tomorrow at 13:00;
- `"EOW"` (the meeting is on a Monday to Friday): if the meeting is on Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday, Friday of that week at 17:00; if on Thursday or Friday, Sunday of that week at 20:00;
- `"<N>M"` (for example `"2M"`, N from 1 to 12): at 08:00 on the first workday of the N-th month; this year if the meeting started before the N-th month, otherwise next year;
- `"Q<N>"` (for example `"Q3"`, N from 1 to 4): at 08:00 on the last workday of the N-th quarter (quarters have three months each: January to March and so on); this year if the meeting is in the N-th quarter or earlier, otherwise next year.
A workday is any day from Monday to Friday. There are no holidays, and leap years count.

Example: `"2008-12-21T14:50:00"`, `"ASAP"` → `"2008-12-22T13:00:00"`.

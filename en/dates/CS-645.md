# Suspicious repeated card payments

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-645?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A bank looks for accidentally repeated payments. The payments `payments` are strings `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS CARD AMOUNT"` (card and amount are digit strings) in any order. Consider the payments by time (equal times in list order). A payment is a repeat if there was an earlier **unflagged** payment with the same card and the same amount at most 600 seconds before it. A flagged repeat does not extend the window: the comparison is always with the last unflagged payment with that card and amount.

Return the indexes (from zero, in the original list) of the flagged payments in increasing order; if there are no repeats, return `[]`. The dates are valid, the calendar is Gregorian, and payments may cross midnight and month ends.

Example: `["2024-03-04 12:00:00 4111 500", "2024-03-04 12:04:10 4111 500", "2024-03-04 12:10:01 4111 500"]` → `[1]`.

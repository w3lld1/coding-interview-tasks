# Meetup

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/JAVA-21?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a year, a month, a selection rule and a day of the week, determine the date of the meetup. The rule is the string "first", "second", "third", "fourth" or "last" (that occurrence of the weekday in the month) or "teenth" (the only such day among dates 13 through 19 inclusive); the day of the week is its English name, such as "Monday". Return the date as a string such as "2013-05-13", taking into account the actual length of the month and leap years.

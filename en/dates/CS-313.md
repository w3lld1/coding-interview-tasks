# Guest nights per month

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-313?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A hotel stores stays as pairs of dates `["check-in", "check-out"]` in the `"YYYY-MM-DD"` format. A guest sleeps every night from the check-in date up to, but not including, the check-out date: a night belongs to the month of the date it starts on. Return a map "month `"YYYY-MM"` → number of nights of all guests together". Months without nights are not in the map. Check-out is not before check-in (equal dates give 0 nights), the calendar is Gregorian, and a single stay has at most 400 nights.

Example: `[["2024-01-30", "2024-02-02"]]` → `{"2024-01": 2, "2024-02": 1}` (nights of 30 and 31 January, and 1 February).

# Is the shop open

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-283?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A shop keeps these hours: Monday to Friday 09:00 to 18:00, Saturday 10:00 to 14:00, closed on Sunday. The opening time counts as open, the closing time does not (at `18:00` on a weekday the shop is already closed). You get a moment as `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` (a date, one space, a time). Return `true` if the shop is open at that moment, otherwise `false`. There are no holidays.

A date is a string `YYYY-MM-DD` of exactly 10 characters: a four-digit year (from `0001`), a two-digit month and a two-digit day. The calendar is Gregorian with no time zones; a year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4 and not by 100, or divisible by 400. A date that does not exist (`2023-02-29`, month `13`) is invalid. A time is a string `HH:MM` of exactly 5 characters: hours `00` to `23`, minutes `00` to `59`. If the moment is written wrongly (another length, another separator, a date or time that does not exist), report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

Example: `"2024-03-09 13:59"` (a Saturday) → `true`.

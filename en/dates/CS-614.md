# ISO 8601 week label

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-614?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a date `date` in the format `"YYYY-MM-DD"` (an existing date, Gregorian calendar). Return its ISO 8601 week as `"YYYY-Wnn"`, where `nn` is the week number in two digits.

ISO rules: a week starts on Monday; the first week of a year is the one that contains its first Thursday (so it contains `4` January). Hence early January may belong to the last week of the previous year and late December to the first week of the next; the string holds the year of the week, not the calendar year of the date.

Example: `"2021-01-03"` → `"2020-W53"`.

# Duration as a clock

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Strings](../strings/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-281?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a number of seconds. Return the duration as `H:MM:SS`: hours without leading zeros (there may be more than 24, days are not split off), minutes and seconds always with two digits.

If the number is negative, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

Example: `3661` → `"1:01:01"`.

# Days in a month

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Math](../math/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-277?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a year and a month number (1 is January, 12 is December). Return how many days that month has in the Gregorian calendar. A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4 and not by 100, or divisible by 400; February has 29 days in a leap year and 28 otherwise.

If the year is below 1 or the month is not from 1 to 12, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

Example: `2024`, `2` → `29`.

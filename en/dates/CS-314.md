# Night shift minutes

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-314?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Night time is from 22:00 to 06:00 (that is, 22:00 to midnight and midnight to 06:00). You get a list of shifts `"HH:MM-HH:MM"`. If the end of a shift is not later than its start, the shift crosses midnight and ends on the next day (equal times mean exactly 24 hours). Every shift is shorter than a day or exactly a day. Return how many minutes of all shifts fall into night time. For an empty list return `0`.

Example: `["23:00-01:00", "02:00-03:30"]` → `210` (120 minutes of the first shift and 90 of the second).

# Gigasecond

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-54?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A gigasecond is 10⁹ seconds. You get a moment in time as a string: either a date `YYYY-MM-DD` (the time is then `00:00:00`) or a date and time `YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ss`. Return the moment exactly one gigasecond later in the format `YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ss` (seconds are always printed, even when zero). There are no time zones; every day has 24 hours.

Example: `"2015-01-24T22:00:00"` → `"2046-10-02T23:46:40"`.

# Earliest arrival on daily flights

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dates and time](../dates/), [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-639?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

The airports `airports` are strings `"CODE ±HH:MM"`: a three-letter code and the offset of local time from UTC (no daylight saving time). The flights `flights` are strings `"FROM TO HH:MM D"`: the flight departs **every day** at `HH:MM` local time of the departure airport and flies `D` minutes (1 to 2000). A traveller is ready to leave `origin` at moment `ready` — local time of `origin` as `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"` — and can board any flight from `origin` departing no earlier than `ready`. At a connecting airport the next flight must depart at least `min_connection` minutes after landing.

Return the earliest possible arrival at `destination` in the local time of `destination`, formatted `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"`, or `""` if it cannot be reached. If `origin` and `destination` are the same, the answer is `ready`. The calendar is Gregorian, all input is valid, there are up to 50 airports and up to 500 flights.

Example: airports `["SVO +03:00", "IST +03:00", "LHR +00:00"]`, flights `["SVO IST 09:30 240", "IST LHR 15:00 240", "SVO LHR 12:00 270"]`, `"SVO"`, `"LHR"`, `"2024-03-04 08:00"`, `60` → `"2024-03-04 13:30"`.

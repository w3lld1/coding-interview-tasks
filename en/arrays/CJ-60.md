# Raids on the Great Wall

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-60?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

The Wall runs along the whole number line and starts at height 0 everywhere. Nomadic tribes attack it: an attack hits the segment `[w, e]` (`w < e`, every real point between them) with strength `s`. The attack is repelled if the Wall is at least `s` high at every point of the segment, otherwise it succeeds. After an attack, every point of its segment lower than `s` is raised to `s`. When several attacks happen on the same day, all of them are checked against the Wall as it was at the start of the day, and the Wall is raised only after them.

`tribes[i]` is eight numbers `[d, n, w, e, s, dd, dp, ds]`: the tribe first attacks on day `d` the segment `[w, e]` with strength `s`, attacks `n` times in total, and each next attack comes `dd` days later (`dd ≥ 1`), shifted by `dp` (negative means west) and stronger by `ds` (may be negative; every attack has strength at least 1). Return the number of successful attacks. There are up to 1000 tribes with up to 1000 attacks each, and coordinates are within 10⁸ in absolute value.

Example: `[[0, 3, 0, 2, 10, 2, 3, -2], [10, 3, 2, 3, 8, 7, 2, 0]]` → `5` (the day-10 attack on `[2, 3]` breaks through, for example at point 2.5).

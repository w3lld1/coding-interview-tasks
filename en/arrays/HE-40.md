# Three numbers summing to zero

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-40?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of integers, return `true` if three elements at three different positions sum to zero, otherwise `false`. Values may repeat: two equal numbers at different positions count as different elements. If the list has fewer than three elements, the answer is `false`.

Example: `[2, 4, -5, 3, 9, 7]` → `true` (2 + 3 − 5 = 0).

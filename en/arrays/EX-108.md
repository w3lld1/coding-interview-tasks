# Yacht dice score

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-108?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given five dice (numbers from 1 to 6, in any order) and a category name, return the score the dice give in that category; if the dice do not satisfy the category, the answer is `0`.

Categories: `"ones"`, `"twos"`, `"threes"`, `"fours"`, `"fives"`, `"sixes"` score the face times the number of dice showing it; `"full house"` is three dice of one face and two of another (five equal dice do not count), scoring the sum of all dice; `"four of a kind"` is at least four equal dice, scoring the sum of four of them (that is 4 × the face); `"little straight"` is the dice 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, scoring 30; `"big straight"` is the dice 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, scoring 30; `"choice"` scores the sum of all dice; `"yacht"` is all five dice equal, scoring 50.

Example: `[3, 3, 3, 5, 5]`, `"full house"` → `19`.

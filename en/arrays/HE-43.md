# Pair summing to zero

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-43?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of integers, return `true` if two elements at different positions add up to zero, otherwise `false`. Two zeros at different positions count, but a single zero does not pair with itself. An empty list or a list of one element gives `false`.

Example: `[2, 4, -5, 3, 5, 7]` → `true` (−5 and 5).

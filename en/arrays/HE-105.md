# Digit names in descending order

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-105?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a list of integers. Keep only the numbers from 1 to 9 inclusive, put them in descending order and replace each by its English name: `"One"`, `"Two"`, `"Three"`, `"Four"`, `"Five"`, `"Six"`, `"Seven"`, `"Eight"`, `"Nine"`. All other numbers (zero, negatives, above 9) are ignored. Duplicates are kept. If nothing qualifies, return an empty list.

Example: `[2, 1, 1, 4, 5, 8, 2, 3]` → `["Eight", "Five", "Four", "Three", "Two", "Two", "One", "One"]`.

# Even numbers at odd positions

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-85?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a non-empty list of integers, return the sum of the elements that are even and sit at odd indices (indexing starts at 0, so positions 1, 3, 5, …). If there are no such elements, the answer is `0`. Negative even numbers count too.

Example: `[4, 2, 6, 7]` → `2` (indices 1 and 3 hold `2` and `7`, and only `2` is even).

# Fresh chocolate

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-20?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Chocolate comes in packs of `pack` pieces (`2 ≤ pack ≤ 4`). Tour groups arrive one at a time; group `i` has `groups[i]` people (`1 ≤ groups[i] ≤ 100`, at most 100 groups) and every person needs exactly one piece. Leftover pieces from earlier groups are handed out first, and a new pack is opened only when no leftovers remain; packs are opened until everyone is served, and the extra pieces stay as leftovers for the next group.

A group gets only fresh chocolate if there are no leftovers when it arrives. You may choose any order of the groups. Return the largest possible number of groups that get only fresh chocolate.

Example: `[5, 6, 4, 4]`, `3` → `3` (in the order `4, 5, 6, 4` every group except the group of 5 gets only fresh chocolate).

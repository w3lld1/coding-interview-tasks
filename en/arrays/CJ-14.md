# Absorbing motes

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-14?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Your mote has size `mote`; other motes with sizes from the list `others` float around. Your mote absorbs another one only if that one is strictly smaller, and then your size grows by the absorbed size. You choose the order of absorption. Before the game you may perform operations of two kinds, in any order and any number of times: add a mote of any positive integer size, or remove any mote of the list. Return the minimum number of operations after which your mote can absorb every other mote. The list may be empty (the answer is `0`).

Example: `10`, `[25, 20, 9, 100]` → `2` (add a mote of size 3 and remove the mote of size 100; sizes may grow beyond the input limits).

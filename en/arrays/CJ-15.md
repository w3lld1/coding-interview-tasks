# Deceitful war

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-15?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Naomi and Ken each have `n` blocks; all `2n` masses are distinct and lie strictly between 0 and 1. The game has `n` rounds: Naomi picks one of her blocks, Ken picks one of his, the heavier block scores a point, and both blocks are gone.

In "War" Naomi announces the mass of her chosen block truthfully. Ken knows his own blocks and plays optimally: if he has a block heavier than the announced mass he plays the lightest such block, otherwise his lightest block. Naomi chooses the order of her blocks to score as many points as possible.

In "Deceitful War" Naomi knows all the masses beforehand and may announce any number strictly between 0 and 1 that equals no mass of Ken's blocks, provided the scale cannot give her away: the block Ken plays must be heavier than the announced number exactly when it is heavier than Naomi's real block. Ken still believes he is playing "War" and follows the same strategy on the announced number. Naomi picks blocks and announcements to score as many points as possible.

Return a list of two numbers: Naomi's score in "Deceitful War" and her score in "War".

Example: `[0.7, 0.2]`, `[0.8, 0.3]` → `[1, 0]`.

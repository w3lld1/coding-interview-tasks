# Odd-edged numbers

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-146?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of integers, return how many of them are greater than 10 and have both the first and the last digit odd (`1`, `3`, `5`, `7`, `9`). Negative numbers never qualify. For an empty list the answer is `0`.

Example: `[33, -2, -3, 45, 21, 109]` → `2` (33 and 109 qualify).

# Odd values at even positions

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-121?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a non-empty list of integers, count positions from zero: the first element is at position 0. Return the sum of the elements that are at an even position (0, 2, 4, …) and are odd themselves. Negative odd numbers count too.

Example: `[5, 8, 7, 1]` → `12` (5 at position 0 and 7 at position 2).

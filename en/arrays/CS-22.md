# Insert an interval

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-22?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a list of closed intervals `[start, end]` with integer ends, sorted by start, where no two intervals share a point. You also get one more interval. Insert it so the result stays sorted and free of shared points: every interval that shares at least one point with the new one (touching ends included) is merged into it. Return the resulting list.

Example: `[[1, 3], [6, 9]]`, `[2, 5]` → `[[1, 5], [6, 9]]`.

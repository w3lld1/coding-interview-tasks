# Subarrays with a sum in a range

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-181?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of integers `nums` (up to 20,000 elements, each at most 10^9 in absolute value) and two integers `lower` ≤ `upper`. A subarray is a non-empty run of consecutive elements. Return how many subarrays have a sum `s` with `lower` ≤ `s` ≤ `upper`. Subarrays at different positions count separately even when their sums are equal. An empty list gives `0`. Sums may not fit in 32 bits, and neither may the answer.

Aim for something faster than trying every pair of ends: O(n log n).

Example: `[4, -3, 1, 2]`, `0`, `3` → `6` (`[4, -3]`, `[1]`, `[2]`, `[-3, 1, 2]`, `[1, 2]`, `[4, -3, 1]`).

# Nearest negative and positive to zero

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-136?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of integers, return a two-element list `[a, b]`: `a` is the largest negative number (the one closest to zero from the left), `b` is the smallest positive number (closest to zero from the right). If there are no negative numbers, `a` is `null`; if there are no positive ones, `b` is `null`. Zero is neither negative nor positive.

Example: `[2, 4, 1, 3, 5, 7]` → `[null, 1]`, `[-6, -4, -3, 1]` → `[-3, 1]`.

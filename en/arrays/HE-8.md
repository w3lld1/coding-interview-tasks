# Sum and product

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-8?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of integers, return a list of two numbers: the sum of all elements and their product, in this order. For an empty list the sum is `0` and the product is `1`. Results fit in a 32-bit integer.

Example: `[1, 2, 3, 4]` → `[10, 24]`.

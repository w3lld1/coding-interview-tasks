# Fewest intervals to remove

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-24?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a list of intervals `[start, end]` with integer ends, `start < end`. Intervals are half-open: `[1, 2]` and `[2, 3]` do not overlap, while `[1, 3]` and `[2, 4]` do. Find the smallest number of intervals to remove so that no two remaining intervals overlap. Equal intervals overlap each other. For an empty list the answer is `0`.

Example: `[[1, 2], [2, 3], [3, 4], [1, 3]]` → `1` (remove `[1, 3]`).

# Largest rectangle in a histogram

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-71?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a list of non-negative integers: the heights of histogram bars standing side by side, each of width `1`. Find the area of the largest rectangle that fits entirely inside the histogram and stands on its base: it covers several consecutive bars, and its height is at most the lowest of them. For an empty list return `0`.

The list has up to 20,000 bars, each from `0` to 20,000 high. An O(n²) solution will not fit the time limit on the largest test.

Example: `[3, 1, 4, 4, 2]` → `8` (the two bars of height 4).

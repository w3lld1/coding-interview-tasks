# Minimum subarray sum

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-114?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a non-empty list of integers, find the smallest sum over all non-empty contiguous sublists (consecutive elements). If all numbers are positive, the answer is the smallest of them. Values may not fit into 32 bits.

Example: `[2, 3, 4, 1, 2, 4]` → `1`; `[-1, -2, -3]` → `-6`.

# List median

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-47?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a non-empty list of integers, return its median as a number with a fractional part: for an odd length it is the middle element of the sorted list, for an even length it is the average of the two middle elements. The original order of the elements does not matter.

Example: `[-10, 4, 6, 1000, 10, 20]` → `8.0` (the average of 6 and 10).

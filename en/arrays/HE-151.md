# Sum of odd squares

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-151?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of numbers, some of which may be fractional, return the sum of squares of the elements that are positive odd whole numbers (for example `3` and `3.0` qualify, `3.5` and `-3` do not). Zero and negative numbers are ignored. For an empty list the answer is `0`.

Example: `[1, 3, 2, 0]` → `10` (1 + 9).

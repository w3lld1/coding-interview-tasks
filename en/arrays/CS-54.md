# Gas stations on a ring road

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-54?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

There are `n` gas stations around a ring road, numbered from `0`. At station `i` you can take `gas[i]` fuel, and the drive from station `i` to the next one (after the last comes station `0`) uses `cost[i]`. The tank is unlimited and starts empty. You choose a starting station, take its fuel and only drive forward by number. The fuel must last on every leg. Return the smallest index of a starting station from which you can complete the full circle and get back to it, or `-1` if there is none. Both lists have the same length, from 1 to 1000.

Example: `[1, 2, 3, 4, 5]`, `[3, 4, 5, 1, 2]` → `3`.

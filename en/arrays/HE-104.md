# Numbers without even digits

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-104?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a list of positive integers. Return a new list of those numbers whose decimal form has no even digit (0, 2, 4, 6, 8), sorted in increasing order. Duplicates are kept. If no number qualifies, return an empty list.

Example: `[15, 33, 1422, 1]` → `[1, 15, 33]`.

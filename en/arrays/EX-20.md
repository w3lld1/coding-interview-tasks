# Best poker hands

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-20?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a list of poker hands. Each hand is a string of five cards separated by spaces; a card is a rank (`2` to `10`, `J`, `Q`, `K`, `A`) and a suit (`S`, `H`, `D`, `C`), for example `"10D JH QS KD AC"`. Return all hands of the highest strength, in any order; if several hands tie, return all of them. Cards may repeat across hands (several decks are in play).

Hand categories from strongest to weakest: straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, high card. A straight is five consecutive ranks; an ace can be high (`10 J Q K A`) or low (`A 2 3 4 5`, the weakest straight), but not in the middle (`Q K A 2 3` is not a straight). Suits matter only for a flush.

Hands of the same category are compared by ranks: first by groups of equal cards, the group with more cards first, then by the higher rank (for two pairs: the higher pair, then the lower pair, then the remaining card; for a full house: the triple, then the pair).

Example: `["4S 5H 6C 8D KH", "2S 4H 6S 4D JH"]` → `["2S 4H 6S 4D JH"]` (a pair beats a high card).

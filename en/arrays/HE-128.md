# Magnitude sum times sign product

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-128?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of integers, the sign of a number is 1 for a positive one, -1 for a negative one and 0 for zero. Return the sum of the absolute values of all numbers multiplied by the product of the signs of all numbers. For an empty list return `null`.

Example: `[1, 2, 2, -4]` → `-9` (sum of absolute values 9, product of signs -1); `[0, 1]` → `0`; `[]` → `null`.

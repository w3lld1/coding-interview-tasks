# Container with most water

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-48?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of non-negative integers (up to 1000 elements): the heights of vertical walls standing at positions `0, 1, 2, …`. Choose two different walls so that as much water as possible fits between them: the area is the distance between the positions times the smaller of the two heights. Return the greatest possible area. If there are fewer than two walls, the answer is `0`.

Example: `[1, 8, 6, 2, 5, 4, 8, 3, 7]` → `49` (walls at positions 1 and 8: `7 * 7`).

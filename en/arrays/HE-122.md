# Sum of short numbers in a prefix

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-122?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a non-empty list of integers and a number `k` (1 ≤ `k` ≤ the list length), look at the first `k` elements and return the sum of those that have at most two digits. A minus sign is not a digit: `-50` qualifies, `-100` and `100` do not.

Example: `[111, 21, 3, 4000, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9]`, `4` → `24` (21 + 3).

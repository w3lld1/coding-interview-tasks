# Dead-end parking alley

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-247?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A dead-end alley holds any number of cars, but it has a single exit: the car that entered last stands closest to the exit. Cars are distinct positive numbers; at first the alley is empty. Implement the operations:
- `enter` (id): a car drives in and stands closest to the exit; the result is the number of cars in the alley;
- `leave` (id): a car wants to go. If it is not in the alley, the result is `-1`. Otherwise every car closer to the exit pulls out temporarily, the car leaves, and the others return in their old order. The result is how many cars had to be moved (0 if it stood first at the exit);
- `front`: the result is the id of the car closest to the exit, or `0` if the alley is empty.

Return the list of results, one per operation. `values[i]` holds the arguments of operation `i`.

Example: `["enter", "enter", "enter", "leave", "front", "leave", "leave"]`, `[[5], [6], [7], [5], [], [9], [7]]` → `[1, 2, 3, 2, 7, -1, 0]`.

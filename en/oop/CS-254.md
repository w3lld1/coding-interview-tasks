# Train of cars

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-254?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A train is a sequence of cars marked with non-negative numbers (numbers may repeat); at first it is empty. Implement the operations:
- `append` (car) and `prepend` (car): couple a car at the end or at the start; the result is the train length;
- `remove_first` (car): uncouple the first car (nearest to the start) with that number. The result is `1` if there was one, otherwise `0`;
- `reverse`: turn the train around; the result is its length;
- `at` (i): the result is the number of the car at place `i` (counted from zero from the start), or `-1` if there is no such place;
- `length`: the result is the train length.

Return the list of results, one per operation. `values[i]` holds the arguments of operation `i`.

Example: `["append", "append", "prepend", "remove_first", "reverse", "at", "at", "length"]`, `[[3], [5], [9], [5], [], [0], [2], []]` → `[1, 2, 3, 1, 2, 3, -1, 2]`.

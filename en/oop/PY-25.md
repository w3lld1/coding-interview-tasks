# Bank Account

**Difficulty:** medium · **Language:** Python · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/PY-25?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement an account with opening, closing, reading the balance, depositing and withdrawing money; after opening, the balance is zero, including when reopening a previously closed account. Operations on a closed account, reopening an account that is already open, negative amounts, and withdrawing more than the balance must raise ValueError. Follow the Python version of the exercise: operations run sequentially, and thread safety is not required.

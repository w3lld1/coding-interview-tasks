# Call-centre hold queue

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-238?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Callers wait for an operator in the order they called; at first the queue is empty. A caller is a positive number (an id), and ids in the queue are distinct. Implement the operations:
- `call` (id): the caller joins the end of the queue; the result is the queue length afterwards;
- `answer`: the operator answers whoever has waited longest, and that caller leaves the queue; the result is their id, or `-1` for an empty queue;
- `hangup` (id): the caller gives up. If they were in the queue they are removed and the result is `1`; otherwise `0`;
- `position` (id): the result is the caller's place in the queue (the first one is at place `1`), or `0` if they are not there.

Return the list of results, one per operation. `values[i]` holds the arguments of operation `i`.

Example: `["call", "call", "call", "hangup", "position", "answer", "answer", "answer"]`, `[[11], [12], [13], [12], [13], [], [], []]` → `[1, 2, 3, 1, 2, 11, 13, -1]`.

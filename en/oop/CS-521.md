# Restaurant waiting list

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-521?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

In a restaurant guests wait for a table as parties; a party is given by its head count. The queue goes in order of arrival and starts empty. Operations:
- `add` (n): a party of n people joins the end of the queue; the result is the number of parties in the queue;
- `seat` (t): a table for t seats is free. The first party in the queue with at most t people is seated (earlier parties that are too big keep waiting); the result is the size of the seated party, or `0` if none fits;
- `cancel` (i): the i-th party in the queue (counting from 1) leaves; the result is its size, or `0` if there is no such party;
- `people`: the result is how many people are waiting in total;
- `parties`: the result is the number of parties in the queue.

Return the list of results, one per operation. `values[i]` holds the arguments of operation `i` (an empty list if there are none).

Example: `["add", "add", "add", "add", "people", "seat", "seat", "seat", "seat", "parties", "people"]`, `[[4], [2], [6], [3], [], [3], [5], [2], [1], [], []]` → `[1, 2, 3, 4, 15, 2, 4, 0, 0, 2, 9]`.

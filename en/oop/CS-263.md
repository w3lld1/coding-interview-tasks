# Basketball scoreboard

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-263?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement a scoreboard for two teams, numbered `1` and `2`. The score starts at `0 : 0`. You get a list of operations `operations` and a list of their arguments `values` (`values[i]` belongs to `operations[i]`). Return a list of results, one per operation.

Operations:
- `"score"` with arguments `team`, `points`: the team scored `points`; result: the new score of that team.
- `"lead"` with no arguments: result: the number of the leading team, or `0` on a tie.
- `"diff"` with no arguments: result: the absolute difference of the scores now.
- `"biggest"` with no arguments: result: the largest absolute difference that was reached after any `"score"` operation (`0` before the first one).

Example: `["score", "score", "lead", "diff"]`, `[[1, 3], [2, 2], [], []]` → `[3, 2, 1, 1]`.

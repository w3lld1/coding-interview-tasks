# Warehouse shelf split into halves

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-714?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A warehouse shelf of `2^levels` cells with addresses `0 … 2^levels − 1` (`levels` from 0 to 30) is handed out in blocks under the buddy system. A block of size `2^k` starts at an address that is a multiple of `2^k`; its buddy is the neighbouring block of the same size, and together they form a block of size `2^(k+1)` (the buddy's address is the block's address with bit `2^k` flipped). At first the whole shelf is one free block. Free buddies always merge at once.

You get a list of operations `operations` and a list of their arguments `values` (`values[i]` belongs to `operations[i]`). Return a list of results, one per operation. A request `n ≥ 1` needs the size `2^k`, the smallest power of two not below `n`.

- `"alloc"` `[n]`: among the free blocks of size at least `2^k`, take the smallest, and among those the one with the lowest address; split it in halves, keeping the left half and freeing the right one, until the size is `2^k`. Result: the block's address, or `-1` if no block fits.
- `"free"` `[addr]`: if a handed-out block starts at `addr`, free it and merge it with buddies while possible; the result is the size of the resulting free block. Otherwise `-1`.
- `"resize"` `[addr, n]`: if no handed-out block starts at `addr`, `-1`. If `2^k` is not larger than the current size, the block shrinks in place (the right halves become free), result `addr`. If it is larger: the block grows in place when `addr` is a multiple of `2^k` and every right buddy on the way up to size `2^k` is entirely free, result `addr`; otherwise the block is freed (with merging) and handed out again as by `alloc`, result the new address. If even that finds no room, everything stays as it was and the result is `-1`.
- `"largest"` `[]`: the size of the largest free block, `0` if there are none.

Example: `4`, `["alloc", "alloc", "alloc", "largest", "free", "free"]`, `[[3], [5], [1], [], [0], [4]]` → `[0, 8, 4, 2, 4, 8]`.

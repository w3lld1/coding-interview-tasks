# Ledger

**Difficulty:** medium · **Language:** JavaScript · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Dates and time](../dates/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/JS-28?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a working JavaScript module createEntry/formatEntries that prints a table of transactions for the en-US and nl-NL locales and the USD and EUR currencies; improve its structure without changing the output. Keep sorting by date, then by amount and description, the localized headers, dates and money amounts, the alignment, and the truncation of long descriptions. Make small changes, checking the output against examples after each one, and write a short log of the refactoring you did; the original module is in the starter code.

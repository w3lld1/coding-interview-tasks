# Grade book per student

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-265?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement a grade book. It is empty at first. You get a list of operations `operations` and a list of their arguments `values` (`values[i]` belongs to `operations[i]`). Students are integer numbers, grades are in `0..100`. Return a list of results, one per operation.

Operations:
- `"add_grade"` with arguments `student`, `grade`: record a grade; result: how many grades the student has now.
- `"average"` with argument `student`: result: the arithmetic mean of their grades, rounded down to an integer, or `-1` if they have none.
- `"top"` with no arguments: result: the number of the student with the highest mean (compare exactly, without rounding); on a tie the smaller number; `-1` if nobody has a grade yet.

Example: `["add_grade", "add_grade", "average", "top"]`, `[[1, 90], [1, 85], [1], []]` → `[1, 2, 87, 1]`.

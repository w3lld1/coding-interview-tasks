# Bakery ticket counter

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-244?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A bakery hands out tickets numbered 1, 2, 3, … in order, and people are served in ticket order. At first the queue is empty. Implement the operations (they take no arguments, so every `values[i]` is an empty list):
- `take`: a new customer takes the next ticket and joins the end of the queue; the result is the ticket number;
- `serve`: serve the first customer in the queue; the result is their number, or `0` for an empty queue;
- `step_aside`: the first customer lets the next two go ahead and stands right behind them (with fewer than two people behind, they go to the end; if they are alone, nothing changes). The result is the number of the new first customer, or `0` if the queue is empty;
- `waiting`: the result is the number of people in the queue.

Return the list of results, one per operation.

Example: `["take", "take", "take", "take", "step_aside", "serve", "serve", "waiting"]`, `[[], [], [], [], [], [], [], []]` → `[1, 2, 3, 4, 2, 2, 3, 2]`.

# Parking meter with coins

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Dates and time](../dates/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-308?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A parking meter takes coins and counts down paid time. Implement it as an operations list: `apply_operations(operations, values)` returns a result string for every operation (a list of the same length). At the start paid minutes are `0` and lost minutes are `0`. Paid time never exceeds 120 minutes.

Operations:
- `"insert"` `[cents]` — put in money: every 25 cents give 15 minutes. The amount must be positive and a multiple of 25, otherwise report an error. If the paid time would exceed 120, keep 120 and add the extra minutes to the "lost" minutes. The result is the paid minutes after the operation;
- `"tick"` `[minutes]` — `minutes` minutes passed (`minutes` ≥ 0, otherwise an error); paid time decreases but not below 0. The result is the paid minutes after the operation;
- `"remaining"` `[]` — the paid minutes;
- `"expired"` `[]` — `"true"` if there are no paid minutes, otherwise `"false"`;
- `"lost_minutes"` `[]` — how many minutes were lost to the cap so far.

All numeric results are strings. The first invalid operation makes the whole call an error: report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

Example: `["insert", "tick", "expired", "tick", "expired"]`, `[[50], [10], [], [20], []]` → `["30", "20", "false", "0", "true"]`.

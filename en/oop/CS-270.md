# Company employee hierarchy

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-270?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement the org chart of a company. Employees are non-negative integer numbers, there are none at first. Everyone except the single director has a direct manager. You get a list of operations `operations` and a list of their arguments `values` (`values[i]` belongs to `operations[i]`). Return a list of results, one per operation.

Operations:
- `"add"` with arguments `employee`, `manager`: hire an employee; `manager = -1` means the director. The result is `1` if hired. The result is `0` (and nothing changes) if the employee already exists, his number is negative, the manager is unknown, or a director already exists and another one is being added.
- `"subordinates"` with argument `employee`: result: how many subordinates the employee has in all, direct and through others (he does not count himself); `-1` if there is no such employee.
- `"depth"` with argument `employee`: result: how many managers are above him up to and including the director (`0` for the director); `-1` if there is no such employee.
- `"common_manager"` with arguments `a`, `b`: result: the number of the nearest employee who is `a` or a manager of `a` (direct or indirect) and at the same time is `b` or a manager of `b`; `-1` if `a` or `b` does not exist.

Example: `["add", "add", "add", "common_manager", "subordinates"]`, `[[1, -1], [2, 1], [3, 1], [2, 3], [1]]` → `[1, 1, 1, 1, 2]`.

# Bus at the stops

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-266?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement a bus with `capacity` seats (`capacity` ≥ 0), empty at first. You get a list of operations `operations` and a list of their arguments `values` (`values[i]` belongs to `operations[i]`). Return a list of results, one per operation.

Operations:
- `"board"` with argument `n`: `n` people at a stop want to get in; only as many as there are free seats will; result: how many people got in.
- `"alight"` with argument `n`: `n` people get out, but not more than are on the bus; result: how many people got out.
- `"free"` with no arguments: result: the number of free seats.
- `"total"` with no arguments: result: how many people have boarded the bus in all.

Example: `5`, `["board", "board", "alight", "free"]`, `[[3], [4], [10], []]` → `[3, 2, 5, 5]`.

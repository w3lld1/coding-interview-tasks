# Checkout with an express lane

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-512?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A shop has two lines for one till: express (for customers with at most 5 items) and regular. Both start empty. Operations:
- `join` (n): a customer with n items joins the back of the express line if n is at most 5, otherwise the back of the regular line; the result is the total number of waiting customers;
- `serve`: the cashier serves the next customer. Normally this is the first one in the express line, but if at least two express customers have been served in a row and the regular line is not empty, the first one in the regular line is served instead. If the express line is empty, the first one in the regular line is served. Serving a regular customer resets the count of express customers served in a row. The result is the item count of the served customer, or `0` if nobody is waiting;
- `waiting`: the result is the total number of waiting customers;
- `express_waiting`: the result is the number of customers waiting in the express line.

Return the list of results, one per operation. `values[i]` holds the arguments of operation `i` (an empty list if there are none).

Example: `["join", "join", "join", "join", "join", "waiting", "express_waiting", "serve", "serve", "serve", "serve", "serve", "serve"]`, `[[3], [12], [4], [2], [20], [], [], [], [], [], [], [], []]` → `[1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 5, 3, 3, 4, 12, 2, 20, 0]`.

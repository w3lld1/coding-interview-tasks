# Stack with access to the middle

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-522?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A stack of boxes holds non-negative integers and starts empty. The "middle" of a stack of n boxes is the box with index `n / 2` (integer division), counting from the bottom starting at 0. So in a stack of 1 box the middle is that box, and in a stack of 4 boxes it is the third from the bottom. Operations:
- `push` (x): put box x on top; the result is the stack size;
- `pop`: remove the top box; the result is its number, or `-1` if the stack is empty;
- `middle`: the result is the number of the middle box (it stays in place), or `-1` if the stack is empty;
- `pop_middle`: pull out the middle box, the others keep their order; the result is its number, or `-1` if the stack is empty.

Return the list of results, one per operation. `values[i]` holds the arguments of operation `i` (an empty list if there are none).

Example: `["push", "push", "push", "middle", "push", "middle", "pop_middle", "middle", "pop", "pop_middle", "pop_middle", "middle"]`, `[[10], [20], [30], [], [40], [], [], [], [], [], [], []]` → `[1, 2, 3, 20, 4, 30, 30, 20, 40, 20, 10, -1]`.

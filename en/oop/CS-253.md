# Towers of Hanoi move checker

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-253?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A game has three rods numbered `0`, `1`, `2` and four discs of sizes 1–4. At first all discs are on rod `0`: the largest (`4`) at the bottom and the smallest (`1`) on top; the other rods are empty. A disc may be placed only on an empty rod or on a larger disc. Implement the operations:
- `move` (from, to): move the top disc of one rod to another. The move is valid if the rods differ, the source has a disc, and the target is empty or holds a disc larger than the one moved. A valid move is made and gives `1`; an invalid one changes nothing and gives `0`;
- `top` (rod): the result is the size of the rod's top disc, or `0` if it is empty;
- `count` (rod): the result is the number of discs on the rod.

Return the list of results, one per operation. `values[i]` holds the arguments of operation `i`.

Example: `["move", "move", "move", "top", "count", "count"]`, `[[0, 1], [0, 1], [1, 2], [0], [1], [2]]` → `[1, 0, 1, 2, 0, 1]`.

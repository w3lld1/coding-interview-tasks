# Leaderboard with shared places

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-269?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement a leaderboard of a game. Players are integer numbers, the table is empty at first. A player counts with their **best** score over all submissions. You get a list of operations `operations` and a list of their arguments `values` (`values[i]` belongs to `operations[i]`). Return a list of results, one per operation.

Operations:
- `"submit"` with arguments `player`, `score`: take the score into account (a player's best score never decreases); result: the player's best score after that.
- `"rank"` with argument `player`: result: the player's place, that is `1` plus the number of players with a strictly higher best score (equal scores share a place, the next place is skipped); `-1` if the player is not in the table.
- `"player_at"` with argument `pos`: result: the number of the player at position `pos` (from `1`) in the order "best score descending, on a tie the smaller player number first"; `-1` if `pos` is outside `1..size`.
- `"size"` with no arguments: result: the number of players in the table.

Example: `["submit", "submit", "submit", "rank", "player_at"]`, `[[7, 50], [3, 80], [5, 50], [5], [2]]` → `[50, 80, 50, 2, 5]`.

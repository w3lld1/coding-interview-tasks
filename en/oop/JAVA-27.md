# Wizards and Warriors

**Difficulty:** medium · **Language:** Java · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/JAVA-27?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Create two subclasses of Fighter, Warrior and Wizard, each with methods for the description, the vulnerability check and the damage calculation. A warrior is never vulnerable and deals 10 damage to a vulnerable opponent or 6 to any other. A wizard is vulnerable until prepareSpell is called and deals 3 damage without preparation or 12 after it. The toString method must return "Fighter is a Warrior" or "Fighter is a Wizard" depending on the type of fighter.

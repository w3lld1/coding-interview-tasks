# Warehouse of batches with expiry dates

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-627?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement a warehouse that stores goods in batches with an expiry date. At first the warehouse is empty and the current day is `0`. A product is a number. A batch whose last good day is `e` can be sold up to and including day `e`; once the current day becomes greater than `e`, what is left of it is thrown away. You get a list of operations `operations` and a list of their arguments `values` (`values[i]` belongs to `operations[i]`). Return a list of results, one per operation.

Operations:
- `"receive"` with arguments `product`, `qty`, `expiry`: a batch of `qty` units arrives with last good day `expiry`. Result: how many units of this product are in stock.
- `"sell"` with arguments `product`, `qty`: sell `qty` units, taking them first from the batch with the earliest expiry, and among equal expiries from the one that arrived earlier. Result: how many batches this sale emptied completely.
- `"advance"` with argument `days`: `days` days pass; every batch (of any product) whose last good day is now below the current day is thrown away. Result: how many units were thrown away.
- `"stock"` with argument `product`: how many units of this product are in stock.
- `"next_expiry"` with argument `product`: the earliest last good day among the product's non-empty batches, `-1` if the product is out of stock.

Report an error (throw an exception, or return an error in Go) if `receive` has `qty` < 1 or `expiry` below the current day, if `sell` has `qty` < 1 or fewer than `qty` units of the product are in stock, or if `advance` has `days` < 0. The error applies to the whole call: the first bad operation aborts it. Up to 2000 operations.

Example: `["receive", "receive", "sell", "next_expiry", "advance"]`, `[[1, 5, 3], [1, 4, 1], [1, 6], [1], [2]]` → `[5, 9, 1, 3, 0]`.

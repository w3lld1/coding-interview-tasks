# Triage waiting room

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Classes and OOP](../oop/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-252?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Patients wait for a doctor in a waiting room. Urgent patients go before ordinary ones, but urgent patients keep their arrival order among themselves, and so do ordinary ones. A patient is a positive number, and ids in the queue are distinct. At first the queue is empty. Implement the operations:
- `arrive` (id): an ordinary patient arrives; the result is the number of waiting patients;
- `arrive_urgent` (id): an urgent patient arrives; they stand behind all urgent patients but before all ordinary ones. The result is the number of waiting patients;
- `next`: the doctor sees the first patient of the overall queue; the result is their id, or `-1` if nobody waits;
- `position` (id): the result is the patient's place in the overall queue (the first one is at place `1`), or `0` if they are not there.

Return the list of results, one per operation. `values[i]` holds the arguments of operation `i`.

Example: `["arrive", "arrive", "arrive_urgent", "arrive_urgent", "position", "next", "next", "position"]`, `[[1], [2], [3], [4], [2], [], [], [1]]` → `[1, 2, 3, 4, 4, 3, 4, 1]`.

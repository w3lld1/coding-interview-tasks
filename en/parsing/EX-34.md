# Math word problem

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Strings](../strings/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-34?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Evaluate a math question written in English. A question looks like `What is <expression>?`: after the words `What is` come whole numbers (possibly negative) and operations separated by single spaces, then `?`. Operations: `plus` (addition), `minus` (subtraction), `multiplied by` (multiplication) and `divided by` (division; it is always exact and never by zero). Evaluate strictly left to right, ignoring the usual precedence: `3 plus 2 multiplied by 3` is `15`. A single number with no operations is its own value. Report an error (throw an exception, or return an error in Go) if the question does not start with `What is` or does not end with `?`, if it contains an unknown operation (such as `cubed`), or if the order is broken: no expression, a missing operand, two operations or two numbers in a row, or any form other than number, operation, number, and so on.

Example: `"What is 5 plus 13 plus 6?"` → `24`.

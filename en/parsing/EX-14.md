# Mini Markdown parser

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-14?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Convert text in a simplified Markdown to HTML. Lines are separated by the `\n` character; every line is a separate block and the input has no empty lines.

- A line of 1 to 6 `#` characters, a space and text is a header: `# Text` → `<h1>Text</h1>`, … `###### Text` → `<h6>Text</h6>`. Seven or more `#`, or no space after them, make an ordinary paragraph.
- A line starting with `* ` is a list item `<li>…</li>`; consecutive items share one `<ul>…</ul>`, and the list ends at the first line that is not an item.
- Any other line is a paragraph `<p>…</p>`.

Inside the text of headers, list items and paragraphs, a pair `__text__` becomes `<strong>text</strong>`, then a remaining pair `_text_` becomes `<em>text</em>`. All other characters, including lone `#` and `*`, stay as they are. Blocks are joined with no separators.

Example: `"# Header!\n* __Bold Item__\n* _Italic Item_"` → `"<h1>Header!</h1><ul><li><strong>Bold Item</strong></li><li><em>Italic Item</em></li></ul>"`.

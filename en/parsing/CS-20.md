# Calculator without parentheses

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-20?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a string with an arithmetic expression made of non-negative integers, the operators `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` and spaces (spaces may appear anywhere between tokens and at the ends). There are no parentheses and no unary minus. Evaluate it by the usual rules: `*` and `/` go before `+` and `-`, and operators of equal priority go left to right. `/` is integer division that rounds toward zero. The expression is valid, there is no division by zero, and every intermediate value fits in a 32-bit integer.

Example: `" 3+5 / 2 "` → `5`.

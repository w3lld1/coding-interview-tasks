# Recognize digits from a drawing

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-73?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a drawing made of text lines: each digit is drawn in a cell 3 characters wide and 4 lines high using the characters `|`, `_` and space. The fourth line of a cell is always blank and is not examined. All lines of the drawing have the same length. Cells of one "line" go left to right, lines go top to bottom, 4 text lines each.

Return the string of digits. If there are several lines, their results are joined with a comma. A cell that does not look like any digit gives `"?"`. If the number of text lines is not a multiple of 4 or the line length is not a multiple of 3, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

The digits (top three lines of a cell): `0` is ` _ `/`| |`/`|_|`, `1` is `   `/`  |`/`  |`, `2` is ` _ `/` _|`/`|_ `, `3` is ` _ `/` _|`/` _|`, `4` is `   `/`|_|`/`  |`, `5` is ` _ `/`|_ `/` _|`, `6` is ` _ `/`|_ `/`|_|`, `7` is ` _ `/`  |`/`  |`, `8` is ` _ `/`|_|`/`|_|`, `9` is ` _ `/`|_|`/` _|`.

Example: `[" _ ", "| |", "|_|", "   "]` → `"0"`.

# Weight of a bracket string

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Parsing and validation](../parsing/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-122?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a balanced bracket string `s` of `(` and `)` characters, at most 60 long. Its weight is defined as follows: the pair `()` weighs `1`; if `A` and `B` are balanced strings, the weight of `AB` is the weight of `A` plus the weight of `B`; the weight of `(A)` is twice the weight of `A`. Return the weight of `s`. For an empty string the answer is `0`.

Example: `"(()(()))"` → `6`; `"()()"` → `2`.

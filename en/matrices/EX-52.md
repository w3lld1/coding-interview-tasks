# Flower field

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-52?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a rectangular garden: a list of strings of equal length where each cell is either a space (empty) or `*` (a flower). Return a new garden: flowers stay as they are, and every empty cell becomes the number of flowers among its neighbours (horizontally, vertically and diagonally, up to 8 cells) written as a digit. If a cell has no flower neighbours it stays a space. An empty list and a list holding one empty string are returned unchanged.

Example: `[" * ", "   "]` → `["1*1", "111"]`.

# Shortest bridge between islands

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-108?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a grid of characters `0` (water) and `1` (land): each string of the list is a row. An island is a group of `1` cells connected by sides (up, down, left, right). The grid contains exactly two islands. Return the fewest water cells that must be turned into land to join the two islands into one. The grid is at most 100×100.

Example: `["0100", "0000", "0000", "0010"]` → `3` (the cells `(1, 1)`, `(2, 1)` and `(2, 2)` are enough).

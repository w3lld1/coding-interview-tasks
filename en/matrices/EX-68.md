# Row or column of a matrix from text

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-68?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A matrix is written as a string: matrix rows are separated by the newline character `\n`, numbers within a row by spaces. You get a mode `kind` (`"row"` or `"column"`), such a string and an `index` counted from 1. Return the list of numbers of that row (left to right) or that column (top to bottom). Numbers can have several digits and can be negative. The index always exists in the matrix.

Example: `"column"`, `"9 8 7\n5 3 2\n6 6 7"`, `1` → `[9, 5, 6]`.

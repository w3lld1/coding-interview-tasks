# How far the robot gets

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-418?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A maze is a list of equal-length strings `grid`: `.` is a free cell, `#` is a wall. You may step to the neighbouring cell up, down, left or right, only onto free cells. The maze is at most 50×50 and has at least one row.

A robot stands in the top-left cell. Return how many cells (counting the starting one) it can visit. If the starting cell is a wall, the answer is `0`.

Example: `[".#.", "#.#", ".#."]` → `1` (all neighbours are walls).

# Warehouse picking with a small cart

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-675?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A warehouse map is given as a list of strings `grid` of equal length: `#` is a shelf, `.` is an aisle, `S` is the packing station (exactly one), `*` is an item to collect (at most 8 items). A picker starts at `S` with an empty cart. Each step moves to a neighbouring cell up, down, left or right that is inside the map and is not a shelf; cells with items and the station can be walked through.

Standing on an item's cell, the picker may put the item into the cart if it holds fewer than `capacity` items (`capacity ≥ 1`); walking over an item without taking it is allowed. On the station the cart is emptied. Return the smallest number of steps after which every item has been brought to the station (the picker ends at `S` with an empty cart). Return `0` if there are no items and `-1` if some item cannot be reached from the station. The map is at most 20 × 20.

Example: `["*..#*", ".#...", "S..*."]`, `capacity = 2` → `16` (one trip of 4 steps to the top-left item and one trip of 12 steps for the other two).

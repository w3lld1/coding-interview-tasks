# Diagonal sums

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-509?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a square table of integers `matrix` of size `n × n` (`n ≥ 1`). Return a list of two numbers: the sum of the main diagonal (from the top-left corner to the bottom-right one) and the sum of the anti-diagonal (from the top-right corner to the bottom-left one). The centre cell of a table of odd size lies on both diagonals and counts in both sums.

Example: `[[1, 2, 3], [4, 5, 6], [7, 8, 9]]` → `[15, 15]`; `[[1, 2], [3, 4]]` → `[5, 5]`.

# Conway's Game of Life

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/PY-26?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a rectangular matrix of zeros and ones, compute one next generation of the cellular automaton. A live cell stays alive with two or three live neighbours, and a dead cell comes alive only with three; in all other cases the result is zero. Neighbours are the eight surrounding positions inside the matrix, without wrapping opposite edges. Compute all changes simultaneously from the original state.

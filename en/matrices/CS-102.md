# Water reaching both oceans

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-102?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a rectangular height map: `heights[r][c]` is the height of a cell. Water moves from a cell to a side-adjacent cell if the neighbour's height is not greater than the current one (equal is allowed), and continues the same way. The top-left ocean touches the first row and the first column, the bottom-right ocean touches the last row and the last column. Water reaches an ocean if there is a chain of such moves to any cell of its shore (a shore cell itself counts). Return every cell `[row, column]` from which water reaches both oceans, ordered by row, then by column. An empty map gives `[]`. The map is at most 150×150.

Example: `[[1, 2, 3], [8, 9, 4], [7, 6, 5]]` → `[[0, 2], [1, 0], [1, 1], [1, 2], [2, 0], [2, 1], [2, 2]]`.

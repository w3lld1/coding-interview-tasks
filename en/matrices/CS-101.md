# Capture surrounded regions

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-101?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a rectangular grid of letters `X` and `O`: each string of the list is one row. A region is a group of `O` cells connected by sides (up, down, left, right). A region is surrounded if none of its cells lies on the border of the grid (first or last row, first or last column). Return a new grid where every cell of a surrounded region is replaced by `X` and everything else stays as it was. An empty grid gives an empty list. The grid is at most 200×200.

Example: `["XXXX", "XOOX", "XXOX", "XOXX"]` → `["XXXX", "XXXX", "XXXX", "XOXX"]` (the last `O` is on the border).

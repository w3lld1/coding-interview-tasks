# Hot spots on a heat map

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-488?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A heat map is a non-empty rectangular list of lists of integers `heat`. A cell is a hot spot if its value is strictly greater than the values of all existing neighbours on the left, right, above and below (diagonals do not count). A cell on the edge has fewer neighbours; the only cell of a 1×1 map is a hot spot. Return the `[row, column]` coordinates of all hot spots in row-major order: by row first, then by column.

Example: `[[1, 3, 1], [2, 2, 4]]` → `[[0, 1], [1, 2]]`.

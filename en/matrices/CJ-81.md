# Square dance elimination

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-81?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Every cell of a rectangular floor holds a dancer; the matrix `skills` gives their skill levels (all at least 1). The competition runs in rounds. A dancer's **compass neighbour** is the nearest dancer still in the competition in the same row or column in one of the four directions (up, down, left, right), so a dancer has 0 to 4 of them.

After each round, all dancers who have at least one compass neighbour and whose skill is strictly less than the average skill of their neighbours are eliminated at once. Neighbours are taken from that round's line-up: a dancer being eliminated still counts for the others. A dancer with no neighbours is never eliminated. If no one is eliminated after a round, the competition ends.

The interest of a round is the sum of the skills of every dancer in it (including those eliminated right after it). Return the total interest of all rounds. The floor may hold a few thousand cells and there may be many rounds, so rescanning the whole floor every round is too slow.

Example: `[[1, 1, 1], [1, 2, 1], [1, 1, 1]]` → `16` (10 in the first round, then the four 1s on the sides leave, 6 in the second).

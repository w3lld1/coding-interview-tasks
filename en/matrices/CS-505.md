# Checkerboard colouring

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-505?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a non-empty table of characters `grid`, a list of equal-length strings. It is coloured like a chessboard if any two cells that share a side hold different characters. Return `true` if so, otherwise `false`. A table of one cell qualifies. Any characters are allowed, not necessarily just two across the whole table.

Example: `["#.#", ".#."]` → `true`; `["ab", "bb"]` → `false`.

# Lonely guests

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-499?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A hall plan is a non-empty list of equal-length strings `seats`: `#` is a taken seat, `.` is a free one. A guest is lonely if none of the eight cells around their seat (sides and diagonals) holds a taken seat. Return the number of lonely guests. There are no cells beyond the edge of the plan.

Example: `["#.#", ".#."]` → `0` (the middle guest touches both others diagonally), `["#..", "..#"]` → `2`.

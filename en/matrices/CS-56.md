# Next generation of Life

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-56?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A board of Conway's Game of Life is given as a list of equal-length strings: `#` is a live cell and `.` is a dead one. The neighbours of a cell are up to eight cells around it (horizontal, vertical and diagonal). The board is bounded: beyond its edge there are no cells, and they count as dead. All cells change at the same time by these rules. A live cell with two or three live neighbours stays alive, otherwise it dies. A dead cell with exactly three live neighbours comes alive. Return the next generation in the same format. An empty board gives an empty list. The board has at most 30 × 30 cells.

Example: `["...", "###", "..."]` → `[".#.", ".#.", ".#."]`.

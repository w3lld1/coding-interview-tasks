# Dead ends in a maze

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-419?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A maze is a list of equal-length strings `grid`: `.` is a free cell, `#` is a wall. You may step to the neighbouring cell up, down, left or right, only onto free cells. The maze is at most 50×50 and has at least one row.

A dead end is a free cell with exactly one free neighbour (in four directions). A cell with no free neighbours is not a dead end, it is isolated.

Return the number of dead ends.

Example: `["..", "##"]` → `2` (both cells are dead ends).

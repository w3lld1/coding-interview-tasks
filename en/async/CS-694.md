# Progress bar with stage weights

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-694?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

An installer runs the stages `stages` at the same time and shows a progress bar. A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw). Stage `i` has the weight `weights[i]` (an integer, at least 1); a stage that fails also counts as done.

Start all the stages at once. The progress in percent is `100 * (sum of the weights of the done stages) / (sum of all weights)`, rounded down. The bar is updated once for every moment at which at least one stage ended; stages that ended at the same moment give one update. Return the list of updates `[moment, percent]` in time order, moments in milliseconds from the call of the function. For empty lists return `[]`. The current time in milliseconds: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python; in the tests the clock is virtual and waits take no real time. Up to 50 stages, the lists have equal length.

Example: stages that take 10 and 20 ms, weights `[3, 1]` → `[[10, 75], [20, 100]]`.

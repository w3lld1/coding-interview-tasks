# Finish order of jobs with dependencies

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-217?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a list of jobs `jobs` and a list `deps` of the same length: `deps[i]` holds the indexes of the jobs that job `i` depends on. A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw).

Job `i` starts (once) at the moment all jobs in `deps[i]` have ended successfully; a job with no dependencies starts at once. If any dependency failed or never ran, job `i` never runs. Any number of jobs may run at once. The dependencies have no cycles, and the indexes in `deps[i]` are distinct and lie in `0..n-1`.

Return the indexes of the jobs that ended successfully, in order of the time they ended; on equal times, the lower index first. For an empty list return `[]`. Up to 50 jobs. The current time in milliseconds: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python.

Example: jobs that take 10, 20 and 5 ms, `deps = [[], [], [0, 1]]` → `[0, 1, 2]` (job 2 starts at 20 ms and ends at 25 ms).

# Retries with growing pauses and an attempt limit

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-210?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a job `job`, a number of attempts `attempts` (at least 1), a base pause `delay` and an attempt limit `timeout` (both in milliseconds). A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw). Call the job until it returns a value, but at most `attempts` times; each call is a new attempt.

An attempt fails if the job throws or does not finish within `timeout` ms of the attempt's start: drop such an attempt without waiting for its end. After failed attempt number k (counting from 0), wait `delay · 2^k` ms before the next one; do not wait after the last attempt. Return the list `[value, time]`: the value of the successful attempt (or `-1` if every attempt failed) and how many milliseconds passed from the call of the function to the answer. The current time in milliseconds: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python. The job's values are non-negative; in the tests no attempt finishes exactly `timeout` ms after its start.

Example: a job fails twice after 5 ms and returns `7` after 5 ms on the third call; `attempts = 4`, `delay = 10`, `timeout = 100` → `[7, 45]` (5 + 10 + 5 + 20 + 5).

# Adaptive concurrency limit with retries

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-673?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw). Each call is a try.

Run the jobs `jobs` through a pool whose limit adapts, as in TCP: the limit starts at 1. A queue first holds all job indexes in increasing order. Whenever fewer tries are running than the limit and the queue is not empty, start a try of the job at the head of the queue (removing it). When a try ends at moment `t`:
- success: the job's answer is its value, and the limit becomes `min(max_limit, limit + 1)`;
- failure: the limit becomes `max(1, limit // 2)` (integer division); if the job has made fewer than `attempts` tries, it waits `delay · 2^(k−1)` ms, where `k` is the number of its tries so far, and then joins the end of the queue; otherwise its answer is `-1`.

A smaller limit stops no running try. Within one moment: first handle all tries that end then, in increasing job index order; then the jobs whose wait is over join the queue in increasing index order; then start tries while the limit allows.

Return a list of `n + 1` numbers: the answers of the jobs in index order, then the moment (in ms from the call) when the last try ended (`0` if there were none). Every try takes at least 1 ms; `max_limit`, `attempts` and `delay` are at least 1; up to 30 jobs. The current time in ms: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python.

Example: four jobs of 10 ms with values `1, 2, 3, 4`, the second fails its first try; `max_limit = 3`, `attempts = 2`, `delay = 5` → `[1, 2, 3, 4, 35]` (at 20 ms the failure of job 1 halves the limit to 1 and the success of job 2 raises it to 2; job 1 is queued again at 25 ms).

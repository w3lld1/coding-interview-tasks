# How many heartbeats are sent

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-581?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

While a long job is running, a client sends the server a «still alive» signal every `interval` milliseconds: the first one `interval` ms after the job started, the second at `2 * interval` and so on. As soon as the job ends (with success or with an error), the signals stop. A job is an async function with no arguments (`await job()`) that returns a number or fails (throws).

Run the job and at the same time start a repeating timer. Return how many signals were sent while the job was running. The duration of the job is never a positive multiple of `interval`, so there are no disputable coincidences. `interval ≥ 1`.

Example: the job lasts 105 ms, `interval = 20` → `5` (signals at 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100 ms).

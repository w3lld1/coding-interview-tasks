# Kettle or toaster: which is ready first

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Functions and closures](../functions/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-568?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

In the morning you switch on a kettle and a toaster. Each is an async function with no arguments (`await kettle()`, `await toaster()`) that returns a number (you may ignore it) and never fails. Switch both on at once and wait for both.

Return `"kettle"` if the kettle finished strictly earlier than the toaster, `"toaster"` if the toaster finished strictly earlier, and `"tie"` if they finished at the same millisecond.

Example: kettle 180 ms, toaster 120 ms → `"toaster"`.

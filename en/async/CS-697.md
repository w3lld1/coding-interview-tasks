# Parking lot that turns cars away

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-697?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A parking lot has `spaces` spaces. Car `i` arrives at moment `arrivals[i]` (milliseconds from the call of the function, non-decreasing). If there is a free space, it parks and stays while the job `stays[i]` runs, then leaves (a failed job is a departure too). A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw). If there is no free space, the car drives away at once without waiting, and its job is not called.

Cars that arrive at the same moment are handled in increasing index order. In the tests no car arrives exactly when another one leaves. Return the indexes of the cars that parked, in increasing order. The current time in milliseconds: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python; in the tests the clock is virtual and waits take no real time. Up to 50 cars, `spaces ≥ 1`.

Example: cars at 0, 5 and 20 ms, each stays 10 ms, one space → `[0, 2]`.

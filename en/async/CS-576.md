# Staggered start of jobs

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-576?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

To avoid overloading a server, jobs are started with a growing delay: job number `i` (from zero) starts `i * gap` milliseconds after the call of the function, not at once. A job is an async function with no arguments (`await job()`) that returns a number or fails (throws); a job that failed counts as ended too.

Jobs do not wait for each other: a started job keeps working while the next ones start. Return how many milliseconds passed from the call to the end of all jobs. For an empty list return `0`. Up to 50 jobs.

Example: jobs of 50, 20 and 10 ms, `gap = 10` → `50` (they end at moments 50, 30 and 30).

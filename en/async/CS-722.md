# Resilience policy chain around requests

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Functions and closures](../functions/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-722?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw). Request `i` arrives at moment `arrivals[i]` (ms from the start) and calls its job `jobs[i]` through the chain of policies `policies`, listed from the outside in: the first policy wraps the second, …, the last one wraps the job call. Every call of a job is a new try.

Policies:
- `"timeout:MS"`: if the wrapped call has not ended within `MS` ms of the start of this call, the policy fails with the error `timeout`; the abandoned call keeps running (and keeps the places it holds), its result is ignored;
- `"retry:N:PAUSE"`: when the wrapped call fails, wait `PAUSE` ms and call again, at most `N` repeats; after the last failure pass its error on;
- `"fallback:V"`: on a failure return the number `V`;
- `"bulkhead:K"`: a limiter shared by all requests: at most `K` wrapped calls through this policy at once (each such policy in the list has its own limiter); the others wait in a queue in order of coming; a place is freed when the wrapped call has ended (with a value or an error) and goes at once to the first waiting call.

Return for each request `"V@t"` (the value and the moment the request ended) or `"err:MSG@t"` (`MSG` is the error text). For an empty list the answer is `[]`. The current time in ms: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python. Up to 20 requests, up to 6 policies, `MS, PAUSE, K ≥ 1`. In the tests no two of these moments coincide: request arrivals, ends of job calls, `timeout` firings, ends of `retry` pauses; and no call ends exactly at the moment of its own timeout.

Example: `arrivals = [0, 1, 2]`, jobs (10 ms → `1`), (10 ms, the first call fails, then `2`), (10 ms → error `"down"`), `policies = ["fallback:-1", "retry:2:5", "timeout:15"]` → `["1@10", "2@26", "-1@42"]`.

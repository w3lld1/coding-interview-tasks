# When the service is marked down

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-693?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A monitor watches a service by its «still alive» signals. The signals come as the jobs of the list `beats`: start them all at once; a job that succeeds at moment `t` is a signal at moment `t`, and a failed job is a lost signal and does not count. A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw).

The service is marked down as soon as `timeout` ms pass with no signal: counted from the call of the function (moment `0`) while there has been no signal yet, or from the last signal. A signal that comes exactly at that moment is already late. Return the moment (in milliseconds from the call of the function) when the service was marked down; after the last signal this always happens. The current time in milliseconds: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python; in the tests the clock is virtual and waits take no real time. Up to 50 jobs, `timeout ≥ 1`.

Example: signals at 30, 90 and 150 ms, `timeout = 100` → `250`; signals at 30 and 140 ms → `130`.

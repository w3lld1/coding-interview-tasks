# Auction with extensions

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-702?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

An online auction closes at moment `close`, but a bid in the last seconds extends it. The bids come as the jobs of the list `bids`: start them all at once; a job that succeeds at moment `t` with the value `v` is a bid `v` (an integer, at least 1) at moment `t`, and a failed job is a bid that did not happen. A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw).

The bids are handled in arrival order (those arriving at the same moment in increasing index order). A bid is accepted only if it came strictly before the current closing moment; a bid at the closing moment or later is not accepted. If after an accepted bid less than `window` ms are left until closing, the auction is extended: the new closing moment is `t + window`. Return the list `[largest accepted bid, final closing moment]`; with no accepted bids the largest is `0`. Moments are in milliseconds from the call of the function. The current time in milliseconds: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python; in the tests the clock is virtual and waits take no real time. Up to 50 bids, `close ≥ 0`, `window ≥ 1`.

Example: bids of 10 at 50 ms, 15 at 90 ms and 20 at 105 ms, `close = 100`, `window = 20` → `[20, 125]`.

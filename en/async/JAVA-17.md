# Bank Account

**Difficulty:** hard · **Language:** Java · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/), [Classes and OOP](../oop/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/JAVA-17?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement an account with opening, closing, reading the balance, depositing and withdrawing funds; on opening, the balance is zero, including after reopening a closed account. Forbid reopening an account that is already open, closing an unopened account, operations on an unopened or closed account, negative amounts, and withdrawing more than the balance. Concurrent deposits and withdrawals from different threads must not lose updates or violate the constraints.

# Typing indicator periods

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-692?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A messenger shows the other person a «typing…» indicator. Key presses come as the jobs of the list `keys`: start them all at once; a job that succeeds at moment `t` means a key press at moment `t` (its value does not matter), and a failed job is a lost event and is not a press. A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw).

The indicator turns on at a press and goes off `idle` ms after the last press: a press at moment `t` moves the switch-off to `t + idle`. A press exactly at the switch-off moment no longer extends it: the indicator has gone off and turns on again. Return the list of display periods `[on, off]` in time order, moments in milliseconds from the call of the function. If there are no presses, return `[]`. The current time in milliseconds: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python; in the tests the clock is virtual and waits take no real time. Up to 50 jobs, `idle ≥ 1`.

Example: presses at 10, 30 and 70 ms, `idle = 50` → `[[10, 120]]`; presses at 10 and 60 ms → `[[10, 60], [60, 110]]`.

# Sum with a limit for each job

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-551?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a list of jobs `jobs` and a limit `limit` in milliseconds. A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw).

Run the jobs one at a time, in list order. Each job gets at most `limit` ms: if it has not ended in that time, abandon it (do not wait further) and move on to the next. A job that fails before the limit is skipped too. Return a list of two numbers: the sum of the values of the jobs that ended successfully in time and the time in milliseconds from the call of the function to its end. The current time in milliseconds: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python. In the tests no job ends exactly `limit` ms after its start. For an empty list return `[0, 0]`.

Example: jobs of 10, 80 and 20 ms return `1`, `2`, `3`, `limit = 50` → `[4, 80]` (the second is abandoned after 50 ms of its run: 10 + 50 + 20).

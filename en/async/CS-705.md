# Messages that overtook earlier ones

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-705?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A chat sends messages on a timer: message `i` goes out at moment `i * gap` (milliseconds from the call of the function), without waiting for the earlier ones to be delivered. The delivery of a message is the job `messages[i]`, called at the moment of sending; a successful end is the delivery, and a failed job means a lost message. A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw).

Message `j` overtook earlier ones if it was delivered strictly before at least one delivered message with a smaller index. Messages delivered at the same moment do not overtake each other, and a lost message neither overtakes nor is overtaken. Return the indexes of the messages that overtook, in increasing order. The current time in milliseconds: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python; in the tests the clock is virtual and waits take no real time. Up to 50 messages, `gap ≥ 0`.

Example: deliveries take 30, 5 and 5 ms, `gap = 10` → `[1, 2]` (the messages are delivered at 30, 15 and 25 ms).

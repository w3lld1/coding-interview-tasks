# Saga with staged steps and compensations

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-674?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw).

An order is placed as a saga: step `i` runs the job `steps[i]` once, and the job `undos[i]` cancels its effect. The list `stages` (non-decreasing, same length) splits the steps into stages: neighbouring steps with equal numbers form one stage. Stages run one after another; the first starts at moment 0. A stage starts all its steps at once and waits until all of them end; if all succeeded, the next stage starts at that moment.

If at least one step of a stage failed, then, at the moment the last step of this stage ended, compensation begins: the stages done so far are undone from this one back to the first. In a stage, the undos of all its succeeded steps start at once; the previous stage is undone when every undo of this one is over (a stage with no succeeded steps takes no time). An undo calls `undos[i]`; after a failed call it waits `delay` ms and calls again, at most `attempts` calls in all. Steps that failed are not undone.

Return the log: `"do:i"` when step `i` succeeded, `"fail:i"` when it failed, `"undo:i"` when its undo succeeded, `"stuck:i"` when its last undo call failed; entries go by moment, and at one moment in increasing `i`. The last entry is `"ok:<sum of the step values>@<moment>"` if every stage succeeded (the moment the last step ended, `0` without steps), otherwise `"failed@<moment>"` (when the last undo ended, or the failed stage ended if there was nothing to undo). Every call takes at least 1 ms; `attempts`, `delay` ≥ 1; up to 30 steps. The current time in ms: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python.

Example: `steps` (10 ms → `5`), (20 ms → `7`), (15 ms → error); `stages = [0, 1, 1]`; `undos` of 4 ms, of 3 ms failing its first call, of 2 ms; `attempts = 2`, `delay = 5` → `["do:0", "fail:2", "do:1", "undo:1", "undo:0", "failed@45"]`.

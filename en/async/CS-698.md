# Batched log shipping

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-698?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A service collects log entries and ships them in batches. The entries come as the jobs of the list `entries`: start them all at once; a job that succeeds at moment `t` is an entry that arrived at moment `t`, and a failed job gives no entry. A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw).

The collected entries are shipped as one batch in two cases: as soon as there are `size` of them (at the arrival of the `size`-th entry), or `wait` ms after the arrival of the oldest entry not yet shipped; entries that arrive exactly at that moment go into the same batch. Entries that arrive at the same moment are handled one by one. Return the list of batches `[ship moment, number of entries]` in shipping order, moments in milliseconds from the call of the function. If there are no entries, return `[]`. The current time in milliseconds: `Date.now()` in JS/TS, `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python; in the tests the clock is virtual and waits take no real time. Up to 50 jobs, `size ≥ 1`, `wait ≥ 1`.

Example: entries at 10, 20, 30 and 40 ms, `size = 3`, `wait = 50` → `[[30, 3], [90, 1]]`.

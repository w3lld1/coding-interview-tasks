# Circuit breaker for calls

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, TypeScript · **Topics:** [Async and concurrency](../async/), [Functions and closures](../functions/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-223?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A job is an async function with no arguments: call it (`await job()`) to run it; it may fail (throw).

You are given a list of jobs `jobs`, the calls to an unreliable service, and integers `gap ≥ 0`, `threshold ≥ 1`, `cooldown ≥ 1`. Call `i` is planned for the moment `i · gap` ms after your function was called, but the calls run strictly one at a time: call `i` starts at `max(i · gap, end of call i − 1)` (the current time in milliseconds is `Date.now()` in JS/TS and `round(asyncio.get_running_loop().time() * 1000)` in Python). The calls go through a circuit breaker with two states.

- **Closed** (the initial state): the job runs. A success gives `"ok:<value>"` and resets the counter of consecutive failures; an error gives `"fail"` and increments the counter. When the counter reaches `threshold`, the breaker opens at the end of that call for `pause` ms, where `pause = cooldown`.
- **Open** until moment `u`: a call that starts before `u` returns `"open"` at once, without running its job and without taking any time. A call that starts at `u` or later is a trial: the job runs. A success gives `"ok:<value>"`, the breaker closes, the counter resets and `pause` is `cooldown` again. An error gives `"fail"`, `pause` doubles and the breaker opens again until "end of the trial call + `pause`".

Return the list of answers for all calls in order. For an empty list the answer is `[]`. Up to 50 calls, durations up to 10⁶ ms.

Example: jobs (5 ms → `1`), four failing jobs of 5 ms each, (5 ms → `7`); `gap = 10`, `threshold = 2`, `cooldown = 25` → `["ok:1", "fail", "fail", "open", "open", "ok:7"]` (it opens at 25 until 50, the calls at 30 and 40 are rejected, the call at 50 is a trial).

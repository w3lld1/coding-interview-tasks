# Split a restaurant bill

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Errors and exceptions](../errors/), [Math](../math/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-330?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A group splits a restaurant bill. The amount `total_cents` is given in cents (an integer) and the number of people is `people`. Split the amount so that nobody pays fractions of a cent, the shares differ by at most one cent, and together they pay exactly the whole bill.

Return a list of `people` shares. The remainder of the even division is handed out one cent at a time to the first people in the list: if the remainder is `r`, the first `r` shares are one cent bigger than the rest.

If there are fewer than one person or the bill is negative, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go. A zero bill is valid: everyone pays 0.

Example: `1000`, `3` → `[334, 333, 333]`; `10`, `4` → `[3, 3, 2, 2]`; `100`, `0` → error.

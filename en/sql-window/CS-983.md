# Coming less often: longer breaks in September than in August

**Difficulty:** hard · **Language:** SQL (SQLite) · **Topics:** [SQL: window functions](../sql-window/), [SQL: aggregation](../sql-aggregate/), [SQL: filtering](../sql-filter/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-983?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Call a break the time in days (with a fraction, by `julianday`) between the start of a member's booking and the start of their previous booking in the same month; a month's first booking has no break. Output the columns `memid`, `aug_gap` and `sep_gap` (the average break in August and in September 2012, rounded to two decimals) for the members (not the guest) whose average September break is greater than their August one. A member with fewer than two bookings in August or in September is left out. The row order does not matter.

The tables and data are already in the starter code and are created afresh on every run (SQLite):

- `facilities(facid, name, membercost, guestcost, initialoutlay, monthlymaintenance)` — club facilities; `membercost` and `guestcost` are the price of one slot for a member and for a guest.
- `members(memid, surname, firstname, address, zipcode, telephone, recommendedby, joindate)` — members; `recommendedby` is the `memid` of who invited them, or `NULL`; `memid = 0` is a guest.
- `bookings(bookid, facid, memid, starttime, slots)` — bookings; one slot is 30 minutes; `starttime` is text such as `2012-09-14 09:00:00`.

# Deviation from the album's average length

**Difficulty:** easy · **Language:** SQL (SQLite) · **Topics:** [SQL: window functions](../sql-window/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-778?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

For the tracks of the album Master Of Puppets (`AlbumId = 152`) output the columns `Name`, `Milliseconds` and `diff_seconds`, how many seconds longer the track is than the average track of this album (negative when shorter), rounded to one decimal. Row order does not matter.

The Chinook music store's tables and data are already in the starter code and are created afresh on every run (SQLite):

- `Artist(ArtistId, Name)` — artists; `Album(AlbumId, Title, ArtistId)` — albums.
- `Track(TrackId, Name, AlbumId, MediaTypeId, GenreId, Composer, Milliseconds, UnitPrice)` — tracks; `Composer` is the author or `NULL`, `Milliseconds` the length, `UnitPrice` the price.
- `Genre(GenreId, Name)` — genres; `MediaType(MediaTypeId, Name)` — file formats.
- `Employee(EmployeeId, LastName, FirstName, Title, ReportsTo, HireDate, City, Country)` — employees; `ReportsTo` is the manager's `EmployeeId`, or `NULL`.
- `Customer(CustomerId, FirstName, LastName, Company, City, State, Country, Email, SupportRepId)` — customers; `State` is the state or province, or `NULL`; `SupportRepId` is the `EmployeeId` of their support rep.
- `Invoice(InvoiceId, CustomerId, InvoiceDate, BillingCity, BillingCountry, Total)` — invoices; `InvoiceDate` is text such as `2021-01-02`, `Total` the sum of its lines.
- `InvoiceLine(InvoiceLineId, InvoiceId, TrackId, UnitPrice, Quantity)` — invoice lines: the tracks bought.
- `Playlist(PlaylistId, Name)` and `PlaylistTrack(PlaylistId, TrackId)` — playlists and their tracks.

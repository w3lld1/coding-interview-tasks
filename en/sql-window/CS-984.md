# A month's share of a facility's summer revenue

**Difficulty:** medium · **Language:** SQL (SQLite) · **Topics:** [SQL: window functions](../sql-window/), [SQL: aggregation](../sql-aggregate/), [SQL: joins](../sql-join/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-984?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A booking's revenue is `slots` times `guestcost` for a guest (`memid = 0`) and times `membercost` for a member. For each facility and each month from July to September 2012 in which the facility had bookings, output the columns `facility` (the facility's name), `month` (such as `2012-07`), `revenue` (the month's revenue) and `percent`, the percentage of the facility's summer revenue (July to September) that fell on that month, rounded to one decimal. The row order does not matter.

The tables and data are already in the starter code and are created afresh on every run (SQLite):

- `facilities(facid, name, membercost, guestcost, initialoutlay, monthlymaintenance)` — club facilities; `membercost` and `guestcost` are the price of one slot for a member and for a guest.
- `members(memid, surname, firstname, address, zipcode, telephone, recommendedby, joindate)` — members; `recommendedby` is the `memid` of who invited them, or `NULL`; `memid = 0` is a guest.
- `bookings(bookid, facid, memid, starttime, slots)` — bookings; one slot is 30 minutes; `starttime` is text such as `2012-09-14 09:00:00`.

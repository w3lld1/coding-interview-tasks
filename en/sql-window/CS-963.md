# Slots over the last two visiting days

**Difficulty:** medium · **Language:** SQL (SQLite) · **Topics:** [SQL: window functions](../sql-window/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-963?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Call a visiting day a date (`date(starttime)`) on which a member has bookings. For each booking of the members with `memid` 1 and 2 output the columns `bookid`, `memid` and `two_days`, the sum of the slots of all the same member's bookings on this booking's day and on their previous visiting day (for the first day, only that day). The row order does not matter.

The tables and data are already in the starter code and are created afresh on every run (SQLite):

- `facilities(facid, name, membercost, guestcost, initialoutlay, monthlymaintenance)` — club facilities; `membercost` and `guestcost` are the price of one slot for a member and for a guest.
- `members(memid, surname, firstname, address, zipcode, telephone, recommendedby, joindate)` — members; `recommendedby` is the `memid` of who invited them, or `NULL`; `memid = 0` is a guest.
- `bookings(bookid, facid, memid, starttime, slots)` — bookings; one slot is 30 minutes; `starttime` is text such as `2012-09-14 09:00:00`.

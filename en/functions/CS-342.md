# Repeat a step n times

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Functions and closures](../functions/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-342?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a step name `name`, a non-negative integer `x` and a repeat count `n`. Apply the step to `x`, then to the result, and so on `n` times in a row; return the final value. For `n = 0` the answer is `x`.

Steps (on the current value `v`):
- `"collatz"`: if `v` is even, `v / 2`, otherwise `3 * v + 1`.
- `"digit_sum"`: the sum of the decimal digits of `v`.
- `"reverse_digits"`: the number written with the digits of `v` in reverse order, without leading zeros (`120` → `21`).
- `"square_mod"`: the remainder of `v * v` divided by `1000`.

`x` is at most 100000, `n` is at most 50, and the intermediate values fit in a 32-bit integer.

Example: `"collatz"`, `6`, `3` → `5` (6 → 3 → 10 → 5).

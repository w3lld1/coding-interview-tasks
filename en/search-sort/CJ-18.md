# Barber queue

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-18?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A barber shop has barbers numbered from 1; barber `k` cuts one customer's hair in exactly `barber_minutes[k − 1]` minutes. A barber who finishes is free at once. The customer at the head of the queue goes to the free barber with the lowest number; if nobody is free, the customer waits until someone is (if several become free at once, the lowest number is chosen). The shop has just opened and every barber is free. You stand in place `place` in the queue (the first customer is place 1). Return the number of the barber who will cut your hair.

Limits: `place` up to 10^9, durations up to 10^5.

Example: `[10, 5]`, `4` → `1` (barber 1 is busy until minute 10, barber 2 frees up at minute 5 and takes the third customer, and at minute 10 both are free).

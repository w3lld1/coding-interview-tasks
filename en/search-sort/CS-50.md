# Minimum eating speed

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-50?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

There are several piles of bananas: `piles[i]` is the size of the `i`-th pile (a positive number up to 10^9; from 1 to 10000 piles). You have `hours` hours, and `hours` is at least the number of piles. You pick an integer speed `v ≥ 1`: in one hour you eat at most `v` bananas from a single pile (if it has fewer than `v`, the hour is still fully spent, and you cannot switch to another pile within it). Return the smallest speed `v` at which all piles are eaten within `hours` hours or fewer.

Example: `[3, 6, 7, 11]`, `8` → `4`.

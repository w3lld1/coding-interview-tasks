# City skyline key points

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-93?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Buildings stand on one line; each is a list `[left, right, height]`: a rectangle from `left` to `right` horizontally (`0 ≤ left < right ≤ 2³¹ - 1`) with height `height` (`1 ≤ height ≤ 2³¹ - 1`). There are up to 10000 buildings, in any order, and they may overlap. The skyline is the upper outline of their union. Describe it by key points `[x, h]`: at `x` the skyline's height becomes `h` and stays so until the next point. Return the points in ascending `x`; two neighbouring points have different heights (no redundant points), and the last point has height `0`. An empty list of buildings gives `[]`.

Example: `[[2, 9, 10], [3, 7, 15], [5, 12, 12], [15, 20, 10], [19, 24, 8]]` → `[[2, 10], [3, 15], [7, 12], [12, 0], [15, 10], [20, 8], [24, 0]]`.

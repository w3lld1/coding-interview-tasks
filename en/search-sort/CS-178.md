# Fewest refuelling stops

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-178?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A car drives along a line from point `0` to point `target`, using one unit of fuel per unit of distance. The tank has no limit and starts with `start_fuel` units. The list `stations` holds stations `[position, fuel]`; positions strictly increase and lie strictly between `0` and `target`. When the car stops at a station it takes all of its fuel; it may also drive past. Reaching a station or the target with an empty tank is allowed.

Return the fewest station stops that let the car reach `target`, or `-1` if it cannot. If the starting fuel is enough, the answer is `0`. Limits: `1 ≤ target, start_fuel ≤ 10^9`, up to 500 stations, each with `1` to `10^9` fuel; the fuel sum may not fit in 32 bits.

Example: `100`, `10`, `[[10, 60], [20, 30], [30, 30], [60, 40]]` → `2`.

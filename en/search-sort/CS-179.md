# Most capital after k projects

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-179?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You have capital `w`. Project `i` can be started only when the current capital is at least `capital[i]`; nothing is spent, and when it finishes the project adds its net profit `profits[i]` to the capital. Projects run one at a time, each at most once. Choose at most `k` projects and their order so that the final capital is as large as possible, and return it.

If no project is affordable, the answer is `w`; `k` may exceed the number of projects. Limits: up to 10 000 projects, `0 ≤ k ≤ 10^5`, `0 ≤ w, profits[i], capital[i] ≤ 10^9`; the answer may not fit in 32 bits.

Example: `2`, `0`, `[1, 2, 3]`, `[0, 1, 1]` → `4` (project 0 first, then project 2).

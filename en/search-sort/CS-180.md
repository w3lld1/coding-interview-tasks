# Sliding window median

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-180?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of integers `nums` and a window size `k` with `1 ≤ k ≤` the list's length, a window of `k` consecutive elements slides along the list one position at a time from left to right. Return the medians of all `n − k + 1` windows in order.

The median of a window is the middle element after sorting when `k` is odd, and the mean of the two middle elements when `k` is even; the answers are floating-point numbers. Values are within 32-bit integers, so the sum of two may not fit in 32 bits. Limits: up to 10 000 elements; sorting every window from scratch is too slow.

Example: `[1, 3, -1, -3, 5, 3, 6, 7]`, `3` → `[1.0, -1.0, -1.0, 3.0, 5.0, 6.0]`.

# Shared birthday

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/), [Dates and time](../dates/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-39?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a list of birth dates in the format `"YYYY-MM-DD"`. Return `true` if two people have the same birthday, that is, the same month and day (the year may differ), and `false` otherwise. Identical full dates count as a match too. An empty list and a list of one date give `false`. Leap years are not special: `02-29` is just one more day.

Example: `["1966-07-29", "1977-02-12", "2001-07-29"]` → `true`.

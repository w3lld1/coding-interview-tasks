# Group the anagrams

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-39?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of words made of lowercase Latin letters (up to 1000 words, a word may be empty), split them into groups of anagrams — strings where one is a rearrangement of the letters of the other — and return the list of groups. Repeated words are kept as many times as they occur. The order is fixed: words inside a group are in alphabetical order, and the groups are in alphabetical order of their first words. An empty list gives an empty list.

Example: `["eat", "tea", "tan", "ate", "nat", "bat"]` → `[["ate", "eat", "tea"], ["bat"], ["nat", "tan"]]`.

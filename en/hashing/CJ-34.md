# Robot rock band

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-34?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are forming a band of four robots, one from each of four candidate lists `a`, `b`, `c` and `d` (all of the same length; every robot has a number, numbers may repeat in a list, and robots with equal numbers are different robots). A band is good if the bitwise XOR of its four numbers equals `k`.

Return the number of good bands, that is the number of position quadruples `(i, j, l, m)` with `a[i] ^ b[j] ^ c[l] ^ d[m] = k`.

Limits: each list has up to 1000 numbers, numbers and `k` are from 0 to 10^9. Trying all quadruples is too slow.

Example: `[0, 0]`, `[2, 0]`, `[0, 0]`, `[0, 1]`, `3` → `4` (you need the numbers 2 and 1 from the second and fourth lists, and either zero from the first and the third).

# Cutting a matrix into cells

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-101?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a rectangular matrix of positive integers. It must be cut into single 1 × 1 cells. Each cut picks one of the current pieces (a rectangular submatrix with more than one cell) and splits it in two with a straight line along the border between two adjacent rows or two adjacent columns. Before each cut you receive as many coins as the minimum value in the piece being cut. Pieces are then cut independently of each other, and you choose the order of the cuts.

Return the largest total number of coins you can collect while cutting the matrix down to single cells. A one-cell matrix gives `0`. The matrix has 1 to 12 rows and 1 to 12 columns, with values from 1 to 100,000.

Example: `[[1, 2], [3, 4]]` → `5` (a horizontal cut first for 1 coin, then the pieces `[1, 2]` and `[3, 4]` give 1 and 3).

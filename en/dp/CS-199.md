# Spelling a word on a rotating ring

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-199?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given strings `ring` and `key` of lowercase Latin letters. The letters of `ring` are written in order around a ring; at the start the letter at index 0 is under the pointer. In one step you can turn the ring by one letter clockwise or anticlockwise (the first letter follows the last one), or press the button, which types the letter under the pointer. You must type the letters of `key` strictly in order: a press counts only when the next needed letter is under the pointer. Return the fewest steps (turns and presses together) needed to type the whole word. `ring` has 1 to 100 letters, `key` has 0 to 100, and every letter of `key` occurs in `ring`; an empty `key` gives `0`.

Example: `"abcde"`, `"ea"` → `4` (turn back one step to `e`, press, turn to `a`, press).

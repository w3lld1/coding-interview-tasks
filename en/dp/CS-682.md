# Trading in a coin at the exchange

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-682?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

At an exchange, a coin of value `k` can either be sold for exactly `k` dollars or traded for three coins of values `k / 2`, `k / 3` and `k / 4` (integer division, rounding down). The new coins can again be sold or traded, any number of times. A coin of value 0 is worth nothing. Return the largest amount of money you can get for a coin `n`. `n` is from 0 to 10^9; the answer may not fit in 32 bits.

Example: `24` → `27` (24 → 12, 8, 6; the 12 is worth trading for 6, 4, 3 and the rest are sold: 6 + 4 + 3 + 8 + 6).

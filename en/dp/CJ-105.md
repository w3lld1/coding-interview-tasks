# Stones that lose energy

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-105?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a list of stones, each a triple `[s, e, l]`: the stone takes `s` seconds to eat, holds `e` units of energy at time 0 and loses `l` units per second until its energy reaches zero (it never goes below zero). Stones are eaten one at a time, starting at time 0 with no pauses between them; any stones may be skipped. A stone started at time `t` immediately gives all its remaining energy `max(0, e - l·t)`.

Return the largest total energy you can get. There are 1 to 50 stones, `1 ≤ s ≤ 50`, `1 ≤ e ≤ 100,000`, `0 ≤ l ≤ 100,000`.

Example: `[[20, 10, 1], [5, 30, 5], [100, 30, 1], [5, 80, 60]]` → `105` (order: the fourth, second, third stone).

# Fewest transfers to settle debts

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-202?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A group of friends wrote down their debts: each record `[a, b, x]` means that person `a` owes person `b` the amount `x`. People are numbered `0` to `11`, a record has `a ≠ b` and `1 ≤ x ≤ 1000`, and there are at most 50 records. One transfer moves any whole amount from one person to another. Return the smallest number of transfers after which everyone's net balance (what they are owed minus what they owe) is zero. If there are no debts or they all cancel out, the answer is `0`.

Example: `[[0, 1, 10], [1, 2, 10]]` → `1` (person `0` sends `10` to person `2`).

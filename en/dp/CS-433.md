# Scored stepping stones

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-433?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A row of stones crosses a creek, each marked with a number of points (possibly negative): the list `scores`. You stand on the first stone and must reach the last. One jump moves you forward by any number of stones from 1 to `k`. The points of every stone you stand on are added to your total, including the first and the last. Return the largest possible total. The list has at least one stone, `k` is at least 1, and there are at most 1000 stones.

The best total on stone `i` is its own points plus the best total among the stones you can jump from.

Example: `[1, -5, 4]`, `2` → `5` (jump straight from the first stone to the third: 1 + 4).

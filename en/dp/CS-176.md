# Cheapest way to cut a stick

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-176?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A stick of length `length` is marked from `0` to `length`. The list `cuts` gives the positions where it must be cut; the cuts are made one at a time, in any order. Each cut costs the length of the piece it splits at that moment. Return the smallest possible total cost of all cuts.

A position may appear in the list more than once: a repeated cut at the same place costs nothing, since the stick is already split there. An empty list gives `0`. Limits: `2 ≤ length ≤ 10^6`, up to 100 positions, each strictly between `0` and `length`.

Example: `7`, `[1, 3, 4, 5]` → `16` (the order 3, 5, 1, 4 costs 7 + 4 + 3 + 2).

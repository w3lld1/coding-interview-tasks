# Cutting chess boards

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-53?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a rectangular sheet of black and white cells: a list of equal-length strings of `0` and `1` (up to 60 × 60). A chess board is a square piece of the sheet with sides along the sheet's sides in which every two cells sharing a side have different colours; a 1 × 1 square is a board too.

Boards are cut out one by one until no cells are left. Each time, the largest board among the remaining cells (made only of cells not cut yet) is cut; among boards of equal size, the one whose top row is higher, and on a tie the one whose left column is further left. Return a list of pairs `[size, count]`: how many boards of each size are cut, by decreasing size, listing only sizes that occur.

Example: `["0011", "0011", "1100", "1100"]` → `[[2, 1], [1, 12]]`.

# Playlists with follow rules

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-444?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A library has `tracks` tracks numbered 0 to `tracks - 1`. The playlist rules say which track may directly follow which: each pair `[a, b]` in the list `allowed` means track `b` may be placed right after track `a` (the reverse `b` after `a` is allowed only if the pair `[b, a]` is present; the pair `[a, a]` allows playing a track twice in a row). Pairs are not repeated. Return the number of playlists of exactly `length` tracks in which every neighbouring pair is allowed. The first track may be any. Playlists in a different order are different. Limits: `tracks` from 1 to 5, `length` from 1 to 12.

Look at the last track: the number of playlists ending in `b` is the sum, over every `a` that may be followed by `b`, of the numbers of one-shorter playlists ending in `a`.

Example: `3`, `[[0, 1], [0, 2], [1, 2]]`, `3` → `1` (the only playlist is 0, 1, 2).

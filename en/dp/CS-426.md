# Staircase with broken steps

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-426?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A stairwell has `n` steps, numbered from 1 at the bottom to `n` at the top; the floor below the first step counts as step 0. One move goes up 1, 2 or 3 steps. Some steps are broken (their numbers are in the list `broken`): you cannot stand on them, but you may jump over them. Return the number of different sequences of moves that take you from the floor to step `n`. If `n` is 0 the answer is 1 (no moves needed); if step `n` itself is broken the answer is 0.

The value for step `i` follows naturally from the last move: add the ways to reach `i - 1`, `i - 2` and `i - 3`. Limits: `n` is at most 30.

Example: `3`, `[]` → `4` (1+1+1, 1+2, 2+1, 3).

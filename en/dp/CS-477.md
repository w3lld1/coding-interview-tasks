# Cheapest laundry in batches

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-477?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A laundromat has `n` loads of laundry in a row. Load `i` can be washed alone for `single[i]`, and two neighbouring loads `j` and `j + 1` can be washed together for `pair[j]`. Every load is washed exactly once and loads are never reordered. Return the smallest total price. `single` has `n` numbers and `pair` has `n - 1` numbers (for `n` = 0 and `n` = 1 `pair` is empty); all numbers are integers, at least 0, and `n` is at most 1000.

Example: `single = [5, 4, 6]`, `pair = [7, 8]` → `13` (the first two loads together for 7, the third alone for 6).

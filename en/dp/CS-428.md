# Toll posts on a bridge

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-428?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A road crosses a river along a row of toll posts; the list `tolls` gives the fee of each post in order. You stand on the bank before the first post and want to reach the bank after the last one. One jump moves you forward 1 or 2 positions: you can step on the next post or skip one. You pay the fee of every post you stand on; skipped posts are free. Both banks cost zero. Return the smallest total fee. If there are no posts, the answer is 0.

The cheapest way to reach a post depends only on the two positions before it. Limits: up to 1000 posts, each fee from 0 to 1000.

Example: `[10, 15, 20]` → `15` (jump to the second post, then to the far bank).

# Swimming pool passes

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-434?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You plan to visit a pool on the days in the list `days` (day-of-year numbers in strictly increasing order, from 1 to 365). Passes come in three kinds with their prices in the list `costs` of three numbers: `costs[0]` for 1 day, `costs[1]` for 7 consecutive days, `costs[2]` for 30 consecutive days. A pass bought for day `d` covers days `d` through `d + 6` (weekly) or through `d + 29` (monthly); you can buy as many as you want, on any day, and passes may overlap. Return the smallest total price that covers every day in `days`. For an empty list the answer is 0.

Look at the first uncovered day and which pass to start from it.

Example: `[1, 4, 6, 7, 8, 20]`, `[2, 7, 15]` → `11` (a weekly pass for days 1-7, single passes for 8 and 20: 7 + 2 + 2).

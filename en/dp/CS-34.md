# House robber on a circle

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-34?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Houses stand in a circle, house `i` holds `nums[i]` coins (a non-negative integer; at most 1000 houses). Adjacent houses, including the first and the last, cannot both be taken: the alarm goes off. Return the largest total you can collect. An empty list gives `0`, and a single house gives its value.

Example: `[2, 3, 2]` → `3` (the first and last houses are neighbours, so only the middle one is taken).

# Split into two equal-sum parts

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-36?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of positive integers (at most 100 elements, each at most 100), can all its elements be distributed into two parts with equal sums? Both parts must be non-empty, and order does not matter. An empty list and a one-element list give `false`.

Example: `[1, 5, 11, 5]` → `true` (`11` and `1 + 5 + 5`).

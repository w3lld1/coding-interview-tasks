# Smooth sequences

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-449?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A sequence is smooth if all its elements are integers from 1 to `max_value` and neighbouring elements differ by at most 1 (they are equal or differ by exactly one). Return the number of smooth sequences of length `length`. Limits: `length` from 1 to 12, `max_value` from 1 to 6.

Look at the last element: after a value `v` may come `v - 1`, `v` or `v + 1` (where they lie between 1 and `max_value`).

Example: `2`, `3` → `7` (11, 12, 21, 22, 23, 32, 33).

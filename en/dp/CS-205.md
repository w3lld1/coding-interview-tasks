# Job schedule with the least difficulty

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Stacks, queues, linked lists](../stacks-lists/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-205?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list `jobs` of job difficulties in the order they must be done, and a number of days `days`. The jobs are split over the days in order: each day takes the next contiguous piece of the list, and every day gets at least one job. A day's difficulty is the largest difficulty among its jobs, and the schedule's difficulty is the sum over all days. Return the smallest possible schedule difficulty. If there are fewer jobs than days (an empty list included), return `-1`.

Limits: up to 2000 jobs, each difficulty from 0 to 1000, `1 ≤ days ≤ 10`. An O(n²·days) solution may run out of time on the largest test; aim for O(n·days).

Example: `[6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1]`, `2` → `7` (day one `[6, 5, 4, 3, 2]` with difficulty 6, day two `[1]` with difficulty 1).

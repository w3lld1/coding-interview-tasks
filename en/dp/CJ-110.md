# Digit product divisible by digit sum

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-110?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Call a positive integer interesting if the product of its digits is divisible by the sum of its digits. For example, `451` is interesting (`4·5·1 = 20` is divisible by `4 + 5 + 1 = 10`), and any number with a digit `0` is interesting because its product is `0`. Given `a` and `b`, return how many interesting integers lie between `a` and `b` inclusive.

Limits: `1 ≤ a ≤ b ≤ 10^8`; checking every number in the range is too slow.

Example: `451`, `460` → `5` (451, 453, 456, 459 and 460).

# Climb without back-to-back leaps

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-443?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A staircase has `n` steps. One move goes up 1 step (a step) or 2 steps (a leap), but two leaps in a row are forbidden: there must be at least one step between leaps. You must land exactly on step `n`. Return the number of different sequences of moves. For `n` = 0 there is one sequence, the empty one. Limits: `n` from 0 to 40.

When counting it matters how the sequence ended: with a step (then both a step and a leap may follow) or with a leap (then only a step).

Example: `4` → `4` (1+1+1+1, 1+1+2, 1+2+1, 2+1+1; the sequence 2+2 is forbidden).

# Packing a batch in boxes by levels

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-685?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A warehouse packs a batch of `n` identical parts by levels. On the first level the parts are split into equal groups of `d1 > 1`, on the second the groups go into boxes of `d2 > 1` groups, and so on until one big box is left: `d1 · d2 · … · dk = n`. A packing plan is the ordered list of factors `[d1, d2, …, dk]`, each greater than 1; plans with the same factors in a different order are different. Return the number of different plans. For `n = 1` there is one plan, the empty one (nothing to pack). `n` is from 1 to 10^6.

Example: `12` → `8` (`[12]`, `[2, 6]`, `[6, 2]`, `[3, 4]`, `[4, 3]`, `[2, 2, 3]`, `[2, 3, 2]`, `[3, 2, 2]`).

# Stock trading with a cooldown

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-144?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of a stock's prices by day (up to 5000 days, prices from 0 to 10000). Each day you may do one action: buy one share, sell it, or do nothing. You can hold at most one share, so you may buy again only after selling. The day after a sale is a mandatory cooldown: you cannot buy on it. You may trade any number of times. Return the maximum profit; for an empty list, or when no profit is possible, return `0`.

Example: `[1, 2, 3, 0, 2]` → `3` (buy at 1, sell at 2, cooldown, buy at 0, sell at 2).

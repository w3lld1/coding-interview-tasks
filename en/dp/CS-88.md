# Ways to strike out letters down to a word

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-88?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given strings `s` and `t` of lowercase Latin letters (each of length 0 to 50), you may strike out any characters of `s` so that the remaining ones, in order, spell `t`. Return the number of different ways to do it: two ways differ when the sets of positions of `s` that remain differ. If `t` is empty there is exactly one way (strike out everything), so the answer is `1`; if `t` is longer than `s` or cannot be obtained at all, the answer is `0`. The answer fits in a 64-bit integer.

Example: `"abbcbc"`, `"abc"` → `5`.

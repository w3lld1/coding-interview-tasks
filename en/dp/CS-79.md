# Popping balloons for the most coins

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-79?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Balloons stand in a row, each with a non-negative number on it: the list `nums`. You pop them one at a time in any order until all are gone. Popping a balloon with number `x` earns `l · x · r` coins, where `l` and `r` are the numbers on the nearest balloons still intact to its left and right; if there is no such neighbour (the balloon is at the edge of those left), use `1` instead. The popped balloon then disappears and its neighbours become adjacent.

Return the largest number of coins you can earn. An empty list gives `0`. There are up to 100 balloons with numbers from 0 to 100.

Example: `[3, 1, 5, 8]` → `167` (order 1, 5, 3, 8: 3·1·5 + 3·5·8 + 1·3·8 + 1·8·1 = 15 + 120 + 24 + 8).

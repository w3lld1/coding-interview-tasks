# Shifts with two rest days

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-476?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A courier sees the earnings `pay[i]` (an integer, at least 0) for every day. If they work on some day, they must rest the next two days, so any two chosen working days differ by at least 3 in index. Any days may be chosen. Return the largest total earnings; for an empty list return `0`. There are at most 1000 days.

Example: `[4, 2, 3, 6, 1, 5]` → `10` (the days earning `4` and `6`).

# Codejamon cipher

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Strings](../strings/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-36?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A monster has a vocabulary `vocabulary` of distinct words (lowercase Latin letters). A sentence is a sequence of vocabulary words; a word may repeat. The monster enciphers a sentence by randomly shuffling the letters of each word and removing the spaces.

For every string in `ciphers`, find how many different original sentences (sequences of words) could have produced it. Return the list of answers in the same order, each modulo 1000000007. If no sentence fits, the answer is `0`. Words with the same letters (such as `"ab"` and `"ba"`) are different words.

Limits: up to 400 vocabulary words of length up to 20, up to 5 ciphers of length up to 4000.

Example: vocabulary `["this", "is", "a", "monster", "retsnom"]`, cipher `"ishtsiarestmon"` → `4` (`is this a monster`, `is this a retsnom`, `this is a monster`, `this is a retsnom`).

# Two-lane runner

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-445?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A runner covers `n` segments, each with two parallel lanes: `lane_a[i]` and `lane_b[i]` are the points for running segment `i` on the first and the second lane (points may be negative). The lists have equal length. The runner picks a lane on the first segment and, when moving on to the next segment, may stay in the lane or hop to the other one, paying `penalty` points. Return the largest total (segment points minus penalties). For empty lists the answer is 0. Limits: up to 1000 segments, `penalty` from 0 to 100.

The best total on segment `i` in each lane comes from the best totals of the two lanes on segment `i - 1`.

Example: `[4, 1, 4]`, `[1, 5, 1]`, `0` → `13` (4 on the first lane, 5 on the second, 4 on the first, free hops).

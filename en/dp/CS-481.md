# Stairs with a mandatory landing

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-481?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You climb a staircase from step 0 to step `n`, taking 1 or 2 steps at a time. You must stand on the landing, the step numbered `landing` (from 0 to `n`; steps 0 and `n` are always visited). Return the number of ways to climb, where ways differ by their sequence of moves. `n` is from 1 to 40.

Example: `n = 4`, `landing = 2` → `4` (two ways to reach step 2, two ways from it to step 4).

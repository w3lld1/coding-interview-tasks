# Heap of stones game

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-448?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Two players take turns removing stones from a single heap that starts with `stones` stones. On a turn a player removes exactly `m` stones, where `m` is one of the numbers in the list `moves` (positive and distinct), and cannot remove more stones than remain. The first player moves first. A player who cannot move (fewer stones remain than the smallest number in `moves`, or none) loses. Both play perfectly. Return `true` if the first player wins, otherwise `false`. Limits: `stones` from 0 to 1000, up to 5 numbers in `moves`.

A position with `s` stones is winning if some move leads to a losing position; otherwise it is losing. This is a recursion over the number of stones, easily memoised.

Example: `3`, `[1, 2]` → `false` (any move leaves the opponent 2 or 1 stones, and they take them all).

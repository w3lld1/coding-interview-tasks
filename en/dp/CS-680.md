# Longest shared stretch of two playlists

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-680?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Two playlists are lists of song numbers `first` and `second`. Find the longest stretch, that is, a run of consecutive songs that appears in both playlists in full and in the same order. Return its length; if the lists share no song or one of them is empty, return `0`. Songs may repeat within a playlist. Each list has 0 to 500 numbers from 1 to 10^6.

Example: `[1, 2, 3, 2, 1]`, `[3, 2, 1, 4, 7]` → `3` (the stretch `3, 2, 1`).

# Best streak of days with one day forgiven

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-687?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A list `profit` holds a kiosk's profit per day; numbers may be negative. A streak is a run of consecutive days. You may drop at most one day from a streak (any day, the first or last one too), but at least one day must stay in it. Return the largest sum of the remaining days over all streaks. The list has 1 to 10,000 numbers, each from -10,000 to 10,000.

Example: `[1, -2, 0, 3]` → `4` (the streak `1, -2, 0, 3` without the day `-2`).

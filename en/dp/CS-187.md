# Least starting health through the dungeon

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-187?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A dungeon is a rectangular table `grid` of numbers (at least 1 × 1): a negative number in a room takes health away, a positive one adds health, `0` changes nothing. The hero starts in the top-left room, moves only right or down and must reach the bottom-right room; the number of every room on the way, the first and the last included, is applied on entering it. If the health ever drops to `0` or below, the hero dies. Return the smallest whole starting health (at least `1`) with which some path can be walked without dying. The table is up to 200 × 200, numbers are from −1000 to 1000.

Example: `[[-2, -3, 3], [-5, -10, 1], [10, 30, -5]]` → `7` (path right, right, down, down).

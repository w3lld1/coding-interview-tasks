# Largest square of ones

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-35?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a matrix of `'0'` and `'1'` characters as a list of equal-length strings (up to 100×100), find the largest square made only of `'1'` and return the length of its side. If there are no ones or the matrix is empty (an empty list), the answer is `0`.

Example: `["10100", "10111", "11111", "10010"]` → `2`.

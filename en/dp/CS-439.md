# Split a number for the best product

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-439?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given an integer `n` of at least 2. Split it into a sum of at least two positive integers (order does not matter, parts may repeat) so that the product of the parts is as large as possible, and return that product. Limits: `n` from 2 to 45, the answer fits a 32-bit integer.

Cut off the first part `first`: the remainder `n - first` is either kept as a single part or split further in the best way.

Example: `10` → `36` (3 * 3 * 4).

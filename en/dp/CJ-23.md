# Controlled inflation

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-23?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

An automatic pump inflates an item to a given pressure in pascals. The pump has two buttons, up and down, each changing the target pressure by exactly 1. Customers stand in a line, each with at least one item; `pressures[i]` holds the target pressures (1 to 10^9) of the items of the `i`-th customer. The customers cannot be reordered: all items of one customer must be inflated before any item of the next, but within a customer any order is allowed. If two items inflated one after another have the same pressure, no buttons are needed.

The pump is initially set to 0, and it may be left at any pressure after the last item. Return the minimum number of button presses.

Example: `[[30, 10, 40], [20, 50, 60], [60, 60, 50]]` → `110`.

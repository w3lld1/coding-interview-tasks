# Selling a ribbon in pieces

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-432?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A shop has a ribbon `n` metres long, where `n` is the length of the list `prices`. A piece of whole length `k` metres sells for `prices[k - 1]`; prices are non-negative and need not grow with length. The ribbon may be cut into any number of whole-metre pieces or sold whole, with no leftovers. Return the largest total revenue. For an empty list the answer is 0. Limits: up to 40 metres.

Think about which piece to cut first: what remains is a shorter ribbon for which the same question is already solved.

Example: `[1, 5, 8, 9]` → `10` (two pieces of 2 metres: 5 + 5).

# Build a number with plus one and double

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-437?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A calculator shows 1. It has two buttons: "+1" (add one) and "x2" (double). Return the number of different sequences of presses after which the screen shows exactly `n`. Sequences that differ in at least one press count as different, even if they pass through the same intermediate numbers (for `n` = 2, "+1" and "x2" are two different sequences). For `n` = 1 there is one sequence, the empty one. Limits: `n` from 1 to 1000.

Look at the last press: after "+1" the screen showed `n - 1`, after "x2" it showed `n / 2`, which is possible only for even `n`.

Example: `4` → `4` ("+1 +1 +1", "+1 x2", "x2 +1 +1", "x2 x2").

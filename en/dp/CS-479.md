# Cheapest three-colour fence painting

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-479?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A fence is a row of boards. Each board is painted in one of three colours `0`, `1`, `2`, and neighbouring boards must have different colours. `costs[i][c]` is the price of painting board `i` in colour `c` (an integer, at least 0, exactly 3 numbers per row). Return the smallest total price for the whole fence; for an empty list return `0`. There are at most 1000 boards.

Example: `[[1, 5, 3], [2, 9, 4]]` → `5` (colours `0` and `2`: `1 + 4`).

# A courier with jobs of different length

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-676?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A courier is offered jobs one after another, numbered `0` to `n - 1`. Job `i` pays `pay[i]`, but if the courier takes it, they miss the next `rest[i]` jobs (numbers `i + 1` to `i + rest[i]`) because they are still on the road. If the courier passes on a job, they can decide on the next one right away. If `rest[i]` reaches past the end of the list, the courier simply takes nothing after job `i`. Return the largest possible total pay. Both lists have the same length, 0 to 1000 items; `pay[i]` is 0 to 10,000 and `rest[i]` is 0 to 1000. For empty lists the answer is `0`.

Example: `pay = [3, 4, 5]`, `rest = [1, 0, 0]` → `9` (jobs 1 and 2; taking job 0 loses job 1 and gives only 8).

# Longest word chain

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-142?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of non-empty words of lowercase Latin letters (up to 1000 words, each up to 16 letters; words may repeat), a chain is a sequence of distinct words from the list in which each next word is the previous one with exactly one letter inserted anywhere (the other letters keep their order). A single word is a chain of length 1. Return the greatest chain length; for an empty list return `0`.

Example: `["a", "b", "ba", "bca", "bda", "bdca"]` → `4` (`b` → `ba` → `bca` → `bdca`).

# Planks on a shelf

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-427?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You must fill a shelf of length `length` with planks, with no gaps and no overlap. Planks come only in the lengths listed in `planks` (positive and distinct), with an unlimited supply of each. Planks are laid from left to right and order matters: the fillings `[1, 2]` and `[2, 1]` are different. Return the number of different fillings. For `length` = 0 there is exactly one filling (the empty one); if the shelf cannot be filled, return 0.

Think about the last plank: if its length is `p`, the rest fills a shelf of length `length - p`. Limits: `length` is at most 30, `planks` has at most 4 lengths.

Example: `4`, `[1, 2]` → `5` (1+1+1+1, 1+1+2, 1+2+1, 2+1+1, 2+2).

# Sightseeing Trip

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-39?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You start in city 1 at time 0 and travel through cities 2, …, N in order, with one bus between each pair of neighbours. The bus from city i to city i+1 is a triple `[S, F, D]`: it leaves at times S, S+F, S+2F, … and the ride takes D time units. Boarding and leaving take no time, and you may wait for a bus as long as you like.

In each of the cities 1..N−1 (not the last) you may spend `sight_time` sightseeing before waiting for the bus, or go straight to waiting; you cannot sightsee twice in one city. You must reach city N no later than `deadline`.

The parameters are `sight_time`, `deadline` and a list `buses` of N−1 triples (N from 2 to 2000, values up to 10^9). Return the largest number of cities you can sightsee in, or `-1` if reaching city N in time is impossible.

Example: `3`, `12`, `[[3, 2, 1], [6, 2, 2], [1, 3, 2]]` → `2`.

# Drone over the rooftops

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-435?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A drone flies over a row of rooftops whose heights are given by the list `heights`. It starts on the first roof and must land on the last. One flight takes it to the next roof or over one roof to the one after. A flight from roof `i` to roof `j` costs `|heights[i] - heights[j]|` units of energy. Return the smallest total energy. For an empty list and for a single roof the answer is 0. Limits: up to 1000 roofs, heights from 0 to 1000.

The drone reaches roof `i` from `i - 1` or `i - 2`, so the answer for `i` follows from the two before it.

Example: `[10, 30, 40, 20]` → `30` (10 → 30 costs 20, then 30 → 20 costs 10; going via 40 costs more).

# Delete and earn

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-146?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of positive integers (up to 1000 elements, values up to 10 000). In one move you pick any element, earn points equal to its value, and remove that element together with every element equal to its value minus 1 or plus 1. Other copies of the chosen value stay and may be taken in later moves. Return the greatest number of points you can earn. For an empty list the answer is `0`.

Example: `[3, 4, 2]` → `6` (take `4`: `3` and `5` are removed, `2` stays; then take `2`: 4 + 2 = 6).

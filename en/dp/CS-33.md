# Cheapest right-and-down route

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-33?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a rectangular grid of non-negative integers (from 1×1 to 100×100), travel from the top-left cell to the bottom-right one, moving only right or down. The cost of a route is the sum of the numbers in all visited cells, including the first and the last. Return the smallest possible cost.

Example: `[[1, 3, 1], [1, 5, 1], [4, 2, 1]]` → `7` (route `1 → 3 → 1 → 1 → 1`).

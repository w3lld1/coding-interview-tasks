# Bacterial tactics

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-78?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a rectangular board as a list of equal-length strings (1 to 12 rows and columns) of `.` (an empty cell) and `#` (a radioactive cell). Two players move in turns. A player who faces a board with no empty cells loses. A move picks an empty cell and places an H or V colony there. An H colony occupies the cell and grows left and right, V grows up and down: in each direction it occupies consecutive empty cells until it reaches the board's edge or a cell already taken by bacteria. If the growth reaches a radioactive cell, the colony mutates and the player who moved loses at once.

Both play optimally. If the first player loses, return `0`. Otherwise return the number of their winning first moves; moves differ if the cell or the colony type differs.

Example: `["#.##", "....", "#.##"]` → `7`.

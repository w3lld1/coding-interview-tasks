# A street where cars park in columns

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-688?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

There are `n` parking spaces in a row along a street. Each space is either free or taken. The parking rule: taken spaces form columns, that is, groups of consecutive taken spaces, and every column has at least `k` spaces. Two columns are always separated by at least one free space (otherwise they are one column). Return the number of different parking layouts, ways to mark the spaces taken or free under this rule. A fully empty street is a layout too. `n` is 0 to 50 and `k` is 1 to 50; for `n = 0` the answer is `1`; the answer may not fit in 32 bits.

Example: `4`, `3` → `4` (`....`, `###.`, `.###`, `####`, where `#` is a taken space).

# Trades with a selling fee

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-145?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of daily stock prices and a fee `fee` (`fee ≥ 0`). Each day you may buy one share or sell one share, but you can hold at most one share at a time; buying and selling on the same day is allowed as long as you sell the old share first. Every sale costs `fee` (the fee is paid once per buy-and-sell trade). You may make any number of trades. Return the greatest possible profit; if no trade pays off or the list is empty, return `0`. A share still held at the end earns nothing: an unfinished purchase does not count.

Example: `[1, 3, 2, 8, 4, 9]`, `2` → `8` (buy at 1, sell at 8: 7 − 2 = 5; buy at 4, sell at 9: 5 − 2 = 3).

# Dance pairings

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-446?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

`n` distinguishable guests attend a dance evening. Each guest dances either alone or in a pair with exactly one other guest. Return the number of different ways to split the guests into singles and pairs. The order of pairs and the order inside a pair do not matter: the pair `{1, 2}` is the same as `{2, 1}`; the split where guest 1 is alone and guests 2 and 3 are together differs from the one where guest 1 is with guest 2. For `n` = 0 the answer is 1. Limits: `n` from 0 to 20, the answer fits a 64-bit integer.

Take the last guest: either they dance alone (the other `n - 1` split any way) or they pair with one of the other `n - 1` guests (the remaining `n - 2` split any way).

Example: `3` → `4` (everyone alone; or pair 1-2, 1-3 or 2-3 with one single).

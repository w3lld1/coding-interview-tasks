# Committee most likely to tie

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Math](../math/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-72?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A department has n members; `probs[i]` is the probability that member i votes yes (otherwise they vote no, nobody abstains, votes are independent). Choose exactly `k` members for a committee, where `k` is even and 2 ≤ `k` ≤ n ≤ 60. A tie means exactly `k/2` yes votes. Choose the committee that maximizes the probability of a tie and return that probability (compared within 1e-6).

Example: `[0.75, 1.0, 0.5]`, `2` → `0.5`.

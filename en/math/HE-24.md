# Largest proper divisor

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-24?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given an integer `n` that is at least `2`, find the largest integer smaller than `n` that divides `n` evenly. For a prime `n` the answer is `1`.

Example: `15` → `5`.

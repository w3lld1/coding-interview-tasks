# Sum of squares of rounded-up numbers

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-133?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of numbers, integers or fractions, round each one up to the nearest integer (the smallest integer that is not less than the number) and return the sum of the squares of the results. For negative numbers rounding up moves towards zero: `-2.4` becomes `-2`. An empty list gives `0`.

Example: `[1.4, 4.2, 0]` → `29` (2² + 5² + 0²).

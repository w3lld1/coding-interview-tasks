# Fractional part

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-2?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a non-negative floating-point number, it splits into an integer part (the largest whole number not above it) and a fractional part, which is always less than 1. Return the fractional part. For a whole number the result is `0`.

Example: `3.5` → `0.5`.

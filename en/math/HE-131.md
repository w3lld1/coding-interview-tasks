# Product of odd digits

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-131?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a positive integer `n`, multiply all of its odd digits together and return the result. If there are no odd digits, return `0`. Even digits (including `0`) are ignored.

Example: `235` → `15` (3 · 5).

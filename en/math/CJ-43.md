# No Nine

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-43?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A number is legal if it is a natural number, has no digit 9 in its decimal notation and is not divisible by 9. Players say legal numbers in turn: first `first`, then each time the next legal number, up to and including `last`. For example, with `first = 16` they say 16, 17, 20, 21, 22, …

Given two legal numbers `first` ≤ `last` (up to 9·10^15), return how many numbers were said, that is, how many legal numbers lie in the range from `first` to `last` inclusive. You cannot iterate over all numbers.

Example: `16`, `26` → `9` (16, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26).

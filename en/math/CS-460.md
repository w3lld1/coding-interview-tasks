# Last one in the circle

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-460?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

In a playground game, `n` kids stand in a circle, numbered 1 to `n` clockwise. Counting starts at kid 1: count `k` kids around the circle, starting with kid 1; the one on whom the count ends leaves, and the next kid starts a new count. This goes on until one kid is left. Return that kid's number. Assume `1 ≤ n ≤ 500` and `k ≥ 1`.

Example: `7`, `3` → `4` (3, 6, 2, 7, 5, 1 leave in that order).

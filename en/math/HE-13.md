# Greatest common divisor

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-13?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given two non-negative integers that are not both zero, return their greatest common divisor: the largest number that divides both. If one of the numbers is `0`, the answer is the other one.

Example: `25`, `15` → `5`.

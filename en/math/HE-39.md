# Prime Fibonacci numbers

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-39?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Fibonacci numbers: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, … (each one is the sum of the two before it). Some of them are prime: 2, 3, 5, 13, 89, … Given `n` from 1 to 10, return the `n`-th prime Fibonacci number in order (counting from one).

Example: `4` → `13`.

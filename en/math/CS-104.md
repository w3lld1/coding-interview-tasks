# Knight moves to a square

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-104?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A chess knight stands on `(0, 0)` of an infinite board. One move shifts it by `(±1, ±2)` or `(±2, ±1)`. Return the fewest moves needed to reach the square `(x, y)`. The coordinates may be negative; `|x|` and `|y|` are at most 100. For `(0, 0)` the answer is `0`.

Example: `2`, `1` → `1`; `1`, `1` → `2`.

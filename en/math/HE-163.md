# Even digits between two numbers

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-163?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get two positive integers `a` and `b`, where `a` may be larger than `b`. Return, in ascending order, all even digits (the single-digit numbers 2, 4, 6, 8) that lie between them, borders included. If there are none, return an empty list. Multi-digit numbers are not part of the answer, even if they are even.

Example: `10`, `2` → `[2, 4, 6, 8]`; `10`, `14` → `[]`.

# Leap year check

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Dates and time](../dates/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-64?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Return `true` if the year is a leap year in the Gregorian calendar, otherwise `false`. A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except for years divisible by 100 that are not divisible by 400. The year is a positive integer.

Example: `1996` → `true`, `1900` → `false`.

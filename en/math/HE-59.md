# Largest prime factor

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-59?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given an integer `n > 1`, return its largest prime factor. If `n` is itself prime, the answer is `n`.

Example: `13195` → `29`, because 13195 = 5 · 7 · 13 · 29.

# Difference of squares

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-48?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get an operation `operation` and an integer `number` (0 to 100). Work with the first `number` natural numbers `1, 2, …, number` (for 0 there are none and all sums are 0).

- `"square_of_sum"`: the square of the sum of these numbers, for 5 it is `(1+2+3+4+5)² = 225`.
- `"sum_of_squares"`: the sum of their squares, for 5 it is `1+4+9+16+25 = 55`.
- `"difference"`: the first minus the second, for 5 it is `225 − 55 = 170`.

Example: `"difference"`, `10` → `2640` (3025 − 385).

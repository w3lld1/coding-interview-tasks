# Is it a right triangle

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-157?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get three positive integers, the side lengths. Return `true` if segments of these lengths form a right-angled triangle, otherwise `false`. The sides can come in any order, and any of them can be the hypotenuse.

Example: `3`, `4`, `5` → `true`; `1`, `2`, `3` → `false`.

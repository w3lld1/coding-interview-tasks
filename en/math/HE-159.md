# Hungry rabbit and carrots

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-159?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A rabbit has already eaten `eaten` carrots and needs to eat `need` more to be full. The stock holds `remaining` carrots. It eats as many as it needs, and if the stock is too small it eats the whole stock and stays hungry. Return a list of two numbers: how many carrots have been eaten in total and how many are left in the stock. All numbers are non-negative.

Example: `5`, `6`, `10` → `[11, 4]`; `2`, `11`, `5` → `[7, 0]`.

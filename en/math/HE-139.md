# Brazilian factorial

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-139?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

The Brazilian factorial of `n` is the product of the factorials of all numbers from `n` down to 1: `n! · (n−1)! · … · 2! · 1!`. Given an integer `n` from 1 to 8, return its Brazilian factorial.

Example: `4` → `288` (24 · 6 · 2 · 1).

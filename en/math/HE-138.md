# Sum of four positive even numbers

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-138?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given an integer `n`, return `true` if it can be written as a sum of exactly four positive even numbers (the terms may repeat), otherwise `false`. Zero and negative numbers give `false`.

Example: `8` → `true` (2 + 2 + 2 + 2), `6` → `false`.

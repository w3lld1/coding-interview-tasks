# Even and odd palindromes

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-107?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get an integer `n` from 1 to 1000. Among the numbers from 1 to `n` inclusive, find those that read the same forwards and backwards (palindromes). Return a list of two numbers: how many of them are even and how many are odd, in that order.

Example: `12` → `[4, 6]` (palindromes 1–9 and 11: 4 even, 6 odd).

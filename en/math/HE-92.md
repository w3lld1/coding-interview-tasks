# One number is the sum of the other two

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-92?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given three numbers, return `true` if all of them are whole numbers (no fractional part; `3.0` counts as whole) and one of them equals the sum of the other two. Otherwise return `false`.

Example: `5`, `2`, `7` → `true` (5 + 2 = 7). Example: `3.6`, `-2.2`, `2` → `false`.

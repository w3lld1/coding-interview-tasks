# Counting with digit reversal

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-68?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You count aloud starting from 1. The next number is either the previous one plus 1, or the previous one with its digits reversed (leading zeros are dropped: after 2300 you may say 32). Return the smallest number of numbers you must say to reach `n`. The first 1 and `n` itself count, and a number said again counts each time. Limits: 1 ≤ n ≤ 10¹⁴.

Example: `23` → `15` (1, 2, …, 12, 21, 22, 23).

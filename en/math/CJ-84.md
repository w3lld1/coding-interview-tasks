# Next roaring year

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-84?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A year `y` is **roaring** if its decimal form (without leading zeros) is the concatenation of two or more consecutive positive integers in increasing order, each also without leading zeros. For example, `2021` = "20" + "21", and `12`, `789`, `910`, `9899100` are roaring too. But `2020` is not ("20", "20" are not consecutive), `778` is not ("7", "7", "8" are not distinct), and `102` is not ("1", "02" has a leading zero).

Given `year` (`1 ≤ year < 10^15`), return the smallest roaring year strictly greater than `year`. Checking the years one by one is far too slow.

Example: `68000` → `78910` (7, 8, 9, 10).

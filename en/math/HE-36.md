# Sevens in multiples of 11 or 13

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-36?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given an integer `n`, consider all integers from 0 to `n - 1` that are divisible by 11 or by 13. Return how many times the digit 7 appears in the decimal notation of these numbers in total (not per number). If there are no such numbers or `n` is not positive, the answer is 0.

Example: `78` → `2` (77 qualifies and has two sevens; 78 is not included).

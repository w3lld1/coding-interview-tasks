# Is the product whole

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-144?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given two strings holding fractions in the form `"numerator/denominator"`, where both parts are positive integers and the denominator is not zero, return `true` if the product of the two fractions is a whole number, otherwise `false`. The fractions may be reducible.

Example: `"1/5"`, `"5/1"` → `true`; `"1/6"`, `"2/1"` → `false`.

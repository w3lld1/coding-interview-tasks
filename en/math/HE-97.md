# Product of last digits

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-97?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given two integers, possibly negative, return the product of their last digits. For a negative number the last digit is taken from its absolute value: for `-15` it is `5`, for `-14` it is `4`.

Example: `148`, `412` → `16` (8 · 2). Example: `14`, `-15` → `20`.

# Darts score

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-46?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A target consists of three concentric circles centred at `(0, 0)`: an inner one with radius 1, a middle one with radius 5 and an outer one with radius 10. A dart lands at the point `(x, y)`, where `x` and `y` are real numbers. Return the points for the throw: 10 if the point is in the inner circle, 5 if in the middle one, 1 if in the outer one and 0 if outside the target. The border belongs to the circle: a point at distance exactly 5 from the centre scores 5.

Example: `3`, `4` → `5` (distance exactly 5); `7.1`, `-7.1` → `0`.

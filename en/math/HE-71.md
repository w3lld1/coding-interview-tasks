# Triangle area from sides

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-71?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get the lengths of three sides as positive integers. If they can form a triangle, return its area rounded to two decimal places. Otherwise return `-1`. A triangle exists when the sum of any two sides is strictly greater than the third; a degenerate case where the sum equals the third side does not count.

Example: `3`, `4`, `5` → `6.0`; `1`, `2`, `10` → `-1`.

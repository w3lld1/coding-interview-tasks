# Even and odd digits

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-155?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get an integer. Return a list of two numbers: how many even digits and how many odd digits its decimal notation has, in this order. The sign is ignored, and `0` counts as a number with one even digit.

Example: `-12` → `[1, 1]`.

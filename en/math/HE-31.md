# Primality check

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-31?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given an integer `n`, return `true` if it is prime (greater than 1 and divisible only by 1 and itself), otherwise `false`. Numbers below 2, including 0 and negatives, are not prime.

Example: `13` → `true`, `15` → `false`.

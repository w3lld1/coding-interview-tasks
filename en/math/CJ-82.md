# Equal pancake slices

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-82?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You have pancake slices — circular sectors with angles `slices[i]` (positive integers) — and `diners` guests (2 to 50). Every guest must get exactly one slice, and all slices handed out must be the same size (any size, not necessarily an integer). One cut turns a slice of size `X` into two slices of sizes `Y` and `X − Y` for any `0 < Y < X`; the pieces can be cut further. Leftover slices of any size may be kept. Slices cannot be joined.

Return the smallest number of cuts that lets you feed every guest. There may be up to a thousand slices, with angles up to `3.6·10^11`.

Example: `[8, 4]`, `3` → `1` (cut the 8 in half and hand out three slices of 4).

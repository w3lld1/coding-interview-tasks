# Math Encoder

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-37?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of positive integers (up to 10000 items, each at most 10000) in any order, consider every non-empty subset of its items (subsets are told apart by position, so equal numbers at different positions are different items). For each subset take the largest number minus the smallest one; a subset of one item gives 0. Return the sum of these differences modulo 1000000007.

Example: `[3, 6, 7, 9]` → `44`.

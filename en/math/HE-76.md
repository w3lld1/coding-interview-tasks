# Simple power of a number

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-76?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get two positive integers `x` and `n`. The number `x` is a simple power of `n` if `x = n^k` for some integer `k ≥ 0`. Return `true` if so, otherwise `false`. Any `n` to the power 0 gives `1`, and 1 to any power stays 1.

Example: `8`, `2` → `true`; `3`, `2` → `false`; `3`, `1` → `false`; `1`, `4` → `true`.

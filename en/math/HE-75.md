# Product of three primes

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-75?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a positive integer. Return `true` if it can be written as a product of exactly three prime numbers, otherwise `false`. The prime factors may repeat: `8 = 2 · 2 · 2`. The number `1` has no prime factors, so its answer is `false`.

Example: `30` → `true` (30 = 2 · 3 · 5); `10` → `false`; `60` → `false` (four factors).

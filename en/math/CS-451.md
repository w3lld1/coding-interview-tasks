# Rounds of digit sums

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-451?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a non-negative integer `n`, replace it by the sum of its digits, then again and again, until a single-digit number (below 10) is left. Return how many replacements were needed. If `n` is already below 10, the answer is `0`.

Example: `38` → `2` (38 → 3+8 = 11 → 1+1 = 2).

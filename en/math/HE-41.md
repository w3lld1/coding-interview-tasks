# Head-on car collisions

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-41?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Cars drive along an infinitely long straight road: `n` cars go left to right, and another `n` cars go right to left. All cars have the same speed, and the two groups start very far apart. A collision is a meeting of a car from the first group with a car from the second. The cars are indestructible and keep going as if nothing happened. Return the total number of collisions. For `n = 0` the answer is `0`.

Example: `3` → `9`.

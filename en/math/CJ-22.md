# Pogo stick: shortest route

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-22?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You stand at `(0, 0)` and want to reach the point `(x, y)` with integer coordinates (`(x, y) ≠ (0, 0)`, `|x|, |y| ≤ 10^9`). You jump on a pogo stick: the `i`-th jump (counting from 1) covers `2^(i-1)` units in one of four directions: `N` (`y` grows), `S` (`y` shrinks), `E` (`x` grows), `W` (`x` shrinks). You must land exactly on `(x, y)` after the last jump.

Return the shortest route as a string of the letters `N`, `S`, `E`, `W`, the directions of the jumps in order; if the point cannot be reached, return `IMPOSSIBLE`. The shortest route is always unique.

Example: `2`, `3` → `SEN` (south by 1, east by 2, north by 4).

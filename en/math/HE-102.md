# Largest even in a range

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-102?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get two positive integers `x` and `y`. Return the largest even number in the range from `x` to `y`, both ends included. If there is no such number (including the case where `x` is greater than `y`), return `-1`.

Example: `12`, `15` → `14`.

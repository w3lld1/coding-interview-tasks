# Sieve

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/PY-27?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Return, in ascending order, all prime numbers not greater than a given integer limit. Use the sieve of Eratosthenes: mark the multiples of each next unmarked number instead of testing every candidate for divisibility separately. If the limit is less than 2, the result is empty.

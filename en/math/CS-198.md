# Most points on one straight line

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-198?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a list of points on the plane: each point is a pair of integer coordinates `[x, y]` from −10,000 to 10,000, and there are at most 300 points. Points may coincide: coinciding points count separately, and any line through that position passes through each of them. Return the largest number of points of the list that lie on one straight line. An empty list gives `0`, one point gives `1`, and any two points always lie on one line.

Example: `[[1, 1], [3, 2], [5, 3], [4, 1], [2, 3], [1, 4]]` → `4` (points `[1, 4]`, `[2, 3]`, `[3, 2]`, `[4, 1]`).

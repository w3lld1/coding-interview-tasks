# Is the number a cube

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-77?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get an integer (it may be negative or zero). Return `true` if it equals the cube of some integer, otherwise `false`. For example, `-8` is the cube of `-2`, and `0` is the cube of zero.

Example: `64` → `true`; `180` → `false`; `-1` → `true`.

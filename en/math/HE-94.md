# Digit sum of the largest prime

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-94?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of integers, find the largest prime number in it and return the sum of its digits. If the list has no prime numbers (it is empty, or holds only `0`, `1`, negative and composite numbers), return `0`.

Example: `[0, 8, 1, 2, 1, 7]` → `7`. Example: `[0, 81, 12, 3, 1, 21]` → `3`.

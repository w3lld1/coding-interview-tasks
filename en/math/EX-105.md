# Triangle kind by sides

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-105?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a `kind` (`"equilateral"`, `"isosceles"` or `"scalene"`) and a list of three side lengths, return `true` if the sides form a triangle of that kind, otherwise `false`.

The sides form a triangle if all of them are greater than zero and the sum of any two is not less than the third (the degenerate case where the sum equals the third side is allowed). Equilateral: all three sides are equal. Isosceles: at least two sides are equal (an equilateral triangle qualifies too). Scalene: all sides are different. If the sides do not form a triangle, the answer is `false` for every kind.

Example: `"isosceles"`, `[3, 4, 4]` → `true`; `"isosceles"`, `[1, 1, 3]` → `false`.

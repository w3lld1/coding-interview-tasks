# Triangle area from side and height

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-45?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given the length of a triangle side and the length of the height drawn to that side, return the triangle area as a number with a fractional part. If one of the values is `0`, the area is `0`.

Example: `5`, `3` → `7.5`.

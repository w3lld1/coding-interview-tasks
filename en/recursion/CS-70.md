# Palindrome splits

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-70?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a non-empty string `s` of lowercase Latin letters, at most 16 long, return every way to cut it into consecutive parts so that each part is a palindrome (reads the same forwards and backwards). A way is written as the list of its parts in order. Order the ways by cut positions: compare the part lengths one by one from the first part, and put the way with the shorter part first.

Example: `"aab"` → `[["a", "a", "b"], ["aa", "b"]]`.

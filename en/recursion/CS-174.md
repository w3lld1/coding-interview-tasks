# Sudoku solver 9×9

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-174?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a 9×9 Sudoku board: a list of 9 strings of 9 characters, where a digit `1`–`9` is a filled cell and `.` is an empty one. Fill every empty cell so that each row, each column and each of the nine 3×3 boxes (rows and columns `0–2`, `3–5`, `6–8`) contains every digit from 1 to 9 exactly once. Filled cells must not change. Return the solved board in the same form: 9 strings of 9 digits.

The given digits are guaranteed not to break the rules and to have exactly one solution; the board may already be full. Some tests are puzzles with 17 clues, where plain backtracking over the cells in order will not fit the time limit.

Example: [`"...9723.1"`, `"6.......7"`, `"...613..8"`, `"5..2.1.3."`, `"26.3.8759"`, `".48.9..2."`, `"..6134..."`, `"1.....67."`, `"8...26413"`] → [`"485972361"`, `"613485297"`, `"972613548"`, `"597261834"`, `"261348759"`, `"348597126"`, `"726134985"`, `"134859672"`, `"859726413"`].

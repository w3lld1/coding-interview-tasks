# Rabbit word

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-462?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A word starts as `"A"`. In one step every letter is replaced by these rules: `A` → `AB`, `B` → `A` (all replacements happen at once, by the letters of the previous word). Return the word after `steps` steps. Assume `steps` is at most 12.

Example: `3` → `"ABAAB"` (A → AB → ABA → ABAAB).

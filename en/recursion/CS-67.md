# Unique permutations

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-67?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of integers, possibly with repeats, of at most 8 elements, return all distinct permutations of its elements: two permutations are the same if they are equal as sequences of values. Order the result lexicographically (by the first element, then by the second on ties, and so on). For an empty list the only permutation is the empty one, so the answer is `[[]]`.

Example: `[1, 1, 2]` → `[[1, 1, 2], [1, 2, 1], [2, 1, 1]]`.

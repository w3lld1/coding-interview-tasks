# Menu pattern expansion

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/), [Strings](../strings/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-470?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A cafe writes its set lunches as a template `pattern`. Ordinary characters stand for themselves, and a group `{option1,option2,…}` means "pick one of the options". An option is a template again: it may hold ordinary characters and nested groups, or be empty (for example `{a,}`). Commas appear only inside groups. Return all resulting strings in writing order: choices on the left change more slowly than choices on the right, and options inside a group go left to right. Equal strings are not removed. The template is valid (braces are balanced) and has at most 100 results.

Example: `"a{b,c{d,e}}f"` → `["abf", "acdf", "acef"]`.

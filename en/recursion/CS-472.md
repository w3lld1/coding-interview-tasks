# Reseating within two seats

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-472?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A row has `n` seats numbered 1 to `n`, with `n` guests seated: guest `i` sits in seat `i`. The guests are reseated so that every guest again takes exactly one seat and every seat holds exactly one guest, and nobody moves more than two numbers away from their original seat (guest `i` may end up in a seat from `i - 2` to `i + 2`; staying put is allowed). Return the number of different reseatings. For `n = 0` there is one way. Assume `n` is at most 12.

Example: `3` → `6` (all permutations of three guests are allowed); `4` → `14` (of 24 permutations, those where guest 1 moves to seat 4 or guest 4 to seat 1 are out).

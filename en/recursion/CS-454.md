# Towers of Hanoi moves

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-454?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Peg `source` holds `n` discs, largest at the bottom. Move them all to peg `target` using peg `spare`. A move takes one top disc, and a larger disc may never lie on a smaller one. Return the shortest sequence of moves as strings `"from->to"` (peg names as passed in), in the order they must be played. The shortest sequence is unique. For `n = 0` there are no moves. Assume `n` is at most 8.

Example: `2`, `"A"`, `"C"`, `"B"` → `["A->B", "A->C", "B->C"]`.

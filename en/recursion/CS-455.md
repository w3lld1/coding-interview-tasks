# Choose k stands out of n

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-455?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

An expo has `n` stands numbered 1 to `n`. Choose exactly `k` different stands to visit; the order of choosing does not matter. Return all options: each is a list of numbers in ascending order, and the options themselves are in lexicographic order (lists compared element by element from the left). For `k = 0` there is one option, the empty list; if `k > n` there are none. Assume `n` is at most 10.

Example: `4`, `2` → `[[1, 2], [1, 3], [1, 4], [2, 3], [2, 4], [3, 4]]`.

# Paper strip creases

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/), [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-467?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A paper strip is folded in half `n` times, always in the same direction, then unfolded. The strip keeps creases, each pointing right (`R`) or left (`L`). The sequence from left to right is built like this: for `n = 0` it is empty; for `n > 0` take the sequence for `n - 1`, append `R`, then the same sequence reversed with every letter swapped for the opposite one (`R` to `L`, `L` to `R`). Return the string. Assume `n` is at most 10.

Example: `3` → `"RRLRRLL"`.

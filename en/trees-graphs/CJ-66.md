# River flow between buildings

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-66?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A river is a rectangular grid of `width` columns (x from 0 to width−1) and `height` rows (y from 0 to height−1). Each cell carries at most 1 unit of water, and water moves between cells that share a side. Every cell of the southern row (y = 0) receives 1 unit of inflow. Return the maximum flow that can reach the cells of the northern row (y = height−1).

Buildings stand in the river: `buildings[i] = [x0, y0, x1, y1]` is the rectangle of cells from the lower-left corner (x0, y0) to the upper-right corner (x1, y1) inclusive. Water cannot pass through building cells. Buildings do not overlap but may touch. Limits: 3 ≤ width ≤ 1000, 3 ≤ height ≤ 10⁸, at most 200 buildings, so the whole grid cannot be built.

Example: `3`, `3`, `[[2, 0, 2, 0], [0, 2, 0, 2]]` → `1`.

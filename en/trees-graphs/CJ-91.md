# Hex game status

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-91?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Two players, Blue and Red, take turns placing stones of their color on empty cells of an `n × n` rhombus board of hexagons; either may move first. The board is a list of `n` strings: `'B'` is a blue stone, `'R'` a red one, `'.'` an empty cell. Cell `(r, c)` touches six cells: `(r, c−1)`, `(r, c+1)`, `(r−1, c)`, `(r−1, c+1)`, `(r+1, c)`, `(r+1, c−1)` (those on the board). Blue wins once its stones form a connected chain from the left column to the right column, Red from the top row to the bottom row (corner cells touch both sides). The game stops right after the first win.

Return `"Impossible"` if no legal game reaches this position; otherwise `"Blue wins"` or `"Red wins"` if someone has won, and `"Nobody wins"` if nobody has yet. The board has `1 ≤ n ≤ 100`.

Example: `["BR", "BB"]` → `"Impossible"` (blue has two more stones).

# Fewest buses between two stops

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-95?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a list of routes: `routes[i]` holds the stop numbers (integers from `0` to `10⁶`) that bus `i` serves. A bus runs its route in a loop forever, so after boarding it at any of its stops you can leave it at any other of its stops. There are up to 500 routes and up to 100000 stops across all routes together; a stop may repeat within a route. You stand at stop `source` and want to reach stop `target` travelling only by bus. Return the fewest buses you have to board; `0` if `source` equals `target` (even when no route serves it), and `-1` if the trip is impossible.

Example: `[[1, 2, 7], [3, 6, 7]]`, `source = 1`, `target = 6` → `2` (the first bus to 7, the second to 6).

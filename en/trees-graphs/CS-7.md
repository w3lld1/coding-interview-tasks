# Two-colouring a graph

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-7?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

An undirected graph is given: `n` vertices numbered `0` to `n - 1` and a list of edges `[u, v]`. There are no self-loops, parallel edges are possible, and the graph may be disconnected. Return `true` if the vertices can be coloured with two colours so that the ends of every edge have different colours, otherwise `false`. A graph with no edges can be coloured. There are at most 1000 vertices and 5000 edges.

Example: `3`, `[[0, 1], [1, 2], [2, 0]]` → `false`.

# All shortest word chains

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Strings](../strings/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-175?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a start word `begin_word`, an end word `end_word` and a list of words `words`. All words consist of lowercase Latin letters and have the same length, the words in the list are distinct, and `begin_word ≠ end_word`. A chain is a sequence of words from `begin_word` to `end_word` where each next word differs from the previous one in exactly one letter at one position and belongs to `words` (`begin_word` itself need not be in the list).

Return all chains of the smallest length, each as a list of words from `begin_word` to `end_word`. Sort the chains lexicographically: chains compare at the first differing word, and words compare as strings. If there is no chain (including when `end_word` is not in the list), return `[]`.

There are up to 1000 words of length up to 5, and at most a few hundred shortest chains in an answer.

Example: `"cat"`, `"dog"`, `["cot", "cog", "dog", "cat", "dot", "cag"]` → `[["cat", "cag", "cog", "dog"], ["cat", "cot", "cog", "dog"], ["cat", "cot", "dot", "dog"]]`.

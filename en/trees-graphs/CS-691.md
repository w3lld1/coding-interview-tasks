# The go-to person of an office

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-691?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

An office has `n` employees numbered `0` to `n - 1`. The list `asks` says who goes to whom for advice: a pair `[a, b]` means that `a` asks `b` for advice. Pairs do not repeat, and `a` is not `b`. The go-to person is an employee whom all the other `n - 1` people ask for advice and who asks nobody. Return their number, or `-1` if there is no such person (there cannot be two). With a single employee, they are the go-to person. `n` is 1 to 1000, with up to 10,000 pairs.

Example: `3`, `[[0, 2], [1, 2]]` → `2`.

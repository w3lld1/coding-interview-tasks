# Cheapest flight with limited stops

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-11?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

There are `n` cities numbered `0` to `n - 1` and a list of flights. A flight `[u, v, price]` goes from city `u` to city `v` for `price` (the price is positive, flights are one-way, two cities may have several flights between them). Find the minimum cost of a trip from `src` to `dst` with at most `k` intermediate cities (that is, at most `k + 1` flights). If `src` equals `dst` the answer is `0`. If no such trip exists, return `-1`. Limits: `n` is at most 100, `k` is at least 0.

Example: `4`, flights `[[0,1,100],[1,2,100],[2,0,100],[1,3,600],[2,3,200]]`, `0`, `3`, `1` → `700` (the trip 0 → 1 → 3; the route through city 2 is cheaper but needs two stops).

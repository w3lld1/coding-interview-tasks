# Shortest path breaking walls

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-206?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a rectangular grid `grid` of zeros and ones, `0` a free cell and `1` a wall, and a number `k`. One step moves to the neighbouring cell up, down, left or right, staying inside the grid. You may enter a wall cell only by breaking that wall, and you may break at most `k` walls over the whole path. Return the fewest steps from the top-left cell to the bottom-right one, or `-1` if it cannot be reached. A one-cell grid gives `0`.

Limits: 1 to 40 rows and columns, `0 ≤ k ≤ 1600`, and the top-left and bottom-right cells are always free.

Example: `[[0, 1], [1, 0]]`, `1` → `2` (one of the two walls is broken).

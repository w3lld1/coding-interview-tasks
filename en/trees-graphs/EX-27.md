# Degrees of separation in a family tree

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-27?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a family tree: a map `family_tree` where a key is a parent's name and the value is the list of that parent's children. All names are unique, and a child has one or two parents. The degree of separation of two people is the smallest number of links to walk from one to the other. The links are parent-child and sibling (children listed under the same parent); any link may be walked in either direction. Two parents of the same child are not linked to each other.

Return the degree of separation between `person_a` and `person_b`. For the same person the result is `0`. If the two people are not connected in the data (including when one of them does not appear in it), return `null`.

Example: tree `{"Helena": ["Isla", "Tariq"], "Isla": ["Uma"]}`, `"Tariq"`, `"Uma"` → `2` (Tariq → Isla → Uma).

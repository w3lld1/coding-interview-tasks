# Eventually safe nodes

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-107?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a directed graph: `graph[u]` lists the vertices that edges from `u` lead to (vertices `0..n-1`). A vertex is safe if every path from it, however you walk, ends in a vertex without outgoing edges, that is, no cycle can be reached from it (a self-loop `u → u` is a cycle). Return all safe vertices in increasing order. An empty graph gives `[]`. There are at most 1000 vertices.

Example: `[[1, 2], [2, 3], [5], [0], [5], [], []]` → `[2, 4, 5, 6]`.

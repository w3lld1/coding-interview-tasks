# Is the tree a mirror of itself

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-388?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A binary tree is given by three lists of equal length: `values[i]` is the value of node `i`, `left[i]` and `right[i]` are the indexes of its left and right child, or `-1` if there is none. Node `0` is the root; an empty tree is three empty lists. A tree is symmetric if its left subtree is the mirror image of its right subtree: the shapes match when reflected and the values in matching nodes are equal. Return `true` for a symmetric tree, otherwise `false`. An empty tree and a one-node tree are symmetric.

Example: `values = [1, 2, 2, 3, 3]`, `left = [1, 3, -1, -1, -1]`, `right = [2, -1, 4, -1, -1]` → `true` (the left 2 has a left child 3, the right 2 has a right child 3).

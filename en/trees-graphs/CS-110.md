# Snakes and ladders

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-110?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

The board is an `n × n` square given as a list of rows from top to bottom. Squares are numbered from `1` to `n²` in a snake pattern: the bottom row (the last list element) runs left to right, the row above it right to left, and so on. The value `-1` is an ordinary square; any other number is a square that sends you along a snake or ladder to the square with that number. Square `1` is always ordinary. You stand on square `1`. On each roll you choose a number from `1` to `6` (the die obeys you) and move forward that many squares; squares beyond the board are not allowed. If you land on a snake or ladder square, you move to its end at once, and a jump is not applied again there. Return the fewest rolls to reach square `n²`, or `-1` if it is impossible. For `n = 1` the answer is `0`. The board is at most 20×20.

Example: board `[[-1, -1], [-1, 3]]` → `1` (from square 1, a roll of 3 reaches square 4, the goal).

# One new delivery office

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-104?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A map is given as a list of equal-length strings of `0` and `1`: a `1` cell is a delivery office, a `0` cell is empty. There is at least one office. The delivery time of a cell is the Manhattan distance `|r1 - r2| + |c1 - c2|` to the nearest office (0 in a cell with an office), and the overall delivery time is the maximum of these values over all cells.

You may build at most one new office in any empty cell. Return the smallest overall delivery time you can achieve. If there are no empty cells, the answer is `0`. The map has up to 100 rows and up to 100 columns.

Example: `["10001", "00000", "00000", "00000", "10001"]` → `2` (a new office in the centre).

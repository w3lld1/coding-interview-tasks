# Words of two languages

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-69?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given `sentences`, each a list of words of lowercase Latin letters. Sentence 0 is surely English, sentence 1 is surely French, and each of the others may be in either language. All words of an English sentence are English words, all words of a French sentence are French words. A word may repeat inside a sentence and across sentences.

Choose the language of every other sentence so that as few words as possible end up being both English and French, and return that smallest count. Limits: 2 ≤ number of sentences ≤ 200, up to 100 words in the first two, up to 10 in the others.

Example: `[["a", "b", "c", "d", "e"], ["f", "g", "h", "i", "j"], ["a", "b", "c", "i", "j"], ["f", "g", "h", "d", "e"]]` → `4`.

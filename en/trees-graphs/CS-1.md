# Islands on a map

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-1?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A map is a list of equal-length strings made of `'1'` (land) and `'0'` (water). An island is a set of land cells connected through a shared side (up, down, left, right); diagonal cells are not connected. Return the number of islands. An empty map (empty list) gives `0`. The map has at most 50 rows of at most 50 characters.

Example: `["11000", "11000", "00100", "00011"]` → `3`.

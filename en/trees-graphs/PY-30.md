# Two Bucket

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/PY-30?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given the capacities of two buckets (bucket 1 and bucket 2), the goal volume and the number of the bucket to fill first (1 or 2), find the number of actions needed to get the goal volume in one bucket. An action fills or empties one bucket completely, or pours from one into the other until the source is empty or the target is full. A state where the starting bucket is empty and the other one is full is not allowed. Return a list [number of actions including the first fill, number of the bucket that holds the goal, volume left in the other bucket]; if the goal cannot be reached, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

# Maze with the most power

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-28?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a rectangular table `grid`: `-1` is a dangerous cell you can never enter, a positive number is a safe cell holding that much power. From a cell you may step up, down, left or right. You stand in cell `start` and must reach cell `finish` (both given as `[row, column]`, both safe and different) in as few steps as possible.

A cell's power is collected once, when you enter it, including the start and finish cells. Among all shortest paths pick the one that collects the most power and return that total. If `finish` cannot be reached, return `-1`.

Example: `[[2, -1, 5], [3, -1, 6]]`, `[0, 2]`, `[1, 0]` → `-1` (a wall of dangerous cells splits the table). For `[[3, 1, 1], [1, 1, 1], [1, 1, 8]]`, `[0, 0]`, `[2, 2]` the answer is `14`: the shortest path takes 4 steps, and the best one collects 3 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 8.

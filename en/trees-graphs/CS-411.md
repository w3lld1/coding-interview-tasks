# The largest group

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-411?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A graph is given by the number of vertices `n` (vertices `0…n-1`) and a list of edges `edges`: an edge `[u, v]` joins `u` and `v` in both directions. Edges are acquaintances. A group is a set of people where any two are linked by a chain of acquaintances; a person who knows nobody is a group of one. Here `1 ≤ n ≤ 1000`.

Return the size of the largest group.

Example: `5`, `[[0, 1], [1, 2], [3, 4]]` → `3`.

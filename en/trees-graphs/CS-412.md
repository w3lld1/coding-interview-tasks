# Reach within k steps

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-412?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A graph is given by the number of vertices `n` (vertices `0…n-1`) and a list of edges `edges`: an edge `[u, v]` joins `u` and `v` in both directions. Edges are acquaintances. News spreads from person `start`: in each step everyone who knows someone informed learns it.

Return how many people other than `start` learn the news in at most `k` steps (`k ≥ 0`). With `k = 0` the answer is `0`.

Example: `6`, `[[0, 1], [0, 2], [1, 3], [2, 4], [3, 5]]`, `0`, `2` → `4` (people 1, 2, 3, 4).

# Identical trees

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-394?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get two binary trees. Each is given by three lists of equal length: `values`, `left`, `right`. Here `values[i]` is the value of node `i`, `left[i]` and `right[i]` are the indexes of the left and right child, or `-1`; node `0` is the root and an empty tree is three empty lists. Return `true` if the trees are identical: the same shape and the same values in the same places (a left child is compared with a left child, a right with a right). The node numbering of the two trees may differ.

Example: tree A `[1, 2]`, `[1, -1]`, `[-1, -1]` (the root has a left child) and tree B `[1, 2]`, `[-1, -1]`, `[1, -1]` (the root has a right child) → `false`.

# Walk every street

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-417?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A graph is given by the number of vertices `n` (vertices `0…n-1`) and a list of edges `edges`: an edge `[u, v]` joins `u` and `v` in both directions. Edges are streets between junctions; two equal streets between one pair of junctions are two different streets. Loops `[a, a]` do not occur.

A postman wants to walk along every street exactly once and return to the junction where they started (any junction may be the start). Return `true` if that is possible, otherwise `false`. If there are no streets, the answer is `true`.

Known fact: such a walk exists if every junction has an even number of streets and all junctions that have streets lie in one connected part.

Example: `3`, `[[0, 1], [1, 2], [2, 0]]` → `true`; `4`, `[[0, 1], [1, 2], [2, 3]]` → `false`.

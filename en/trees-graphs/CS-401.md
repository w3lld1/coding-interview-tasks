# Leaves left to right

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-401?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A binary tree is given by three lists of equal length: `values[i]` is the value of node `i`, `left[i]` and `right[i]` are the indexes of its left and right child, or `-1` if there is none. Node `0` is the root; an empty tree is three empty lists. A leaf is a node with no children. Return the values of all leaves in left-to-right order, as you would see them in a drawing of the tree: the leaves of the left subtree first, then of the right one. A one-node tree gives a list with its value, an empty tree gives an empty list.

Example: `values = [4, 2, 6, 1, 3]`, `left = [1, 3, -1, -1, -1]`, `right = [2, 4, -1, -1, -1]` → `[1, 3, 6]`.

# Circle of best friends

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-70?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A class has n children numbered 0 to n−1. Child i has a best friend `bff[i]` (bff[i] ≠ i), and friendship is not necessarily mutual. Seat as many children as possible in a circle so that every seated child sits next to their best friend, on the left or on the right. The other children do not take part. Return the largest possible circle size. A circle of two children is allowed when they are each other's best friends. Limits: 2 ≤ n ≤ 1000.

Example: `[1, 2, 3, 0]` → `4`.

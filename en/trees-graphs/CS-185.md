# Largest island after one landfill

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-185?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a rectangular map `grid` of zeros (water) and ones (land), from 1×1 to 500×500. An island is a maximal group of ones joined through shared sides (diagonal cells are not neighbours), and its size is its number of cells. You may turn at most one zero into a one, or change nothing. Return the largest island size you can get this way. With no zeros on the map the answer is the number of all cells; with no ones it is `1`.

Recounting the island from scratch for every zero is too slow: aim for O(m·n).

Example: `[[1, 1, 0], [0, 0, 1], [1, 1, 1]]` → `7`: filling the centre cell joins the 2-cell island at the top and the 4-cell island at the bottom and right.

# Cheapest network between points

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-112?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given points on a plane: `points[i] = [x, y]`, integer coordinates from -10⁶ to 10⁶, at most 1000 points, equal points are allowed. A cable between two points costs their Manhattan distance `|x1 - x2| + |y1 - y2|`. Return the smallest total cost of cables that connects all points to each other (directly or through other points). For an empty list and for a single point the answer is `0`.

Example: `[[0, 0], [2, 2], [3, 10], [5, 2], [7, 0]]` → `20`.

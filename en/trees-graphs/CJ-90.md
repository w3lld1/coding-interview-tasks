# Crossing at traffic lights

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Math](../math/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-90?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A city is a grid of `n` east-west streets and `m` north-south streets meeting at `n·m` intersections. You are given `lights`: `lights[i][j] = [s, w, t]` is the traffic light of the intersection in row `i` (row `0` is the northmost) and column `j` (column `0` is the westmost). Each intersection has four corners the pedestrian walks between.

The pedestrian starts at minute `0` at the south-west corner of the south-west intersection and must reach the north-east corner of the north-east intersection. Crossing a street at an intersection (between two neighbouring corners of the same intersection) takes 1 minute and is allowed only if the needed light is green for the whole crossing. Walking along a block to the corner of the neighbouring intersection takes 2 minutes at any time. She may wait at a corner as long as she likes; diagonal moves are not allowed.

A light `[s, w, t]` repeats a cycle of length `s + w`: at minute `t` (and every `s + w` minutes before and after it) it turns green for crossing north or south for `s` minutes, then green for crossing east or west for `w` minutes. Return the earliest arrival time in minutes. The grid is up to 20 × 20, `1 ≤ s, w ≤ 10^7`, `0 ≤ t ≤ 10^8`.

Example: `[[[3, 2, 10]]]` → `4` (cross north, wait 2 minutes, cross east).

# Relay mail with tiring horses

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-73?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

There are n cities (2 ≤ n ≤ 30), each with one horse: `horses[i] = [e, s]` means city i's horse can travel at most `e` kilometres in total at a speed of `s` km/h. `distances[i][j]` is the length of the road from city i to city j in kilometres, or `-1` if there is none (a road of length `0` is still a road and takes 0 hours; roads are one-way, `distances[i][i] = -1`, the triangle inequality need not hold). The rider starts on the starting city's horse; in any city they may instantly switch to that city's horse, and the distance a horse has used never comes back. For each query `queries[k] = [u, v]` (cities numbered from 0, u ≠ v) return the minimum delivery time from u to v in hours, or `-1` if delivery is impossible. Answers are compared within 1e-6.

Example: `[[2, 3], [2, 4], [4, 4]]`, `[[-1, 1, -1], [-1, -1, 1], [-1, -1, -1]]`, `[[0, 2]]` → `[0.5833333333]`.

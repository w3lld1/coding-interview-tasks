# Cards Game

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-40?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A box holds N cards (2 ≤ N ≤ 100). Card i has a red number `red[i]` on one side and a blue number `blue[i]` on the other, both positive integers up to 10^9. While the box holds at least two cards, you make a move: take out two cards, choose a red number R from one and a blue number B from the other, add `R xor B` (bitwise exclusive OR) to the total, then put one of the two cards back into the box and discard the other for good. The game ends when one card is left. The total starts at 0.

Return the smallest possible final total.

Example: `[1, 2]`, `[3, 3]` → `1` (the red number 2 of the second card and the blue number 3 of the first card give `2 xor 3 = 1`).

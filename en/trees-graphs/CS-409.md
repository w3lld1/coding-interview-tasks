# Friends of friends

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-409?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A graph is given by the number of vertices `n` (vertices `0…n-1`) and a list of edges `edges`: an edge `[u, v]` joins `u` and `v` in both directions. Edges are friendships. A «friend of a friend» of person `start` is someone who is a friend of one of `start`'s friends, but is neither a friend of `start` nor `start` themself.

Return the numbers of such people in increasing order. If there are none, return an empty list.

Example: `6`, `[[0, 1], [0, 2], [1, 3], [2, 4], [3, 5]]`, `0` → `[3, 4]`.

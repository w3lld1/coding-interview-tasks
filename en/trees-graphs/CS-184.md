# Swimming in rising water

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-184?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a rectangular height map `grid` (from 1×1 to 50×50, heights are integers from 0 to 10^6). At time `t` (an integer starting at 0) the water level is `t`, and you may be in a cell only when its height is at most `t`. The swimmer appears in the top-left cell at the earliest allowed time, that is at time `grid[0][0]`. Swimming into a side-neighbour takes exactly 1 unit of time: starting at time `t`, the swimmer is in the neighbour at time `t + 1`, and its height must be at most `t + 1`. The swimmer may wait in any cell for any whole number of units.

Return the earliest time at which the swimmer can be in the bottom-right cell. For a 1×1 map that is `grid[0][0]`.

Example: `[[0, 4, 0], [5, 6, 0]]` → `6`: wait at the start until time 3, reach the height-4 cell at time 4, then the cells to the right at times 5 and 6.

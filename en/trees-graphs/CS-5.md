# Clear path through a grid

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-5?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A square table of zeros and ones is given: `0` is a free cell, `1` is a wall. Walk from the top-left cell to the bottom-right cell, moving between free cells to any of the 8 neighbouring cells (diagonals included). Return the length of the shortest path, counted in cells, including the first and the last. If either of those two cells is a wall, or there is no path, return `-1`. A table with one free cell gives `1`, an empty table gives `-1`. The side is at most 100.

Example: `[[0, 1], [1, 0]]` → `2`.

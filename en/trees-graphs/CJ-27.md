# Teleporters and turbolifts

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-27?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A spaceship has `n` rooms numbered 1 to `n`; `colors[i]` is the colour of room `i + 1` (a string of one or two characters from `a-z`, `0-9`). From any room you can teleport instantly, in 0 seconds, to any other room of the same colour.

There are also one-way turbolifts: `lifts[j] = [a, b, t]` carries you from room `a` to room `b` in `t` seconds (`0 ≤ t ≤ 1000`); there may be several lifts, including a lift from a room to itself. For every query `queries[j] = [p, q]`, find the smallest travel time from room `p` to room `q`, or `-1` if there is no way. Return the list of answers in query order.

Example: `["gl", "t3", "t3"]`, `[[1, 2, 217], [3, 2, 567], [1, 1, 21]]`, `[[2, 1], [2, 3]]` → `[-1, 0]`.

# Mutual friends

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-407?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A graph is given by the number of vertices `n` (vertices `0…n-1`) and a list of edges `edges`: an edge `[u, v]` joins `u` and `v` in both directions. Edges are friendships. A mutual friend of people `a` and `b` is someone who is a friend of both `a` and `b`. Repeated edges are allowed and must not create duplicates in the answer; `a` and `b` are different people.

Return the numbers of the mutual friends in increasing order, without repeats. If there are none, return an empty list.

Example: `5`, `[[0, 2], [1, 2], [0, 3], [1, 3], [0, 4]]`, `0`, `1` → `[2, 3]`.

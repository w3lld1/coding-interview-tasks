# Knockout stage tickets

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-54?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A knockout tournament has 2^P teams numbered 0 to 2^P − 1 (1 ≤ P ≤ 10) and P rounds. In the first round team 0 plays team 1, team 2 plays team 3, and so on; in each later round the winners of neighbouring matches of the previous round meet in the same order, and the last round is a single final. `prices` holds P lists: `prices[r][j]` is the ticket price of the j-th match of round r (from zero), from 0 to 100000.

A fan is willing to miss at most `misses[i]` matches of team i (from 0 to P). Tickets are bought in advance, before the tournament, and the set of tickets must satisfy all these limits whatever the results of the matches. Return the minimum total price of such a set.

Example: `misses = [1, 2, 3, 2, 1, 0, 1, 3]`, `prices = [[100, 150, 50, 90], [500, 400], [800]]` → `1350` (every possible match of team 5 and the first match of team 0).

# Words on a letter board

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Trees and graphs](../trees-graphs/), [Strings](../strings/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-74?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You are given a board of lowercase Latin letters (a list of strings of equal length, one per row) and a list of words. A word is found if it can be read along a chain of cells where each next cell shares a side with the previous one (up, down, left or right) and no cell is used twice within the word. Return all found words without repeats, sorted alphabetically. If nothing is found or the board is empty (`[]`), return `[]`.

The board is up to 12 × 12; there are up to 1000 words, each non-empty and at most 10 letters long; the list may repeat words.

Example: `["cat", "oxe", "wen"]`, `["cat", "cot", "axe", "net", "ten", "wax"]` → `["axe", "cat", "net", "ten"]`.

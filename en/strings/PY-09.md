# Transpose

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/PY-09?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a multi-line text whose lines may have different lengths, turn its columns into rows. Fill missing characters with spaces only when a character from the original text is still needed further right in the resulting row. Do not add spaces for missing trailing positions, but keep spaces that were actually present in the input.

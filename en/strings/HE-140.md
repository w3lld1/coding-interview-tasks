# Replace spaces with underscores and dashes

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-140?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a string, replace its spaces as follows: a single space or two spaces in a row become the same number of `_` characters; a run of three or more consecutive spaces becomes a single `-`. Other characters stay as they are. Runs at the start and at the end of the string are handled the same way.

Example: `" Example   3"` → `"_Example-3"`.

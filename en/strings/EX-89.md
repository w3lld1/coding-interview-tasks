# Transcribe DNA to RNA

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-89?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A DNA strand consists of the characters `A`, `C`, `G`, `T`. To get the RNA, replace every nucleotide with its complement: `G` → `C`, `C` → `G`, `T` → `A`, `A` → `U`. Return the resulting string. An empty strand gives an empty result.

Example: `"ACGTGGTCTTAA"` → `"UGCACCAGAAUU"`.

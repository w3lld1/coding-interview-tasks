# Kindergarten garden

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-63?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A class has 12 children, in alphabetical order: Alice, Bob, Charlie, David, Eve, Fred, Ginny, Harriet, Ileana, Joseph, Kincaid, Larry. A garden diagram is two rows separated by the character `\n`; every character is a plant: `G` is `"grass"`, `C` is `"clover"`, `R` is `"radishes"`, `V` is `"violets"`. Each child owns two neighbouring cups in each row: Alice has the first two columns, Bob the next two, and so on in alphabetical order. Return the four plants of the child: first the two from the first row left to right, then the two from the second row.

Example: diagram `"RC\nGG"`, `"Alice"` → `["radishes", "clover", "grass", "grass"]`.

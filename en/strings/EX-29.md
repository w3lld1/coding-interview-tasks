# Number in English words

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Math](../math/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-29?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Write a whole number in English words, the way it is said out loud. Numbers from 21 to 99 that are not multiples of ten use a hyphen (`twenty-two`); all other words are separated by single spaces, and the word `and` is never used. Groups of three digits are named `thousand`, `million` and `billion`, and a group that is zero is skipped completely. The valid range is `0` to `999999999999` inclusive: for `0` return `"zero"`, and for any number outside the range report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

Example: `1234` → `"one thousand two hundred thirty-four"`.

# The killer word in Hangman

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/), [Recursion and backtracking](../recursion/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-55?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You play Hangman with the dictionary `dictionary`: distinct words of lowercase Latin letters, 1 to 10 letters long (up to a few thousand words). You pick a word from the dictionary and write one blank per letter. Your opponent goes through his list of 26 distinct letters in order. He asks for the next letter only if some dictionary word contains it and agrees with everything he knows so far (the length, the revealed positions, the asked letters missing from your word); otherwise he skips it. If the asked letter is in your word, you reveal all its positions; otherwise he loses a point. The game goes on until he has asked every letter of your word.

Given the dictionary and `lists`, the opponent's letter orders (each a string of 26 distinct letters), return for each order the word that makes him lose the most points; on a tie, the one earlier in the dictionary.

Example: `["banana", "caravan", "pajamas"]`, `["abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz"]` → `["pajamas"]` (after `a` the opponent asks `c` and loses a point).

# Proverb from a list

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-80?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of words, build a rhyming proverb and return its lines as a list. For every pair of neighbouring words `a`, `b` (in list order) add the line `For want of a <a> the <b> was lost.`, where `<a>` and `<b>` are these words. If the list has at least one word, add `And all for the want of a <X>.` as the last line, where `<X>` is the first word of the list. An empty list gives an empty list; a list of one word gives only the last line.

Example: `["nail", "shoe", "horse"]` → `["For want of a nail the shoe was lost.", "For want of a shoe the horse was lost.", "And all for the want of a nail."]`.

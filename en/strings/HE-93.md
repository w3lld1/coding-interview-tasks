# Case swap and vowel shift cipher

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-93?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Encode a message made of Latin letters and spaces in two steps. First swap the case of every letter: lowercase becomes uppercase and vice versa. Then replace every vowel (`a`, `e`, `i`, `o`, `u`, in either case) with the letter two places after it in the alphabet, keeping the case. All other characters, including spaces, stay as they are.

Example: `"test"` → `"TGST"`.

# Sum of uppercase codes

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-66?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a string, return the sum of the ASCII codes of its uppercase Latin letters `A`–`Z`; every other character (lowercase letters, digits, spaces, punctuation) is ignored. For an empty string, or one with no uppercase letters, the answer is `0`.

Example: `"abAB"` → `131` (65 + 66).

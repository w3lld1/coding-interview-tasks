# Lowercase Roman numerals

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-156?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get an integer from 1 to 1000. Return its Roman numeral in lowercase using the usual subtractive rules: `iv` for 4, `ix` for 9, `xl` for 40, `xc` for 90, `cd` for 400, `cm` for 900.

Example: `426` → `"cdxxvi"`.

# Optimal full justification of text

**Difficulty:** hard · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Dynamic programming](../dp/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-192?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of words and a line width `width`. Split the words, in order, into lines so that in every line the words separated by single spaces take at most `width` characters. The penalty of a line is the square of its free characters (`width` minus the length of the line with single spaces); the last line (the one holding the last word) has no penalty. Choose the split with the smallest total penalty; if several splits tie, put as many words as possible on the first line, then, under that, on the second, and so on.

Then pad every line to exactly `width`. In a line of two or more words other than the last line, share the free characters as spaces between the words equally, and give the remainder one extra space per gap from left to right. Align a one-word line and the last line to the left: words separated by one space, spaces on the right. Return the list of lines; for an empty list of words return `[]`.

Up to 300 words, each of 1 to `width` characters without spaces, `width` from 1 to 100.

Example: `["aaa", "bb", "cc", "ddddd"]`, `6` → `["aaa   ", "bb  cc", "ddddd "]` (penalty 9 + 1; the greedy `"aaa bb"` gives 0 + 16).

# Rotational cipher (ROT)

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-92?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement the Caesar cipher. Every Latin letter is moved forward in the alphabet by `shift_key` positions (0 to 26), wrapping around: `z` is followed by `a`. Case is preserved and all other characters (spaces, digits, punctuation) stay unchanged. A shift of 0 or 26 leaves the text as is.

Example: `"OMG"`, `5` → `"TRL"`; `"The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog."`, `13` → `"Gur dhvpx oebja sbk whzcf bire gur ynml qbt."`.

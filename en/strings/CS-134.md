# Multiply numbers as strings

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-134?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given two non-negative integers as strings of decimal digits (no leading zeros except the number `0` itself; each up to 200 characters), return their product as a string without leading zeros. Converting a whole string to a numeric type is not allowed: the numbers may not fit into `int` or `long`.

Example: `"123"`, `"45"` → `"5535"`.

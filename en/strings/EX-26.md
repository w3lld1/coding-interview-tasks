# Rectangles in an ASCII drawing

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Matrices and grids](../matrices/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-26?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a drawing made of characters, a list of rows of equal length. The character `+` is a corner, `-` is part of a horizontal side, `|` is part of a vertical side, a space is empty. Count all rectangles whose four corners are `+` and whose sides are complete lines: the top and bottom sides consist of `+` and `-`, the left and right sides of `+` and `|`. Rectangles may overlap and share sides, a rectangle may have height or width 1, and anything may be inside it. For an empty list and for a drawing without rectangles return `0`.

Example: `["+-+", "| |", "+-+"]` → `1`.

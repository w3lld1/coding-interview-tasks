# One letter after replacements

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-42?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a string of uppercase Latin letters (up to 1000 characters) and an integer `k ≥ 0`, you may replace at most `k` characters with any uppercase letters. Return the greatest length of a substring (contiguous piece) that consists of one repeated letter after such replacements. For an empty string the answer is `0`.

Example: `"AABABBA"`, `1` → `4` (replacing one `B` in `AABA` gives `AAAA`).

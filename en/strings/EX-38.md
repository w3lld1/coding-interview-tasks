# Atbash cipher

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-38?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Implement the Atbash cipher for the Latin alphabet: the first letter is replaced by the last, the second by the second-last, and so on (`a` ↔ `z`, `b` ↔ `y`, …). You get a mode `mode` (`"encode"` or `"decode"`) and a text `phrase`.

Letter case does not matter and the result is lowercase. Digits are kept unchanged and all other characters (spaces, punctuation) are dropped. When encoding, the result is split into groups of 5 characters separated by a space (the last group may be shorter, with no trailing space). When decoding, the result has no spaces.

Example: `"encode"`, `"x123 yes"` → `"c123b vh"`; `"decode"`, `"gvhg"` → `"test"`.

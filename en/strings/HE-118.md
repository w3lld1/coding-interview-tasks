# Closest inner vowel from the right

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-118?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a word of Latin letters, find the vowel that stands between two consonants and is closest to the end of the word, and return it in its original case. Vowels are `a`, `e`, `i`, `o`, `u` in both cases; all other letters are consonants. Vowels at the start or at the end of the word do not count (they lack a consonant on one side). If there is no such vowel, return an empty string; words shorter than three letters also give an empty string.

Example: `"yogurt"` → `"u"`; `"quick"` → `""`.

# Pangram

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-74?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a string. Return `true` if it contains each of the 26 letters of the Latin alphabet at least once, otherwise `false`. Case does not matter, and digits, spaces, punctuation, underscores and letters of other alphabets are ignored. An empty string gives `false`.

Example: `"the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog"` → `true`.

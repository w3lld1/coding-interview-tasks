# Flip letter case

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-27?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a string, replace every lowercase letter with its uppercase form and every uppercase letter with its lowercase form. Other characters (digits, spaces, punctuation) stay unchanged. The empty string gives the empty string. The tests use Latin letters only.

Example: `"Hello!"` → `"hELLO!"`.

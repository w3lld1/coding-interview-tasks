# Pig Latin

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-19?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Translate English text to Pig Latin. You get a phrase of lowercase words separated by single spaces; return the phrase with every word translated. The vowels are `a`, `e`, `i`, `o`, `u`, all other letters are consonants. The rules for a word:

1. If the word starts with a vowel, or with `xr` or `yt`, add `ay` to the end.
2. If the word starts with one or more consonants, move them to the end and add `ay`.
3. If the word starts with zero or more consonants followed by `qu`, move those consonants together with `qu` to the end and add `ay`.
4. If the word starts with one or more consonants followed by `y`, move the consonants before the `y` to the end and add `ay`. At the very start of a word, `y` counts as a consonant.

Example: `"quick fast run"` → `"ickquay astfay unray"`.

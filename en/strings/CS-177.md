# Word pairs that form a palindrome

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-177?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of **distinct** words made of lowercase Latin letters (one word may be the empty string), find every index pair `[i, j]` with `i ≠ j` such that the concatenation `words[i] + words[j]` reads the same left to right and right to left. Order matters: `[i, j]` and `[j, i]` are different pairs.

Return the pairs sorted by `i` ascending, and by `j` ascending for equal `i`. If there are none, return `[]`. Limits: up to 2000 words of length up to 30; checking every pair is too slow on large inputs.

Example: `["ab", "ba", "abc", "c", ""]` → `[[0, 1], [1, 0], [2, 1], [3, 4], [4, 3]]`.

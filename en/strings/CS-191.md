# Words glued from other words

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Dynamic programming](../dp/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CS-191?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a list of distinct non-empty words of lowercase Latin letters. A word is composite if it can be cut completely into two or more pieces so that each piece is another word of the same list (the same word may appear among the pieces several times, but a composite word is never a piece of itself). Return all composite words sorted alphabetically. If there are none or the list is empty, return `[]`.

Up to 1000 words, each at most 30 letters long.

Example: `["sun", "flower", "sunflower", "s", "sunflowers"]` → `["sunflower", "sunflowers"]` (`sunflowers` = `sun` + `flower` + `s`).

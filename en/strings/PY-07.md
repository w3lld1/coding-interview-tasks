# Word Count

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/PY-07?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

From an ASCII text, build a dictionary of how many times each word occurs, case-insensitively. Whitespace and punctuation separate words, but an apostrophe inside a contraction stays part of the word. Numeric sequences also count as words, and apostrophes used as quotes around a word are not part of the key.

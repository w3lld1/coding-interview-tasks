# Largest Series Product

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/), [Errors and exceptions](../errors/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/PY-17?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a string of decimal digits and a non-negative window length, find the largest product of digits among all contiguous substrings of that length. Account for zeros inside the substrings and do not change the order of the digits. For length zero, return 1, including for an empty string. For a negative length, a length greater than the string's length, or non-digit characters with a positive window, report an error: throw an exception, or return an error in Go.

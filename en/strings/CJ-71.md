# Closest possible scoreboard

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Searching and sorting](../search-sort/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-71?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

A scoreboard shows two scores of equal length: the strings `coders` and `jammers` made of digits and `?` (leading zeros allowed, length 1 to 18). Each `?` is a missing digit. Replace every `?` with a digit so that the absolute difference of the two numbers is as small as possible. Among those fillings pick the one with the smallest `coders` number, and among those the one with the smallest `jammers` number. Return a list of two strings of the original length: the filled `coders` and `jammers`. With no `?` at all, return the strings unchanged.

The numbers reach 10¹⁸ − 1: in a language without 64-bit integers, compare them as equal-length strings.

Example: `"?5"`, `"?0"` → `["05", "00"]`.

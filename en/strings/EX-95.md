# Scrabble word score

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-95?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a word of Latin letters (case does not matter), return the sum of the points of its letters.

Points: `A E I O U L N R S T` are 1; `D G` are 2; `B C M P` are 3; `F H V W Y` are 4; `K` is 5; `J X` are 8; `Q Z` are 10. The empty word is worth 0.

Example: `"cabbage"` → `14` (3 + 1 + 3 + 3 + 1 + 2 + 1).

# The Twelve Days of Christmas

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-106?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Return the lines of the song "The Twelve Days of Christmas" from verse `start_verse` to verse `end_verse` inclusive (numbers from 1 to 12, `start_verse` <= `end_verse`), one string per verse, with no line breaks.

Verse `n` looks like `On the <day> day of Christmas my true love gave to me: <gifts>.` Days: first, second, third, fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh, eighth, ninth, tenth, eleventh, twelfth. The gifts are listed separated by `, ` from gift number `n` down to the first: 1 is `a Partridge in a Pear Tree`, 2 is `two Turtle Doves`, 3 is `three French Hens`, 4 is `four Calling Birds`, 5 is `five Gold Rings`, 6 is `six Geese-a-Laying`, 7 is `seven Swans-a-Swimming`, 8 is `eight Maids-a-Milking`, 9 is `nine Ladies Dancing`, 10 is `ten Lords-a-Leaping`, 11 is `eleven Pipers Piping`, 12 is `twelve Drummers Drumming`. From the second verse on, the last (first) gift is preceded by `and `.

Example: `2`, `2` → `["On the second day of Christmas my true love gave to me: two Turtle Doves, and a Partridge in a Pear Tree."]`.

# Alien rhyme

**Difficulty:** medium · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Hash maps and sets](../hashing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/CJ-21?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Every word of an alien language has exactly one accented letter; the part of the word starting from it is its accent-suffix. Two words rhyme if their accent-suffixes are equal. For example, `PROL` and `TARPOL` rhyme if the accent falls on `O` or on `L` in both, but not if it falls on `R`.

You are given a list of distinct words of uppercase Latin letters (a word has up to 50 letters, there are up to 1000 words). You may discard any words, place the accents in the rest and split them into pairs so that each word rhymes only with its partner and with no other chosen word. Return the largest number of words that can be arranged into pairs (0 if there are no pairs).

Example: `["PI", "HI", "WI", "FI"]` → `2` (all four words can only rhyme at `I`, so just one pair can be used).

# Acronym from a phrase

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-35?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a phrase made of words, return its acronym: the first letters of all words, upper-cased and written together without separators. Words are the parts of the phrase separated by spaces and hyphens `-` (any number of them in a row). Other punctuation (commas, apostrophes, underscores, exclamation marks) is ignored: the word `_Not_` gives `N`, and `It's` gives `I`. A word without any letter adds nothing. An empty phrase gives an empty string.

Example: `"Thank George It's Friday!"` → `"TGIF"`; `"Liquid-crystal display"` → `"LCD"`.

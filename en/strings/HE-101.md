# Words split by commas and spaces

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-101?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a string whose words are separated by commas, spaces, or both; any number of separators may appear in a row. Return the list of words in their original order, with no empty entries. For an empty string, or a string made only of separators, return an empty list.

Example: `"Hi, my name"` → `["Hi", "my", "name"]`.

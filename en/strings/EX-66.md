# Ticket with an ordinal number

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Math](../math/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-66?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

You get a name and a customer number from 1 to 999. Return the sentence `<name>, you are the <number><suffix> customer we serve today. Thank you!`, where the suffix is the English ordinal ending. Numbers ending in 1 get `st`, numbers ending in 2 get `nd`, numbers ending in 3 get `rd`, except that numbers ending in 11, 12 or 13 always get `th`. All other numbers get `th`.

Example: `"Mary"`, `1` → `"Mary, you are the 1st customer we serve today. Thank you!"`.

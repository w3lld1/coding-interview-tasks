# Bob's replies

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-40?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Bob answers with one of five phrases. Given a remark `text`, return Bob's reply. Spaces, tabs and line breaks at the ends of the remark are ignored.

- The remark is empty or only whitespace: `"Fine. Be that way!"`.
- The remark is YELLED (it has at least one Latin letter and no lowercase letter) and ends with `?`: `"Calm down, I know what I'm doing!"`.
- The remark is yelled but not a question: `"Whoa, chill out!"`.
- The remark ends with `?` and is not yelled: `"Sure."`.
- Anything else: `"Whatever."`.

Example: `"WATCH OUT!"` → `"Whoa, chill out!"`; `"1, 2, 3"` → `"Whatever."`.

# The house that Jack built

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Arrays and lists](../arrays/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/EX-59?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Generate the nursery rhyme "This is the House that Jack Built". You get the numbers of the first and the last verse (1 to 12, the first not greater than the last). Return a list of strings: one string per verse, with no empty lines between them. Verse n starts with `This is `, then the n-th character, then the chain of "who did what" down to the house, and finally `that Jack built.`, everything joined by spaces on one line.

Characters in order: the house, the malt, the rat, the cat, the dog, the cow with the crumpled horn, the maiden all forlorn, the man all tattered and torn, the priest all shaven and shorn, the rooster that crowed in the morn, the farmer sowing his corn, the horse and the hound and the horn. The link between character k and the previous one: 2 is `that lay in`, 3 `that ate`, 4 `that killed`, 5 `that worried`, 6 `that tossed`, 7 `that milked`, 8 `that kissed`, 9 `that married`, 10 `that woke`, 11 `that kept`, 12 `that belonged to`.

Example: `2`, `3` → `["This is the malt that lay in the house that Jack built.", "This is the rat that ate the malt that lay in the house that Jack built."]`.

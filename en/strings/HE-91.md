# Bored sentences

**Difficulty:** easy · **Languages:** any of Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, Go · **Topics:** [Strings](../strings/), [Parsing and validation](../parsing/)

**[▶ Solve online on CodeShare](https://code-share.online/practice/HE-91?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tasks-repo)**

Given a text whose sentences are separated by `.`, `?` or `!`, call a sentence bored if it starts with the letter `I` followed immediately by a space. Spaces before a sentence after a separator are ignored. Return the number of bored sentences.

Sentences such as `Is it` or `I, robot` do not count: there is no space right after `I`. An empty text gives `0`.

Example: `"The sky is blue. I love this weather"` → `1`.
